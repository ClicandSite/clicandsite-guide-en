Périgueux, two thousand years of history

A domed cathedral, Roman remains and an old Renaissance quarter on the Isle: the capital of the Périgord is a perfect day out.

Capital of the Dordogne, Périgueux grew from the Gallo-Roman town of Vesunna, which left the Vesunna tower and a large Roman house now sheltered in a museum. In the Middle Ages the town developed around Saint-Front cathedral, a stop on the pilgrim routes to Santiago de Compostela.

The old centre around the cathedral is explored on foot: lanes, Renaissance mansions and market squares.

A bit of history

In ancient times Périgueux was called Vesunna and was the capital of the Petrocorii, the Gallic people who gave the Périgord its name. The Vesunna tower and the domus in the Vesunna museum are their legacy.

In the Middle Ages two rival towns lived side by side: the Cité, around the old Roman town, and Puy-Saint-Front, around the abbey. They eventually united in the 13th century. Saint-Front became the cathedral in the 17th century, then Paul Abadie restored it in the 19th century before designing the Sacré-Cœur in Paris.

Things to see in Périgueux

Saint-Front cathedral

A Romanesque cathedral with five domes, UNESCO-listed as part of the Routes of Santiago de Compostela; its 19th-century restoration by Paul Abadie inspired the Sacré-Cœur in Paris. See things to do in Périgueux and the Périgord

The Vesunna museum

A museum by Jean Nouvel built over the remains of a wealthy Gallo-Roman house, the domus of Vesunna.

The Vesunna tower

The tall circular wall of a Roman temple, in a public garden.

The protected old town

Medieval lanes, Renaissance façades, the Mataguerre tower and the banks of the Isle.

The markets

Markets on Wednesday and Saturday mornings, and in winter the foie gras and truffle markets.

How to get to Périgueux

By train

Périgueux is about 1 h 20 from Bordeaux by TER; from Paris, allow about four hours with a change.

By car

About 1 h 30 from Bordeaux on the A89.

Practical info 2026: Périgueux

Tips for visiting Périgueux

  • Enter the cathedral from Place de la Clautre for the best view of the domes.
  • Come on a Wednesday or Saturday morning for the markets.
  • Combine with Brantôme or Sarlat for a stay in the Périgord.

Frequently asked questions

What can you see in Périgueux in a day?

Saint-Front cathedral, the old quarter, the Vesunna tower and the Vesunna museum.

Is the Vesunna museum free?

It is free on the first Sunday of the month; otherwise entry is €8 (€5 reduced).

How long should you spend in Périgueux?

Allow one day.

What is there to see near Périgueux?

In this guide, the closest places are Beynac castle (about 63 km away), Sarlat (about 63 km away), La Roque-Gageac (about 67 km away).
