The Marqueyssac gardens spread across a rocky spur in Vézac, 9 km from Sarlat, facing La Roque-Gageac, Castelnaud and Beynac.

More than 6 km of shady paths lead to the Dordogne viewpoint, 130 metres above the river and rated 3 stars by the Michelin Green Guide.

A bit of history

The Marqueyssac estate was shaped in the 19th century by Julien de Cerval: inspired by Romantic gardens, he added viewpoints, rockeries and dry-stone huts to heighten the picturesque setting.

Long neglected, the gardens were restored from 1996 and opened to the public on 23 March 1997. They now hold the Remarkable Garden label and the viewpoint is a listed site.

Things to see at Marqueyssac

The clipped box

The scene that made the gardens famous: 150,000 century-old box trees clipped by hand into rounded shapes. See tours around Marqueyssac

The Dordogne viewpoint

At the tip of the spur, a panorama over the valley, its castles and La Roque-Gageac.

The cliff walk

A path along the ochre limestone cliffs, with plunging views of the river.

The château

A few rooms of the château, at the start of the walk, can be visited with the gardens.

Candlelit evenings

On Thursday evenings in July and August, the gardens are lit by candles, with live music.

How to get to Marqueyssac

By car

About 15 minutes from Sarlat; free parking.

By canoe

Dordogne canoe trips pass below the Marqueyssac cliffs.

By train

The nearest station is Sarlat; you then need a car or taxi.

Practical info 2026: Marqueyssac Gardens

  • 2026 prices: €12.90 per adult, €6.50 for ages 10 to 17, free for under-10s.
  • Joint ticket with Castelnaud: €24 per adult and €12 for ages 10 to 17.
  • Opening: every day of the year; last admission one hour before closing.

Prices and opening times checked in November 2026 with official websites and local tourist offices; they may change, so check before you go.

Tips for visiting Marqueyssac

  • Allow 2 to 3 hours for the paths and the viewpoint.
  • In summer, come in the morning or late afternoon for cooler air and better light.
  • Book a candlelit evening if you are there on a summer Thursday.
