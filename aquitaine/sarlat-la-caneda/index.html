Sarlat, capital of the Périgord Noir

Golden stone lanes and stone-slab roofs almost untouched since the Renaissance, one of France’s finest markets and the Dordogne castles minutes away.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 13 November 2026

Sarlat has kept one of the best-preserved medieval and Renaissance centres in France: golden stone houses, turrets, stone-slab roofs and narrow lanes. It was the first town to benefit from the 1962 Malraux heritage protection law.

The pedestrian centre takes half a day, but Sarlat is above all the ideal base for exploring the Dordogne valley.

A bit of history

Sarlat grew up around a Benedictine abbey founded in Carolingian times and became the seat of a bishopric in 1317. After the ravages of the Hundred Years’ War the town prospered and filled with mansions, which explains its medieval and Renaissance ensemble.

Bypassed by modernisation, the old town was saved by the 1962 Malraux law: Sarlat was the first town to benefit from it. Its intact setting has since drawn many film shoots.

Things to see in Sarlat

Place de la Liberté

The heart of the old town, lined with old houses, where the big market is held. See tours of Sarlat and the Périgord Noir

Sainte-Marie church and its panoramic lift

The former church, turned into a covered market by architect Jean Nouvel, hides a glass lift in its bell tower that rises above the rooftops.

La Boétie’s house

The Renaissance house where Étienne de La Boétie, Montaigne’s friend, was born in 1530.

Saint-Sacerdos cathedral and the Lantern of the Dead

The former abbey church, now a cathedral, and behind it a curious 12th-century conical tower.

Lanes and mansions

Rue de la Salamandre, Place du Marché-aux-Oies with its bronze geese, and the Hôtel de Maleville.

How to get to Sarlat

By train

TER trains link Bordeaux to Sarlat via Bergerac in about 2 h 40.

By car

About 2 h 30 from Bordeaux; car parks surround the pedestrian centre.

Practical info 2026: Sarlat

  • Panoramic lift: from €4 per adult; open from April to December, weather permitting.
  • Markets: Wednesday morning and Saturday; truffle market on Saturday mornings from December to mid-March.

Prices and opening times checked in November 2026 with official websites and local tourist offices; they may change, so check before you go.

Tips for visiting Sarlat

  • Come on Saturday for the big market, but early: the town is packed in summer.
  • Come back in the evening, when the lanes empty and light up.
  • Park outside the centre and walk in.

Frequently asked questions

What day is Sarlat market?

Saturday (the biggest) and Wednesday morning; in winter there is also a truffle market on Saturday mornings.

What is there to see near Sarlat?

La Roque-Gageac, Beynac, Castelnaud and the Marqueyssac gardens are about fifteen minutes away by car.

How long should you spend in Sarlat?

Allow half a day, more for the valley.
