Château des Milandes, Josephine Baker’s home

A Renaissance château by the Dordogne that became the home of Josephine Baker and her twelve adopted children: discover her life as an entertainer, Resistance member and activist.

Château des Milandes stands in Castelnaud-la-Chapelle, on the left bank of the Dordogne, a few kilometres from Castelnaud Castle.

The visit tells both the story of the château, built from 1489, and that of Josephine Baker, who bought it in 1947. With its gardens listed as a Remarkable Garden and its birds of prey shows, it is a hit with children too.

A bit of history

After the Hundred Years’ War, François de Caumont, lord of Castelnaud, had Les Milandes built from 1489 on the left bank of the Dordogne and left his fortress. The château was remodelled in the early 20th century.

In 1947 Josephine Baker, music-hall star and Resistance figure, bought it to live there with her twelve adopted children, her “Rainbow Tribe”. The château now holds the Maison des Illustres label in her honour.

Things to see at Les Milandes

The Josephine Baker exhibition

More than fourteen rooms trace her life: music hall, the Resistance, the fight against racism and her “Rainbow Tribe”. See tours around Les Milandes

The gardens

Terraced French-style gardens above the valley, listed as a Remarkable Garden.

The birds of prey show

Flying displays with the château’s falconers, included in the ticket.

The chapel

An early 16th-century chapel, open to visitors.

Castelnaud nearby

The fortress and its museum of medieval warfare are a few minutes’ drive away.

How to get to Les Milandes

By car

About 25 minutes from Sarlat; free on-site parking.

By train

The nearest station is Sarlat; you then need a car or taxi.

By canoe

Dordogne canoe trips between Castelnaud and Beynac pass close to the château.

Practical info 2026: Château des Milandes

  • 2026 prices: €15 per adult (17 and over), €9.50 for ages 5 to 16, €12.50 for students, €11.50 for visitors with reduced mobility, free for under-5s.
  • Opening: from February to the Christmas holidays; last admission one hour before closing.
  • On site: brasserie, gift shop and free parking.

Prices and opening times checked in November 2026 with official websites and local tourist offices; they may change, so check before you go.

Tips for visiting Les Milandes

  • Check the birds of prey show times on arrival to plan your visit.
  • Allow about 1 hour for the château and as long for the gardens.
  • Combine with Castelnaud on the same day.

Frequently asked questions

Did Josephine Baker live at Château des Milandes?

Yes, she bought it in 1947 and raised her twelve adopted children there; the château is now devoted to her life.

How much does it cost to visit Château des Milandes?

€15 per adult and €9.50 for ages 5 to 16 in 2026; the birds of prey show is included.

How long should you spend at Les Milandes?

Allow about 2 hours with the gardens.

What is there to see near Les Milandes?

In this guide, the closest places are Beynac castle (about 4 km away), Castelnaud Castle (about 4 km away), La Roque-Gageac (about 7 km away).
