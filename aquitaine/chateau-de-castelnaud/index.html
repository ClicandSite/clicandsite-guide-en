Castelnaud Castle, the fortress facing Beynac

Perched above the Dordogne, right opposite Beynac, Castelnaud houses a museum of medieval warfare, with armour, weapons and full-size siege engines.

Castelnaud Castle towers over the village of Castelnaud-la-Chapelle and the Dordogne valley, 9 km south of Sarlat. It is the most visited castle in the Dordogne.

Restored from the 1970s onwards, it now houses the museum of medieval warfare: a great family outing, with one of the finest views of the valley.

A bit of history

At the start of the 13th century the first castle belonged to Bernard de Casnac, a vassal of the Count of Toulouse, during the Albigensian Crusade. In the Hundred Years’ War it passed by marriage to the Caumont family, often on the English side, while Beynac just opposite stayed French.

In 1442, after a three-week siege ordered by Charles VII, the garrison handed over the castle for 400 gold coins. Sold as national property in the Revolution, it was used as a stone quarry before being listed as a historic monument in 1966 and restored.

Things to see at Castelnaud

The museum of medieval warfare

Armour, swords, crossbows and models explain how castles were attacked and defended. See tours around Castelnaud

The siege engines

In the lower courtyard, full-size reconstructions of trebuchets and other siege machines, with demonstrations in season.

The view

From the ramparts, the view takes in the Dordogne valley, Beynac, Marqueyssac and La Roque-Gageac.

The village

The lanes of Castelnaud-la-Chapelle run steeply down to the river.

Beynac opposite

The rival castle can be visited on the other bank, a few minutes away by car.

How to get to Castelnaud

By car

About 15 minutes from Sarlat. Paid parking above the castle, 3 minutes on foot, or free parking by the Dordogne, 15 minutes on foot.

By canoe

Many rental companies offer trips down the Dordogne that pass below the castle.

By train

The nearest station is Sarlat; you then need a car or taxi.

Practical info 2026: Castelnaud Castle

  • 2026 prices: €13.60 per adult, €6.80 for ages 10 to 17, free for under-10s.
  • Opening: every day, all year; 9 am to 8 pm in July and August, shorter hours the rest of the year. Last entry one hour before closing.
  • Joint ticket: available with the Marqueyssac gardens at a reduced price.

Prices and opening times checked in November 2026 with official websites and local tourist offices; they may change, so check before you go.

Tips for visiting Castelnaud

  • Come early in summer, the upper car park fills quickly.
  • Check the schedule for siege-engine demonstrations.
  • Combine with Beynac or Marqueyssac on the same day.
