The Dune du Pilat, Europe’s tallest

Over a hundred metres of sand between the ocean and the Landes forest, looking out to the Banc d’Arguin and Cap Ferret: an unforgettable climb, especially at sunset.

At the entrance to the Arcachon Bay, the Dune du Pilat is the tallest sand dune in Europe: just over a hundred metres high and nearly three kilometres long, with the ocean on one side and a pine forest on the other.

You climb by a staircase in season or straight up the sand, then walk along the ridge facing the Banc d’Arguin and the tip of Cap Ferret.

A bit of history

The dune formed over a few thousand years from sand brought in by the ocean and driven by westerly winds. It still moves inland every year, gradually burying the pine forest.

Its name comes from the Gascon word “pilot”, meaning a heap or mound. The site, mainly owned by the Conservatoire du Littoral coastal trust, is protected and welcomes hundreds of thousands of visitors a year.

Things to see at the Dune du Pilat

The ridge and the view

From the top you see the ocean, the Banc d’Arguin, Cap Ferret and, inland, forest as far as the eye can see. See trips to the Dune du Pilat and the Arcachon Bay

Sunset

The most spectacular moment: the sun sinks into the ocean facing the dune.

The Banc d’Arguin

A nature reserve of sandbanks below the dune, reached by boat from Arcachon or Cap Ferret.

Arcachon and the Winter Town

The neighbouring resort, with its beach, pier and 19th-century villas on the hill.

How to get to the Dune du Pilat

By train then bus

TER from Bordeaux-Saint-Jean to Arcachon in just under an hour, then the bus line to the dune.

By car

About 1 h 15 from Bordeaux; the main car park is at the foot of the dune, on the forest side.

Practical info 2026: Dune du Pilat

  • Access to the dune: free, all year round.
  • Car parking in high season: €7 for 4 hours, €9 for 8 hours, €11 for 12 hours.
  • Stairs: in place from April to early November to make the climb easier; in winter you climb straight up the sand.

Prices and opening times checked in November 2026 with official websites and local tourist offices; they may change, so check before you go.

Tips for visiting the Dune du Pilat

  • Arrive an hour before sunset.
  • Avoid climbing in the hottest hours in summer.
  • Bring water and a windbreaker, it is windy at the top.

Frequently asked questions

Do you have to pay to visit the Dune du Pilat?

No, access is free; only parking is charged.

Do you have to use the stairs?

No, but they make the climb much easier; they are in place from April to early November.

How long should you spend at the Dune du Pilat?

Allow two to three hours.
