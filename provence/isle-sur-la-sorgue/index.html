L’Isle-sur-la-Sorgue, the Venice of the Comtat

A small town encircled by the arms of a crystal-clear river, moss-covered water wheels and one of France’s biggest gatherings of antique dealers.

L’Isle-sur-la-Sorgue, in the Vaucluse, is ringed by the arms of the Sorgue, a river of clear green water that once turned dozens of mills. Several water wheels remain and have become the town’s emblem.

The town is best known for its antique and second-hand dealers, grouped in antique villages, and for its big Provençal market on Sunday mornings, one of the most famous in the region.

Things to see in L’Isle-sur-la-Sorgue

The water wheels

The old mill wheels, covered in moss, along the quays of the Sorgue. Book a Luberon day trip from L’Isle-sur-la-Sorgue

The Sunday market

A big Provençal market and flea market along the quays, every Sunday morning.

The antique villages

Hundreds of antique and decoration dealers, mostly open Saturday to Monday.

Notre-Dame-des-Anges collegiate church

A church with surprisingly rich Baroque decoration, in the heart of the old town.

Fontaine-de-Vaucluse

The source of the Sorgue at the foot of a cliff, about ten minutes away by car.

How to get to L’Isle-sur-la-Sorgue

By train

TER from Avignon to L’Isle–Fontaine-de-Vaucluse station, in about half an hour.

Nearby

Gordes is twenty minutes away, Avignon half an hour.

Practical info 2026: L’Isle-sur-la-Sorgue

  • Market: Sunday mornings, with a flea market; a smaller market is held on Thursday mornings.
  • Antique dealers: most open Saturday to Monday; some close on weekdays in winter.

Tips for visiting L’Isle-sur-la-Sorgue

  • Arrive early on Sunday: the market is very busy and car parks fill up fast.
  • Head up to Fontaine-de-Vaucluse to see the spring, spectacular after rain.
  • Combine with Gordes and the Luberon villages.

Frequently asked questions

When is the market in L’Isle-sur-la-Sorgue?

On Sunday mornings, with a flea market; a smaller market is also held on Thursday mornings.

Are the antique dealers open all week?

Most open Saturday to Monday; in winter, many close on weekdays.

How do I get to L’Isle-sur-la-Sorgue?

By TER from Avignon in about half an hour, or by car.
