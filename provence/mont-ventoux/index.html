Mont Ventoux is the highest point in the Vaucluse, at 1,910 metres. Its bare summit, covered in white limestone scree, looks like a lunar landscape and can be seen from dozens of kilometres away.

You can go up by car, by bike or on foot. On a clear day, the view stretches from the Alps to the Cévennes and sometimes to the Mediterranean.

A bit of history

Ventoux became a cycling legend through the Tour de France. A memorial near the summit honours the British rider Tom Simpson, who died on its slopes in 1967. The mountain is also a Unesco biosphere reserve.

What to see on Mont Ventoux

The summit

The observatory tower, the chapel and the 360-degree panorama.

Col des Tempêtes

Just below the summit, a windswept balcony with a dizzying view to the north.

Chalet Reynard

Where the Bédoin and Sault roads meet, the starting point for walks and, in winter, sledging and skiing.

Mont Serein

The small resort on the north side, above Malaucène.

How to get up Mont Ventoux

From Bédoin (south side)

The most famous climb: 21.8 km and nearly 1,600 metres of ascent, at an average gradient of 7.5%.

From Malaucène (north side)

21 km, more wooded, via the Mont Serein resort.

From Sault (east side)

The gentlest road, joining the Bédoin road at Chalet Reynard.

Practical info 2026: Mont Ventoux

  • Winter closure: the summit road (D974) closes every year around mid-November, beyond Chalet Reynard and Mont Serein. In 2026 it had reopened on 17 April on the south side and 13 May on the north side.
  • In winter: Chalet Reynard and Mont Serein stay accessible, with winter equipment required.
  • At the top: park only in the designated car parks.

Information checked in November 2026 with the Vaucluse department; opening dates vary each year with the snow, so check before you go.

Tips for going up Mont Ventoux

  • Take a jacket even in summer: it is often 10 to 15 degrees cooler than below, and windy.
  • Go up early in the morning for the clearest view.
  • By bike, take food and water, there is almost nothing between Chalet Reynard and the summit.
  • Check the road is open in spring and autumn.

Frequently asked questions

Can you drive up Mont Ventoux in winter?

Not to the summit: the road closes around mid-November and reopens in spring. You can drive up to Chalet Reynard or Mont Serein.

How high is Mont Ventoux?

1,910 metres.

What is the hardest climb up Ventoux?

The one from Bédoin: 21.8 km at an average gradient of 7.5%, with steeper sections.
