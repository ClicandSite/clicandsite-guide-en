Roussillon, in the Luberon, is one of the most beautiful villages in France. Its houses show every shade of the ochre dug for two centuries from the nearby quarries.

Below the village, the Ochre Trail crosses these old quarries, among coloured cliffs, rock spires and green pines. It is one of the most spectacular walks in Provence.

A bit of history

Ochre was mined in Roussillon from the late 18th century to the mid-20th century to make pigments. The old Mathieu factory is now an ochre museum, Ôkhra.

What to see in Roussillon

The Ochre Trail

Two waymarked loops, of 30 minutes and one hour, through the old quarries.

The village

Sloping lanes, ochre and red façades, and the viewpoint at the top.

Ôkhra, the ochre museum

The old factory where ochre was turned into pigment, with tours and workshops.

The Colorado Provençal

At Rustrel, about twenty minutes away, larger and wilder ochre quarries.

How to get to Roussillon

By car

About 1 hour from Avignon, 15 minutes from Gordes and 15 minutes from Apt. Paid car parks at the entrance to the village.

By bus

Zou! bus 917 links Apt and Roussillon in about twenty minutes.

On a tour

Day trips from Avignon or Aix combine Roussillon, Gordes and Fontaine-de-Vaucluse.

Practical info 2026: the Ochre Trail

  • Price: €3.50 per adult, free under 10; combined ticket with Ôkhra €7.50.
  • Opening: every day from February to early January, with hours that follow the seasons; closed in January.
  • Weather: the trail may close in heavy rain or when the fire risk is high.

Prices and hours checked in November 2026 with the Pays d’Apt Luberon tourist office; they may change, so check before you go.

Tips for visiting Roussillon

  • Do not wear white: ochre stains.
  • Come late in the day, when the cliffs glow their warmest.
  • The trail has many steps: it is not suitable for pushchairs.
  • Combine with Gordes and Fontaine-de-Vaucluse in a day.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the Roussillon Ochre Trail?

€3.50 per adult in 2026, free for under-10s.

How long is the Ochre Trail?

There are two loops: one of 30 minutes and one of about an hour.

Is the Ochre Trail open in winter?

Yes, from February to early January, with shorter hours; it is closed in January.
