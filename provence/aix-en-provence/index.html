Aix-en-Provence, Cézanne’s town

A fountain on every square, golden-stone mansions, markets every morning and Mont Sainte-Victoire on the horizon: Aix is the most elegant town in Provence.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 12 November 2026

The former capital of Provence, Aix is best explored on foot, from the plane-tree-lined Cours Mirabeau to the streets of the Mazarin quarter. It is also the birthplace of Paul Cézanne, who painted Mont Sainte-Victoire more than eighty times.

From 13 November to 31 December 2026, the Christmas market sets up its craft and producer chalets on the Cours Mirabeau. The santon fair runs alongside it, a Provençal tradition before Christmas.

What to see in Aix-en-Provence

The Cours Mirabeau

Aix’s grand avenue, with its plane trees, cafés and the Rotonde fountain.

The markets

The Provençal market on Place Richelme and the flower market on Place de l’Hôtel-de-Ville, every morning.

In Cézanne’s footsteps

The Lauves studio, where the painter worked late in life, and the view of Sainte-Victoire.

The Mazarin quarter

A 17th-century district of mansions around the Four Dolphins fountain.

How to get to Aix-en-Provence

By TGV

About 3 hours from Paris to Aix-en-Provence TGV station, then a shuttle bus to the centre.

From Marseille

Coach shuttle every 10 to 15 minutes, about 40 minutes.

By car

Underground car parks around the old town, which is pedestrianised.

Our tips for visiting Aix-en-Provence

  • Come in the morning for the markets, then have lunch on a café terrace.
  • Try calissons, Aix’s almond and candied-melon speciality.
  • For the view of Sainte-Victoire, walk up to the Terrain des Peintres above Cézanne’s studio.
  • In December, combine the Christmas market and the santon fair in one stroll.

Frequently asked questions

When is the Aix-en-Provence Christmas market in 2026?

From 13 November to 31 December 2026 on the Cours Mirabeau, with craft and local producer chalets. It is closed on 25 December.

How long do you need to visit Aix?

One day is enough for the old town, the markets and Cézanne’s studio. Allow an extra half-day for Sainte-Victoire.

How do you get from Marseille to Aix-en-Provence?

By coach, with departures every 10 to 15 minutes from Saint-Charles station, about 40 minutes.
