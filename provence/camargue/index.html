The Camargue, land of pink flamingos

Lagoons as far as the eye can see, thousands of pink flamingos, white horses roaming free and huge beaches: the Camargue is one of France’s great wild places.

The Camargue stretches between the two arms of the Rhône, south of Arles, down to the Mediterranean. It is a delta of marshes, salt lagoons, rice fields and meadows, protected as a regional nature park.

People come for the pink flamingos, present all year round, for the white horses and black bulls raised by the gardians, and for Saintes-Maries-de-la-Mer, the village by the sea.

Things to see in the Camargue

Pont de Gau bird park

Saintes-Maries-de-la-Mer

The white village by the sea, its fortified church and its arena.

Horses and bulls

The ranches and horse rides through the marshes.

The salt marshes

The salt pans and their pink waters, towards Salin-de-Giraud and Aigues-Mortes.

The wild beaches

Beauduc and Piémanson beaches, huge and unspoilt.

How to get to the Camargue

By car

A car is almost essential; Saintes-Maries-de-la-Mer is about forty minutes from Arles.

By bus

Buses link Arles and Saintes-Maries-de-la-Mer, stopping at the bird park.

Practical info 2026: The Camargue

  • Pink flamingos: visible all year; winter is a good season, with fewer people and fewer mosquitoes.
  • Gypsy pilgrimage: a big festival in Saintes-Maries-de-la-Mer every year at the end of May.

Prices and opening times checked in November 2026 with official websites and local tourist offices; they may change, so check before you go.

Tips for visiting the Camargue

  • Come early in the morning or late in the day to see the flamingos at their most active.
  • Bring mosquito repellent from May to September.
  • Start from Arles to combine the Roman town with wide open spaces.

Frequently asked questions

When can you see pink flamingos in the Camargue?

All year round; the Pont de Gau bird park lets you see them up close.

Do you need a car to visit the Camargue?

It is more practical, but buses link Arles and Saintes-Maries-de-la-Mer.

Can you go horse riding in the Camargue?

Yes, many ranches offer horse rides through the marshes.
