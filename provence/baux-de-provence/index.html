Les Baux-de-Provence and the Carrières des Lumières

A village perched on a spur of the Alpilles, the ruins of a medieval fortress and old quarries turned into a light show: Les Baux is unique in Provence.

By Anthony, Cannes · Updated 12 November 2026

Listed among the most beautiful villages in France, Les Baux-de-Provence sits on a rocky spur in the heart of the Alpilles. The ruined castle covers 7 hectares and looks out as far as the Camargue.

Four hundred metres from the village, the Carrières des Lumières project immersive exhibitions onto their giant walls. In 2026 the show is devoted to Picasso and Frida Kahlo, until 3 January 2027, at a full price of €16.50.

What to see in Les Baux-de-Provence

The Carrières des Lumières

An old limestone quarry where the works of great painters are projected to music.

The Château des Baux

The ruins of the fortress, the keep, reconstructed siege engines and the panorama.

The village

Pedestrian lanes, the Saint-Vincent church and Renaissance mansions.

The Val d’Enfer

A valley of rocks sculpted by erosion, just below the village.

How to get to Les Baux-de-Provence

By car

About 30 minutes from Avignon and 20 minutes from Arles. Paid car parks below the village.

On a tour

Day trips from Avignon, Aix and Marseille combine Les Baux, Saint-Rémy and the Alpilles.

On foot

The Carrières des Lumières are 400 metres from the village, on the Maillane road.

Our tips for visiting Les Baux

  • Get the Pass Baux-de-Provence, which combines the castle and the Carrières des Lumières.
  • Arrive early: the village is small and fills up quickly at weekends.
  • Bring a layer for the Carrières: it is cool under the rock all year round.
  • Combine with Saint-Rémy-de-Provence, 15 minutes away, for a day in the Alpilles.

Frequently asked questions

How much are the Carrières des Lumières?

€16.50 full price, €14 reduced and €13 for ages 7 to 17 in 2026. Free for under-7s.

What is the 2026 exhibition at the Carrières des Lumières?

Picasso and Frida Kahlo, showing since 13 February 2026 and until 3 January 2027.

How long do you need to visit Les Baux?

Half a day for the village, the castle and the Carrières des Lumières.
