Avignon and the Palais des Papes

The largest Gothic palace in Europe, the bridge from the famous song and a whole town still ringed by ramparts: Avignon is easy to explore on foot all year round.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 12 November 2026

In the 14th century the popes moved to Avignon and built a vast fortress-palace. The Palais des Papes and the Pont Saint-Bénezet are Unesco World Heritage sites, and the whole old town can be explored on foot inside the ramparts.

The palace is open every day: 10 am to 5 pm from 2 November to 18 December, then 10 am to 6 pm over the Christmas holidays. Since May 2026 the visitor route has been renewed, with rooms never shown to the public before.

What to see in Avignon

The Palais des Papes

The great state rooms, the chapel, the private apartments and the terraces, with the HistoPad tablet included.

The Pont Saint-Bénezet

The famous Pont d’Avignon, with four arches still standing over the Rhône.

The Rocher des Doms

A garden perched above the palace, with views over the Rhône and Mont Ventoux.

The ramparts and old town

4.3 km of medieval walls, the Place de l’Horloge and the lanes around Les Halles.

How to get to Avignon

By TGV

About 2 h 40 from Paris to Avignon TGV station, then a 5-minute shuttle train to the centre.

By car

Exit Avignon Nord or Sud on the A7. Free car parks with a shuttle to the ramparts.

On a tour

Day trips from Avignon head to the Luberon, Les Baux-de-Provence and the Pont du Gard.

Our tips for visiting Avignon

  • Get the combined palace + bridge ticket, cheaper than two separate tickets.
  • Go up to the Rocher des Doms late in the day for the light over the Rhône.
  • Avignon is the best base for the Luberon and the Alpilles.
  • In December an ice rink is set up in the town centre for the holidays.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to visit the Palais des Papes?

Allow about €16 full price and €13 reduced, free for under-8s. Combined tickets with the Pont d’Avignon and the gardens are available.

What are the Palais des Papes winter opening hours?

10 am to 5 pm from 2 November to 18 December, 10 am to 6 pm from 19 to 31 December. Last entry one hour before closing.

Can you visit Avignon as a day trip from Paris?

Yes, the TGV takes about 2 h 40. One day is enough for the palace, the bridge and the old town.
