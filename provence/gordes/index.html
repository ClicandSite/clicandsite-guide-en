Gordes and the Luberon villages

A stone village clinging to its cliff, a Cistercian abbey at the bottom of a valley and ochre-red villages: the Luberon is picture-postcard Provence.

By Anthony, Cannes · Updated 12 November 2026

Listed among the most beautiful villages in France, Gordes looks out over the Calavon valley from its rocky spur. Four kilometres away, Notre-Dame de Sénanque Abbey, founded in 1148, is still home to a community of Cistercian monks.

The abbey is open all year round. Visits of the interior are by reservation, at €8 for an adult in 2026. Out of season the Luberon villages are calm again, far from the summer crowds.

What to see in the Luberon

Gordes

The hilltop village, its Renaissance château and the viewpoint on the Cavaillon road.

Sénanque Abbey

The abbey church, the cloister and the chapter house, among the lavender fields.

Roussillon

The village of red and orange façades, and the Ochre Trail.

The Village des Bories

A group of dry-stone huts, 2 km from Gordes.

How to get to Gordes

By car

About 45 minutes from Avignon. A car is almost essential in the Luberon.

On a tour

Day trips from Avignon, Aix and Marseille visit Gordes, Sénanque and Roussillon.

To Sénanque

4 km from Gordes on the D177, with the best view of the abbey.

Our tips for visiting the Luberon

  • Book your Sénanque visit in advance: places are limited and the visit is held in silence.
  • The Sénanque lavender blooms from late June to mid-July.
  • Combine Gordes, Sénanque and Roussillon in one day.
  • Come at sunset to photograph Gordes from the Cavaillon road.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to visit Sénanque Abbey?

€8 for an adult in 2026, €6 for students and €4 for children. Booking is required to visit the interior.

When can you see the lavender at Sénanque?

From late June to mid-July, when it is in bloom. The rest of the year the abbey is quiet.

Can you visit the Luberon without a car?

It is difficult. The easiest option is an organised day trip from Avignon, Aix or Marseille.
