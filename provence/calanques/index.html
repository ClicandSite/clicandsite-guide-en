The Calanques of Cassis

White limestone cliffs dropping into turquoise water: between Marseille and Cassis, the Calanques National Park can be explored on foot, by boat or by kayak.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 12 November 2026

The Calanques National Park stretches along 20 km of coast between Marseille and Cassis. From Cassis harbour, three calanques can be reached on foot: Port-Miou, Port-Pin and the most spectacular, En-Vau.

Winter is an excellent season for hiking, quiet and without the summer heat. Boat trips leave Cassis harbour all year round, with more departures from March to November.

What to see in the calanques

Port-Miou

The calanque closest to Cassis, 20 minutes on foot from the centre, lined with boats.

Port-Pin

A small pebble beach shaded by pines, 15 minutes’ walk beyond Port-Miou.

En-Vau

The most famous, framed by tall cliffs and reached by a difficult path.

Cap Canaille

The highest sea cliffs in France, seen from the Route des Crêtes.

How to get to the calanques

On foot from Cassis

Start at Port-Miou. Allow about an hour more for En-Vau, on a steep, rocky path.

By boat

One- to two-hour boat trips leave Cassis harbour and run along the calanques.

From Marseille

Train to Cassis (about 25 minutes) or boat trips from the Old Port.

Our tips for visiting the calanques

  • Wear proper walking shoes: the rocks at Port-Pin and En-Vau are slippery.
  • Bring water, there is nowhere to buy any in the calanques.
  • If you are not a walker, a one-hour boat trip shows all three calanques.
  • In summer some areas may be closed when the fire risk is high.

Frequently asked questions

Can you visit the calanques in winter?

Yes, it is the best season for hiking. Boats leave Cassis harbour all year round, with fewer departures in winter.

How long does it take to walk to En-Vau?

About an hour from Port-Miou on a difficult path, and the same back.

How can you see the calanques without hiking?

By boat from Cassis harbour: one hour is enough to see Port-Miou, Port-Pin and En-Vau.
