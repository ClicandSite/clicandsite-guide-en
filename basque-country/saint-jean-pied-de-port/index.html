Saint-Jean-Pied-de-Port, the pilgrims’ town

A small fortified town of pink sandstone on the Nive, the last French stop on the Way of St James.

Saint-Jean-Pied-de-Port, the former capital of Lower Navarre, nestles at the foot of the Pyrenees, an hour from Bayonne. Its ramparts, citadel and pink sandstone houses line the river Nive.

Every year thousands of pilgrims start the Way of St James to Spain here, over the Roncesvalles pass.

A bit of history

The Saint-Jacques gate, through which pilgrims enter, is on the Unesco World Heritage list with the Routes of Santiago de Compostela. The citadel above the town was strengthened in the 17th century.

What to see in Saint-Jean-Pied-de-Port

Rue de la Citadelle

The cobbled street running down from the Saint-Jacques gate to the Nive, lined with old houses and carved lintels.

The bridge and Notre-Dame church

The old bridge over the Nive and the Gothic church by the Notre-Dame gate: the most photographed view.

The citadel

A steep climb for a view over the town and the mountains.

The Monday market

Sheep’s cheese, chilli pepper, gâteau basque and produce from the valley farms.

Irouléguy vineyards

The terraced vines nearby, one of the smallest wine areas in France.

How to get to Saint-Jean-Pied-de-Port

By train

A TER line links Bayonne with Saint-Jean-Pied-de-Port in about 1 hour 15 minutes, along the Nive.

By car

About 1 hour from Bayonne or Biarritz through the Nive valley.

On foot

The centre is best explored on foot; park outside the ramparts.

Practical info 2026: Saint-Jean-Pied-de-Port

  • Town and ramparts: free access.
  • Market: Monday morning.
  • Pilgrims: pilgrim office on Rue de la Citadelle, for the credential.

Information checked in November 2026; it may change, so check before you go.

Tips for visiting Saint-Jean-Pied-de-Port

  • Come on a Monday for the market.
  • Climb to the citadel late in the day for the light over the valley.
  • Take the train from Bayonne: the ride along the Nive is superb.

Can you get to Saint-Jean-Pied-de-Port by train?

What day is the market in Saint-Jean-Pied-de-Port?
