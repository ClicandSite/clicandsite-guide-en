Hendaye, at the end of the Basque coast

The last French town before Spain: a long family beach, a wild clifftop road and a scientist’s castle.

Hendaye is the last town on the French Basque coast, at the mouth of the Bidassoa, facing Hondarribia in Spain. Its long, sheltered sandy beach is one of the most family-friendly on the coast.

Offshore, the two Deux Jumeaux rocks are the town’s emblem. The corniche towards Saint-Jean-de-Luz has some of the finest cliff scenery in the Basque Country.

A bit of history

The Château d’Abbadia was built from 1864 to 1884 by Eugène Viollet-le-Duc for Antoine d’Abbadie, a scientist and explorer of Ethiopia, who set up an observatory there. In the Bidassoa, Pheasant Island, where the Treaty of the Pyrenees was signed in 1659, changes country every six months.

What to see in Hendaye

The beach and the Deux Jumeaux

About three kilometres of sand facing the two rocks.

Château d’Abbadia

A neo-Gothic castle with an observatory and Oriental-inspired decor, on guided tours.

The Basque corniche

A road and path along the cliffs towards Saint-Jean-de-Luz.

Hondarribia

The Spanish town opposite, with its walled old town, a few minutes away by boat.

How to get to Hendaye

By train

Hendaye is served by TGV and regional trains; the Basque Topo train runs to San Sebastián.

By boat

A shuttle links Hendaye marina and Hondarribia about every 15 minutes, in seven minutes.

By car

About 30 minutes from Biarritz on the motorway.

Practical information 2026: Hendaye

  • Château d’Abbadia: guided tour €12.90, €9.90 for ages 14 to 18; booking online advised.
  • Beach: free access; lifeguards in summer.
  • Spain: an ID card is enough to cross to Hondarribia or San Sebastián.

Our tips for visiting Hendaye

  • Take the shuttle boat for a pintxos dinner in Hondarribia.
  • Walk the Abbadia estate at sunset.

Frequently asked questions

Can you go to Spain from Hendaye on foot or by boat?

Yes: a shuttle boat links Hendaye and Hondarribia in seven minutes, and a bridge crosses the Bidassoa to Irun.

How much is the Château d’Abbadia?

The guided tour costs €12.90, and €9.90 for ages 14 to 18.

Is Hendaye beach good for children?

Yes, it is a large sheltered beach known as one of the most family-friendly on the Basque coast.
