Biarritz, queen of the Basque beaches

An imperial resort turned surf capital, with Belle Époque villas, ocean-battered rocks and big beaches facing the Atlantic.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 12 November 2026

Biarritz owes its fortune to Empress Eugénie, who had her villa built here, now the Hôtel du Palais. The resort has kept its villas, casino and seaside promenades from that era.

Since the 1950s, Biarritz has also been the birthplace of European surfing: the Côte des Basques and the Grande Plage welcome surfers and surf schools all year round.

Things to see in Biarritz

The Rocher de la Vierge

A rock linked to the shore by a metal footbridge, with its statue of the Virgin facing the waves. Book a surf lesson or activity in Biarritz

The Grande Plage and Hôtel du Palais

The main beach, its Art Deco casino and Empress Eugénie’s former villa.

Biarritz lighthouse

A lighthouse on Pointe Saint-Martin, with views along the whole Basque and Landes coast.

The Côte des Basques

The surfers’ beach below the cliffs, with views of the Basque mountains.

The Aquarium and the Cité de l’Océan

Two ocean-themed attractions, ideal in bad weather.

The market hall

The covered market, for ham, sheep’s cheese and Basque tapas.

How to get to Biarritz

By train and plane

Direct TGV from Paris-Montparnasse in just over 4 hours; Biarritz airport is served all year.

Nearby

Bayonne is a quarter of an hour away, Saint-Jean-de-Luz twenty minutes.

Practical info 2026: Biarritz

  • Surfing: schools open all year, wetsuit provided; in winter the waves are bigger and best left to experienced surfers.
  • Rocher de la Vierge: free access, footbridge closed in heavy seas.

Tips for visiting Biarritz

  • Watch the sunset from the lighthouse or the Côte des Basques.
  • Have lunch at the market hall, at the counters of its little tapas bars.
  • Combine with Bayonne and Saint-Jean-de-Luz, close by.

Frequently asked questions

Can you surf in Biarritz in winter?

Yes, schools stay open, but winter waves are often powerful and best suited to experienced surfers.

What can you see in Biarritz in a day?

The Rocher de la Vierge, the Grande Plage, the lighthouse, the Côte des Basques and the market hall.

How do I get to Biarritz from Paris?

By direct TGV from Paris-Montparnasse in just over 4 hours, or by plane.
