Ainhoa, a few kilometres from the Spanish border, is listed among the most beautiful villages in France. Its Labourd-style houses with red or green timbering line a single main street.

The village can be seen in an hour or two, and combines easily with its neighbours Espelette and Sare.

A bit of history

Ainhoa is a 13th-century bastide town, a stop on a route to Spain and Santiago. Many of its houses were rebuilt in the 17th century, and their lintels still bear the families’ names and dates.

What to see in Ainhoa

The main street

White half-timbered houses, wooden balconies and carved lintels, over a few hundred metres.

Notre-Dame-de-l’Assomption church

A typical Basque church, with wooden galleries on several levels.

The fronton

The Basque pelota court, in the heart of the village.

The Aubépine chapel

A chapel on the hill above the village, with stations of the cross and a view over the whole valley.

How to get to Ainhoa

By car

About 30 minutes from Bayonne and Saint-Jean-de-Luz, and 10 minutes from Espelette.

On a tour

Day trips from Biarritz or Bayonne combine Ainhoa, Espelette and Sare.

On foot

You can walk up to the Aubépine chapel from the village in about half an hour.

Practical info 2026: Ainhoa

  • Village: free access.
  • Parking: car parks at the entrance to the village.
  • Border: the Dancharia venta, on the Spanish side, is a few minutes away.

Information checked in November 2026; it may change, so check before you go.

Tips for visiting Ainhoa

  • Come early in the morning or late in the day to photograph the main street without the crowds.
  • Walk up to the Aubépine chapel for the view.
  • Combine with Espelette, 10 minutes away, and the Rhune train.

Frequently asked questions

How long do you need in Ainhoa?

One to two hours is enough; allow half a day with the walk up to the Aubépine chapel.

Is Ainhoa one of the most beautiful villages in France?

Yes, Ainhoa is one of the Plus Beaux Villages de France.

What is there to see near Ainhoa?

Espelette, Sare and the Rhune train are less than 20 minutes away.
