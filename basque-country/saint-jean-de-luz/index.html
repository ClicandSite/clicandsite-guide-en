Saint-Jean-de-Luz, the king’s Basque port

A sheltered bay, a colourful fishing port and the church where Louis XIV was married: Saint-Jean-de-Luz is the most charming town on the Basque coast.

Saint-Jean-de-Luz, a few kilometres from Spain, is a fishing port renowned for tuna and anchovies. Its large bay, closed off by sea walls, offers one of the calmest beaches on the Basque coast.

It was here, in 1660, that Louis XIV married the Infanta Maria Theresa of Spain, in the church of Saint-Jean-Baptiste. The houses where the king and the infanta stayed can still be seen near the port.

Things to see in Saint-Jean-de-Luz

The bay and the beach

A large sandy beach sheltered from the waves by sea walls, ideal for family swimming. Book a boat trip or tour in Saint-Jean-de-Luz

The fishing port

Colourful boats, the quays and views of Ciboure and La Rhune.

Saint-Jean-Baptiste church

The church of Louis XIV’s wedding, with its wooden galleries and great gilded altarpiece.

The Maison Louis XIV and the Maison de l’Infante

The shipowners’ houses where the king and his bride stayed in 1660.

Rue Gambetta

The pedestrian shopping street, with its pâtisseries, macarons and Basque linen.

La Rhune

The emblematic mountain of the Basque Country, climbed on foot or by little rack railway from the Col de Saint-Ignace, in season.

How to get to Saint-Jean-de-Luz

By train

TGV from Paris-Montparnasse to Saint-Jean-de-Luz–Ciboure, in just over 4 hrs 30.

Nearby

Biarritz is twenty minutes away, Hendaye and the Spanish border a quarter of an hour.

Practical info 2026: Saint-Jean-de-Luz

  • La Rhune rack railway: it only runs for part of the year, usually spring to autumn; check the dates before you go.
  • Beach: the bay is lifeguarded in season and sheltered from the swell, unlike Biarritz’s beaches.

Tips for visiting Saint-Jean-de-Luz

  • Try Saint-Jean-de-Luz macarons and Basque cake on Rue Gambetta.
  • Walk across the port to Ciboure to see the town from the other bank.
  • Combine with Espelette and the inland villages.

Frequently asked questions

Where did Louis XIV get married?

In the church of Saint-Jean-Baptiste in Saint-Jean-de-Luz, to the Infanta Maria Theresa, in 1660.

Is the beach at Saint-Jean-de-Luz good for children?

Yes, the bay is sheltered by sea walls and much calmer than Biarritz’s beaches.

How do you get up La Rhune?

On foot, or by the little rack railway from the Col de Saint-Ignace, which only runs part of the year.
