La Rhune, Larrun in Basque, is the iconic mountain of the Basque Country. Its 905-metre summit sits on the French–Spanish border, with views of the ocean, the coast from Biarritz to San Sebastián and the Pyrenees.

You can walk up or take the little rack railway, which leaves from the Col de Saint-Ignace, between Sare and Ascain.

A bit of history

The Rhune railway opened in 1924. Its wooden carriages climb at 9 km/h on a 4.2 km track, with gradients of more than 20%. The track was completely relaid in 2023.

What to see on La Rhune

The panorama

The ocean, the bay of Saint-Jean-de-Luz, the Landes and, on the other side, the peaks of the Pyrenees.

The little train

A climb of about 35 minutes in period carriages, through the moorland.

The pottoks

Small Basque horses roaming the slopes, along with sheep and vultures.

The ventas

At the top, on the Spanish side, traditional shops and bars.

How to get to La Rhune

By car

The train station is at the Col de Saint-Ignace, about 15 minutes from Saint-Jean-de-Luz via Ascain. Parking on site.

On foot

Several trails climb to the summit from Sare, Ascain or the Col de Saint-Ignace; allow several hours there and back.

On a tour

Day trips to the Basque villages combine Sare, Ainhoa and Espelette.

Practical info 2026: La Rhune

  • Train season: 28 March to 1 November 2026; closed in winter, reopening planned on 28 March 2027.
  • 2026 prices: return trip €26 for adults, €18 for ages 4 to 12, free under 4.
  • Departures: several departures a day, more frequent in July and August.

Prices and dates checked in November 2026 with the Train de la Rhune and the tourist office; they may change, so check before you go.

Tips for going up La Rhune

  • Book online in summer: trains often leave full.
  • Go up on a clear day, the view vanishes quickly in the clouds.
  • Bring a jacket, it is cooler and windier at the top.
  • Combine with the villages of Sare and Ainhoa.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the Rhune train?

In 2026, €26 return for adults and €18 for ages 4 to 12.

When does the Rhune train run?

From 28 March to 1 November 2026. It is closed in winter and due to reopen on 28 March 2027.

Can you walk up La Rhune?

Yes, several trails start from Sare, Ascain and the Col de Saint-Ignace.
