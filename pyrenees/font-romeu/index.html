Font-Romeu: skiing in the Cerdagne sunshine

A sun-drenched plateau, slopes through the pine trees and a little yellow train to get there. Font-Romeu is the big resort of the Catalan Pyrenees.

Font-Romeu overlooks the Cerdagne, a high plateau in the Pyrénées-Orientales known for its sunshine. Together with Bolquère-Pyrénées 2000 it forms a ski area of about 43 runs rising to the Roc de la Calme at 2,213 metres, covered by more than 500 snow cannons.

For 2026-2027, opening is announced for 4 December 2026, until late March or early April 2027 depending on snow. It is one of the first Pyrenean resorts to open.

Things to do in Font-Romeu

The Font-Romeu Pyrénées 2000 ski area

Wide runs through the pines, ideal for improving, with a few steeper sections at La Calme.

Cross-country skiing

More than 100 kilometres of cross-country trails around the resort, among the finest in the Pyrenees.

The Odeillo solar furnace

A huge parabolic mirror just below the resort, one of the largest in the world, with a visitor centre.

The Font-Romeu hermitage

A baroque chapel in the middle of the forest, reached on foot or on snowshoes.

How to get to Font-Romeu

By train

Regional train to Villefranche-de-Conflent or Latour-de-Carol, then the Little Yellow Train to Font-Romeu-Odeillo-Via station. The ride is a sight in itself.

From Perpignan

About 1 hour 45 minutes by road up the Têt valley.

From Toulouse

About 2 hours 30 minutes by road via the Ariège and the Puymorens pass.

Tips for a ski trip to Font-Romeu

  • Pack sunglasses and sun cream: the Cerdagne sun is strong, even in January.
  • Head for La Calme in the afternoon, when the south-facing runs get heavy.
  • Keep half a day for the Little Yellow Train or the solar furnace.
