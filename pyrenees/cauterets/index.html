Cauterets: the snow of the Cirque du Lys

A small spa town, a gondola into a mountain cirque and one of the longest seasons in the Pyrenees.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 12 November 2026

Cauterets is a spa town in the Hautes-Pyrénées, on the edge of the national park. Its ski area, the Cirque du Lys, runs from about 1,730 to 2,415 metres and gets some of the heaviest snowfall in the range. You reach it from the town centre by the Lys gondola.

For 2026-2027, the Cirque du Lys opens as early as 27 November 2026, just after Luz-Ardiden, with the season planned until 18 April 2027.

Things to do in Cauterets

The Cirque du Lys

A mountain amphitheatre with runs for every level and a freestyle park.

The Pont d’Espagne

A protected natural site with frozen waterfalls, cross-country trails and snowshoe walks.

Les Bains du Rocher

The town’s thermal spa, with outdoor pools facing the mountains.

Berlingots

Cauterets’ signature boiled sweets, made in front of visitors.

How to get to Cauterets

From Lourdes

About 40 minutes by road. Buses link Lourdes station to Cauterets.

By train

TGV or regional train to Lourdes, then a bus.

Getting around

The Lys gondola leaves from the town centre: no car needed to ski.

Tips for a ski trip to Cauterets

  • Make the most of the early opening in late November to ski before everyone else.
  • Mix downhill skiing at the Lys with snowshoeing at the Pont d’Espagne.
  • Keep an evening for Les Bains du Rocher.

Frequently asked questions

When does Cauterets open in 2026?

The Cirque du Lys opens on 27 November 2026, depending on snow, until 18 April 2027.

Can you ski in Cauterets without a car?

Yes, the Lys gondola leaves from the centre and buses link Lourdes to Cauterets.

What is there to do in Cauterets besides skiing?

Snowshoeing at the Pont d’Espagne, Les Bains du Rocher and a stroll round town.
