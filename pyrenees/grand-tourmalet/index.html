Grand Tourmalet: La Mongie, Barèges and the Pic du Midi

Two resorts linked by the slopes on either side of the Tourmalet pass, and a cable car up to the Pic du Midi observatory.

By Anthony, Cannes · Updated 12 November 2026

The Grand Tourmalet brings together La Mongie, a ski-in ski-out resort at about 1,800 metres, and Barèges, an old spa village, on either side of the famous pass. The area has around 100 kilometres of runs and rises to 2,500 metres, making it one of the largest in the Pyrenees.

For 2026-2027 it is open from 5 December 2026 to 29 March 2027. The big new feature this season is the Tournaboup gondola on the Barèges side.

Things to do on the Grand Tourmalet

The Pic du Midi

A cable car leaves La Mongie for the observatory at 2,877 metres, with views over the whole Pyrenees range.

La Mongie

The modern, ski-in ski-out resort, with plenty of blue runs for families.

Barèges

A quieter stone village, starting point for the wooded runs of Tournaboup.

Freeride

The slopes of the Pic du Midi offer renowned off-piste descents, to do with a guide.

How to get to the Grand Tourmalet

From Lourdes

About 1 hour by road to Barèges or La Mongie. Lourdes is served by TGV trains.

By plane

Tarbes-Lourdes airport is about 1 hour away, Toulouse 2 hours 15 minutes.

Good to know

The Tourmalet pass road is closed in winter: you cross from one side to the other on skis, not by car.

Tips for a ski trip to the Grand Tourmalet

  • Pick your side for the night: La Mongie for ski-in ski-out, Barèges for village charm.
  • Go up the Pic du Midi on a clear day, and book ahead in school holidays.
  • Do not go off-piste without a guide: the Pic du Midi terrain is demanding.

Frequently asked questions

When does the Grand Tourmalet open in 2026?

On 5 December 2026, until 29 March 2027, depending on snow.

Can you go up the Pic du Midi in winter?

Yes, by the cable car from La Mongie, weather permitting.

Can you drive from La Mongie to Barèges in winter?

No, the Tourmalet pass is closed to traffic in winter: you have to go round by the valley.
