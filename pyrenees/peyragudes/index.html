Peyragudes: two sides and a run straight out of a film

A resort straddling two valleys, a run named after James Bond and a gondola rising from the lake at Loudenvielle.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 12 November 2026

Peyragudes spreads across the Hautes-Pyrénées and Haute-Garonne on two linked sides, Peyresourde and Les Agudes. The ski area of about sixty kilometres rises to 2,400 metres at the Cap des Hittes. Its altiport appears in the opening of a James Bond film, hence the name of the 007 run.

For 2026-2027, Peyragudes plans an early opening from 5 to 9 December 2026, then full opening from 12 December 2026 to 29 March 2027, depending on snow.

Things to do in Peyragudes

The 007 run

A long blue run passing near the altiport, with views of the border peaks.

The Skyvall

The gondola that takes you from Loudenvielle up to the slopes in minutes.

Cap des Hittes

The highest point, at 2,400 metres, with its freeride routes.

Balnéa

Loudenvielle’s big thermal spa on the lakeside, at the foot of the gondola.

How to get to Peyragudes

From Toulouse

About 2 hours by road on the A64.

From Luchon

About fifteen kilometres over the Peyresourde pass.

Via the Louron valley

Ride the Skyvall gondola up from Loudenvielle and leave the car behind.

Tips for a ski trip to Peyragudes

  • The Fast Pass, now available across the resort, cuts queues at the main lifts in the holidays for an extra charge.
  • Stay in Loudenvielle to combine skiing with Balnéa.
  • Look at the shared Aure and Louron valley pass to ski Saint-Lary too.

Frequently asked questions

When does Peyragudes open in 2026?

Early opening from 5 to 9 December 2026, then open from 12 December 2026 to 29 March 2027, depending on snow.

Why is there a 007 run in Peyragudes?

The resort’s altiport appears at the start of the film Tomorrow Never Dies.

Can you reach Peyragudes without a car?

Yes from Loudenvielle, on the Skyvall gondola.
