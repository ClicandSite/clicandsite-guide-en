Saint-Lary: a real village and 100 km of runs

Take the gondola from the village and ski up to more than 2,500 metres. Saint-Lary is one of the largest ski areas in the French Pyrenees.

Saint-Lary-Soulan is a village in the Aure valley, in the Hautes-Pyrénées, linked to its ski area by a gondola up to Pla d’Adet. The area has about 100 kilometres of runs across three sectors, up to 2,515 metres.

For 2026-2027, opening is planned for 4 December 2026, until late March or early April 2027 depending on snow.

Things to do in Saint-Lary

The three sectors

Pla d’Adet, Espiaube and the Vallon du Portet: a change of scene without taking your skis off.

The snowpark

One of the best-known parks in the Pyrenees, which hosts freestyle competitions.

Sensoria

The village’s thermal spa, to relax after a day on the slopes.

The Néouvielle reserve

Lakes and mountain pines above the valley, to explore on snowshoes with a guide.

How to get to Saint-Lary

From Toulouse

About 2 hours by road on the A64, exit Lannemezan.

By train

To Lannemezan station, then a bus to Saint-Lary.

By plane

Tarbes-Lourdes airport, about 1 hour 15 minutes by road, or Toulouse-Blagnac.

Tips for a ski trip to Saint-Lary

  • Stay in the village and take the gondola up: you get village life in the evening.
  • Look at the shared pass for the Aure and Louron valley resorts if you are staying a week.
  • Book Sensoria ahead during French school holidays.

Frequently asked questions

When does Saint-Lary open in 2026?

Opening is planned for 4 December 2026, depending on snow.

How do you get to the slopes from the village?

By the gondola linking Saint-Lary village to Pla d’Adet.

Is Saint-Lary a big resort?

Yes, with about 100 kilometres of runs it is one of the largest ski areas in the French Pyrenees.
