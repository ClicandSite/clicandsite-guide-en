Châtel: a Savoyard village on the Swiss border

A real mountain village at the head of the Abondance valley, slopes running into Switzerland and a family feel. Châtel is the other gateway to the Portes du Soleil.

Châtel is the last village in the Abondance valley, close to the Swiss border. Its resort is linked to the Portes du Soleil, a huge Franco-Swiss ski area of about 600 kilometres of pistes that it shares with Avoriaz, Morzine and Champéry, among others.

For 2026-2027, Châtel is open from 19 December 2026 to 18 April 2027, with a partial early opening on 5 and 6 December and from 11 to 18 December, depending on snow.

Things to do in Châtel

The Portes du Soleil

From Châtel you can ski into Switzerland, towards Morgins or Torgon, or to Avoriaz on the French side.

The village

Wooden chalets, farms and cheese makers: Châtel has kept the spirit of an alpine farming village.

The Abondance valley

A valley known for its AOP cheese and its abbey, worth a stop on the way down.

Off the slopes

Snowshoeing, tobogganing, dog sledding and a water park.

How to get to Châtel

By train

To Thonon-les-Bains, then a bus to Châtel through the Abondance valley.

From Geneva

About 1 hour 30 minutes by road, with transfers in season.

By car

Via Thonon-les-Bains and the Abondance valley.

Tips for a ski trip to Châtel

  • In December, check which sectors are open: the early opening covers only part of the area.
  • Get the Portes du Soleil pass for at least one day to ski into Switzerland.
  • Try Abondance, the valley’s cheese, at a farm in the village.

Frequently asked questions

When does Châtel open in 2026?

On 19 December 2026, with a partial early opening on 5 and 6 December and from 11 to 18 December, until 18 April 2027.

Is Châtel part of the Portes du Soleil?

Yes, the resort is linked to this Franco-Swiss area, with Avoriaz, Morzine and Champéry.

How do you get to Châtel without a car?

By train to Thonon-les-Bains, then by bus.
