Serre Chevalier: larch forests and southern sunshine

A huge area linking four villages, with runs through larch forest. Serre Chevalier is the Alps with southern light.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 12 November 2026

Serre Chevalier stretches along the Guisane valley from Briançon to Le Monêtier-les-Bains, via Chantemerle and Villeneuve. Its area has about 410 hectares of marked pistes and 59 lifts, with many runs through larch forest, welcome in bad weather.

For 2026-2027, Serre Chevalier is open continuously from 5 December 2026 to 18 April 2027, depending on snow.

Things to do in Serre Chevalier

Les Grands Bains du Monêtier

Natural hot-water pools in the open air, facing the mountains: the best moment of the day after skiing.

Briançon

The highest town in France, with its Vauban fortified city, a UNESCO World Heritage site.

Forest runs

The larches shelter you from the wind and improve visibility when it snows.

Le Monêtier-les-Bains

The most authentic village in the valley, at the foot of the highest runs.

How to get to Serre Chevalier

By train

To Briançon station, including the night train from Paris, then a shuttle to the villages.

From Grenoble

About 2 hours by road over the Col du Lautaret.

From Italy

Turin is about 2 hours away via the Col de Montgenèvre.

Tips for a ski trip to Serre Chevalier

  • Stay in Le Monêtier for the Grands Bains and the highest snow, or in Briançon for the town.
  • The area is very long: plan your days sector by sector.
  • Book the Grands Bains for late afternoon, the nicest time.

Frequently asked questions

When does Serre Chevalier open in 2026?

Continuously from 5 December 2026 to 18 April 2027, depending on snow.

Can you get to Serre Chevalier by train?

Yes, to Briançon station, with a night train from Paris, then a shuttle.

The Grands Bains du Monêtier, Briançon’s Vauban city, snowshoeing and dog sledding.
