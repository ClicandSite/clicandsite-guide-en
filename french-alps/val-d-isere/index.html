Val d’Isère: skiing the Tignes-Val d’Isère area

A real mountain village at the head of the Tarentaise, a huge ski area linked with Tignes and the Olympic Bellevarde run. Val d’Isère opens in late November.

By Anthony, Cannes · Updated 12 November 2026

Val d’Isère grew up around its old village and church, below the Col de l’Iseran, which is closed in winter. Together with Tignes it forms one of the largest linked ski areas in France, known for high-altitude snow and off-piste skiing.

For the 2026-2027 season, Val d’Isère announces an opening on 28 November 2026, depending on snow, with a season running into spring.

Things to do in Val d’Isère

Face de Bellevarde

The 1992 Olympic downhill run, steep and demanding, finishing in the heart of the resort. For very strong skiers.

The old village

The church and stone houses of the historic centre, a short walk from the lifts.

Le Fornet and Pissaillas

The head of the valley, with a glacier and a wilder feel.

The link with Tignes

With an area pass you can ski over to Tignes for a full day across both resorts.

How to get to Val d’Isère

By train

Train to Bourg-Saint-Maurice, then a bus or shuttle up to Val d’Isère at the head of the valley.

By plane

The nearest airports are Chambéry, Geneva and Lyon, with shuttles and private transfers.

By car

Through the Tarentaise valley and Bourg-Saint-Maurice. Winter equipment is compulsory.

Tips for skiing in Val d’Isère

  • Off-piste is one of Val d’Isère’s great assets: always go with a guide or instructor.
  • Early in the season the high sectors open first: check which runs are open before you go.
  • Book early for Christmas and February: the resort is in high demand with an international crowd.
