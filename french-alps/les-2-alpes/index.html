Les 2 Alpes: glacier skiing from late November

A glacier rising to 3,600 metres, a season that starts in late November and a young, lively atmosphere. Les 2 Alpes is the big resort of the Isère.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 12 November 2026

Les 2 Alpes stretches across a plateau at 1,650 metres, above the Romanche valley. Its area of more than 200 kilometres climbs to the Mont-de-Lans glacier, one of the largest skiable glaciers in Europe, which means snow at the start and end of the season.

For 2026-2027 the resort opens on 28 November 2026 and the winter season runs until 2 May 2027, followed by a few weeks of glacier skiing in spring. It is one of the first big resorts to open.

Things to do in Les 2 Alpes

The glacier

The top of the area, above 3,000 metres, with easy runs and views of the Meije and the Écrins on a clear day.

The ice cave

A tunnel dug into the glacier with ice sculptures, open to non-skiers via the lifts.

The snowpark

One of the best known in the Alps, with zones for beginners and experts.

Off-piste in La Grave

Next door, the ungroomed terrain of La Grave draws expert skiers, always with a guide.

Après-ski

Bars, concerts and a lively evening atmosphere along the main street.

How to get to Les 2 Alpes

From Grenoble

About 1 hour 15 to 1 hour 30 minutes by road. Buses link Grenoble bus station to the resort in season.

By train

TGV to Grenoble, then a bus to Les 2 Alpes.

From the airports

Lyon-Saint-Exupéry or Grenoble-Alpes-Isère, with transfers in season.

Tips for a ski trip to Les 2 Alpes

  • In December, stick to the upper slopes: the lower runs can be short of snow early in the season.
  • Head up to the glacier early on a clear day; the light and snow are best in the morning.
  • Never go off-piste in La Grave without a mountain guide.

Frequently asked questions

When does Les 2 Alpes open in 2026?

On 28 November 2026, until 2 May 2027, followed by a few weeks of glacier skiing in spring.

Can you ski on the glacier in Les 2 Alpes?

Yes, the Mont-de-Lans glacier is skiable in winter and part of the spring.

How do you get to Les 2 Alpes without a car?

By train to Grenoble, then by bus to the resort.
