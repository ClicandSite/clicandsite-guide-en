Alpe d’Huez: sunny skiing and the Sarenne run

The Alps’ island in the sun: a sunny ski area, Pic Blanc at 3,330 metres and La Sarenne, one of the longest black runs in the world.

In the heart of Isère, Alpe d’Huez is known for its sunshine and its big ski area, linked to the villages of Auris-en-Oisans, Oz, Vaujany, Villard-Reculas and Huez. It is also famous for its Tour de France hairpins.

For the 2026-2027 season the resort announces an opening on 5 December 2026 and a closing on 25 April 2027, depending on snow.

Things to do in Alpe d’Huez

Pic Blanc

At 3,330 metres, the top of the area offers a vast panorama; on a clear day you can see much of the Alps, as far as Mont Blanc.

La Sarenne

A 16-kilometre black run from Pic Blanc down to the Sarenne gorge, one of the longest in the world. It eases off after the first section.

The Tunnel run

A descent that goes right through the mountain, for confident skiers.

The linked villages

Auris, Oz, Vaujany and Villard-Reculas, quieter and often more affordable, on the same ski area.

How to get to Alpe d’Huez

From Grenoble

About 1 hour 30 minutes by road or bus from Grenoble, which has a train station.

By plane

Grenoble-Isère and Lyon airports, with shuttles in season.

By car

Via Bourg-d’Oisans and the famous 21-hairpin climb. Winter equipment is compulsory.

Tips for skiing in Alpe d’Huez

  • Go up Pic Blanc early in the morning: the snow is better and the queue shorter. You can book a slot for the gondola.
  • Only take on La Sarenne if you are a strong skier: the top is steep and the bottom very long.
  • For a quieter stay, base yourself in one of the linked villages.
