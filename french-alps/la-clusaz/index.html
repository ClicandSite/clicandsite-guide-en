La Clusaz: a real village in the Aravis

A church with an onion-domed bell tower, wooden chalets and five mountain areas to ski, less than an hour from Annecy. La Clusaz is the classic village resort.

La Clusaz is an old mountain village in the heart of the Aravis range, about 40 minutes from Annecy. Its ski area spreads across five mountain sectors with different moods, from tree-lined runs to open slopes, and the resort will host events of the 2030 Winter Olympics.

For 2026-2027, La Clusaz is open from 12 December 2026 to 25 April 2027, with an early opening on 5 and 6 December, depending on snow.

Things to do in La Clusaz

The village

The church, the chalets and the lively streets: the resort has kept a real village life all year.

The Balme area

The highest and wildest sector, popular with strong skiers.

Beauregard and L’Étale

Easy, sunny slopes, ideal for families and beginners.

Reblochon

The Aravis cheese, to try in a tartiflette or straight from the farm.

How to get to La Clusaz

From Annecy

About 40 minutes by road. In season, buses link Annecy station to the resort.

From Geneva

About 1 hour by road, with transfers in season.

By train

TGV to Annecy, then a bus to La Clusaz.

Tips for a ski trip to La Clusaz

  • Avoid Saturdays in February: the resort draws many day skiers from Annecy and Geneva.
  • Combine with Le Grand-Bornand, the neighbouring Aravis resort.
  • Keep an evening for Annecy and its old town, 40 minutes away.

When does La Clusaz open in 2026?
