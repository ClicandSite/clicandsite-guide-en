Chamonix: skiing and trips below Mont Blanc

The capital of mountaineering, facing Mont Blanc. People come to ski, but also to go up to almost 3,900 metres without being climbers.

Chamonix is not a single resort but a valley of separate ski areas: Brévent and Flégère facing Mont Blanc, Grands Montets above Argentière, Balme at the head of the valley and Les Houches at its entrance. Free valley shuttles link them.

For the 2026-2027 season, the first areas, Grands Montets and Flégère, are due to open at the end of November, with the others following in December. The Aiguille du Midi cable car and the Montenvers train close for a few weeks of maintenance every November before reopening for winter.

Things to do in Chamonix

The Aiguille du Midi

The cable car climbs to 3,842 metres in about twenty minutes. At the top: terraces facing Mont Blanc and the Step into the Void, a glass box above a 1,000-metre drop. Go in the morning, on a clear day.

The Montenvers train and the Mer de Glace

A red rack railway runs from Chamonix to Montenvers, facing France’s largest glacier. A gondola and steps lead down to the ice cave dug out every year.

The Vallée Blanche

A legendary off-piste run of about twenty kilometres from the Aiguille du Midi. It must be done with a mountain guide.

Brévent and Flégère

The two sunny-side ski areas, with the finest views of the Mont Blanc massif. Ideal for intermediate skiers.

Grands Montets

The area for strong skiers, above Argentière: big slopes, high-altitude snow and glacier views.

Chamonix town centre

Pedestrian streets, Belle Époque façades and the statue of Balmat and Saussure pointing at Mont Blanc. Après-ski happens here.

How to get to Chamonix

By train

TGV or TER to Saint-Gervais-les-Bains - Le Fayet, then the Mont-Blanc Express to Chamonix in about forty minutes.

From Geneva airport

About 1 hour 15 minutes by road. Shuttles and private transfers run all season.

Getting around

Valley shuttles serve the ski areas. The guest card from your accommodation gives access to local transport.

Tips for a winter trip to Chamonix

  • Check the summit weather before going up the Aiguille du Midi: on a cloudy day you will see nothing.
  • Off-piste skiing in Chamonix means high mountains: never go without a guide and safety equipment.
  • For February, book your accommodation early: the valley is in high demand during school holidays.

Frequently asked questions

When does Chamonix open in 2026?

The first areas, Grands Montets and Flégère, are due to open at the end of November 2026, with the rest of the valley in December, depending on snow.

Can you go up the Aiguille du Midi in winter?

Yes, the cable car runs in winter after its annual maintenance closure in November. Check the summit weather before you go.

How do you get to Chamonix without a car?

By train to Saint-Gervais-Le Fayet then the Mont-Blanc Express, or by shuttle from Geneva airport.
