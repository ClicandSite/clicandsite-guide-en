La Grave: freeride beneath La Meije

Next to no groomed runs, a cable car to 3,200 metres and more than 2,000 metres of descent: La Grave is the mecca of off-piste skiing.

La Grave is a small village in the Oisans, at the foot of La Meije (3,983 m). There is no big resort here: a cable car climbs from 1,450 to 3,200 metres, and a drag lift on the Girose glacier can take you up to 3,550 metres. Apart from the single groomed run on the glacier, the area is neither groomed nor patrolled: you ski at your own risk.

For winter 2026-2027 the cable car is announced open from 20 December 2026 to 3 May 2027, depending on snow and weather.

Things to do in La Grave

Les Vallons de la Meije

One of the great classic off-piste descents, for experienced, fully equipped skiers only.

The Girose glacier

The only groomed run in the area, at high altitude.

The view from 3,200 m

Open to pedestrians: views of La Meije, Mont Blanc and the Vanoise, with a restaurant at the top.

The ice cave

Carved into the glacier, near the top cable car station.

How to get to La Grave

From Grenoble

About 1 hour 45 minutes by road up the Romanche valley.

From Briançon

About 45 minutes over the Lautaret pass.

By train

To Briançon or Grenoble, then a bus.

Tips for a ski trip to La Grave

  • Go with a guide or instructor: the terrain is glaciated and not patrolled.
  • Avalanche transceiver, shovel and probe are essential to ski off the glacier.
  • Not an expert skier? Ride up as a pedestrian for the view and the ice cave.

Frequently asked questions

When does the La Grave cable car open in 2026?

It is announced open from 20 December 2026 to 3 May 2027, depending on snow and weather.

Is La Grave suitable for beginners?

Not for skiing: the area is mostly unpatrolled off-piste. Pedestrians can still ride up to enjoy the view.

Do you need a guide in La Grave?

It is strongly advised: the terrain is glaciated and you ski at your own risk.
