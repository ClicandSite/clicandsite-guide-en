Avoriaz: the car-free resort of the Portes du Soleil

Not a single car in the streets, horse-drawn sleighs instead of taxis and slopes all the way into Switzerland. Avoriaz is a resort like no other.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 12 November 2026

Perched at 1,800 metres above Morzine, Avoriaz is an entirely car-free resort, with wood-clad buildings that follow the cliff. You get around on foot, on skis or by horse-drawn sleigh.

Its area of about 75 kilometres sits at the centre of the Portes du Soleil, one of the largest ski areas in the world with about 600 kilometres of pistes across France and Switzerland. For 2026-2027, Avoriaz is open from 11 December 2026 to 18 April 2027, with early-opening weekends in late November and early December depending on snow.

Things to do in Avoriaz

The Portes du Soleil

In a single day you can ski across the border to Champéry or Morgins in Switzerland, then come back via Châtel or Les Lindarets.

The snowparks

Avoriaz was one of the first resorts in France to welcome snowboarding; its parks, including The Stash in the forest, are still a benchmark.

The children’s village

A dedicated area for little ones, with a ski school and snow gardens.

Aquariaz

A tropical water park with slides, ideal on bad-weather days.

Les Lindarets

The “goat village” in the middle of the ski area, with its mountain restaurants.

How to get to Avoriaz

From Geneva

About 1 hour 30 minutes by road to Avoriaz, with shuttles and private transfers in season. This is the easiest way.

By train

To Cluses or Thonon-les-Bains, then a bus to Morzine and Avoriaz.

By car

Cars stay in the car parks at the resort entrance; you can also go up from Morzine on the Prodains cable car.

Tips for a ski trip to Avoriaz

  • Get the Portes du Soleil pass for at least one day to do the France-Switzerland loop.
  • Plan a luggage transfer or a sleigh to reach your accommodation: the resort is car-free.
  • Keep an eye on the weather: the area is exposed to wind and some links close in bad weather.

Frequently asked questions

When does Avoriaz open in 2026?

On 11 December 2026, until 18 April 2027, with a partial early opening in late November and early December depending on snow.

Can you drive to Avoriaz?

Yes, as far as the car parks at the resort entrance, but not into the streets: Avoriaz is car-free.

Which airport for Avoriaz?

Geneva, about 1 hour 30 minutes by road.
