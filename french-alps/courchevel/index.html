Courchevel: skiing and staying in the 3 Valleys

Perfectly groomed pistes, an altiport and grand hotels: Courchevel is the most prestigious resort in the Alps, and also an excellent ski area for every level.

By Anthony, Cannes · Updated 12 November 2026

Courchevel is made up of several villages at different heights: Saint-Bon, Le Praz, Courchevel Village, Moriond and Courchevel 1850, the best known. With La Tania they form the Courchevel ski area, linked to the 3 Valleys via Méribel.

The resort announces a season from 4 December 2026 to 18 April 2027, depending on snow. Its ski area, known for the quality of its grooming, suits beginners and experts alike.

Things to do in Courchevel

Courchevel 1850

The heart of the resort: luxury shops, Michelin-starred restaurants and the altiport, where small planes land between the slopes.

The Grand Couloir

One of the most famous black runs in the Alps, from La Saulire. For experts only and dependent on snow.

The Le Praz ski jumps

Built for the 1992 Albertville Olympics, they still host ski-jumping competitions.

Aquamotion

A large aquatic and wellness centre in Courchevel Moriond, perfect for a day off skis.

Moriond and La Tania

More family-friendly, more affordable villages with direct access to the same ski area.

How to get to Courchevel

By train

Train to Moûtiers - Salins - Brides-les-Bains, then a regional bus to the different Courchevel villages.

By plane

Chambéry, Lyon and Geneva airports, with shuttles and private transfers. The altiport also takes private flights.

Getting around

Free shuttles link the villages of the resort.

Tips for skiing in Courchevel

  • For a more reasonable budget, stay in Moriond, Le Praz or La Tania: you will ski the same area.
  • The runs to Méribel let you ski the 3 Valleys in a day: check that your pass covers it.
  • Book mountain restaurants ahead during the Christmas and February holidays.

Frequently asked questions

When does Courchevel open in 2026?

The resort announces an opening on 4 December 2026 and a closing on 18 April 2027, depending on snow.

Which Courchevel village should I choose?

1850 for prestige and altitude, Moriond for families, Le Praz and La Tania for charm and gentler prices.

How do you get to Courchevel by train?

To Moûtiers station, then by regional bus to the resort.
