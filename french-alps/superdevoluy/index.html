SuperDévoluy: the Dévoluy’s big family ski area

Two resorts, 100 kilometres of runs and limestone peaks: the Dévoluy is one of the big family ski areas of the Southern Alps.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 12 November 2026

The Dévoluy massif in the Hautes-Alpes has two linked resorts: SuperDévoluy, founded in 1966, ski-in ski-out, and La Joue du Loup, more of a chalet village. The ski area of about 100 kilometres lies between 1,500 and 2,500 metres.

For 2026-2027 it is open from 12 December 2026 to 11 April 2027. New on the SuperDévoluy side is the Jas gondola, which replaces an old chairlift and halves the ride time.

Things to do in the Dévoluy

SuperDévoluy

The ski-in ski-out resort, with a big snow front and an indoor sports centre.

La Joue du Loup

The chalet atmosphere, and O’dycéa, the Dévoluy spa, to relax.

The Jas gondola

New this winter, it gets you to the top of the area faster.

Snowshoeing and cross-country

Marked trails in a wild limestone massif.

How to get to the Dévoluy

From Gap

About 45 minutes by road.

From Marseille or Lyon

About 2 hours 30 minutes by road.

By train

To Gap, then a bus or taxi.

Tips for a ski trip to the Dévoluy

  • Pick SuperDévoluy for ski-in ski-out, La Joue du Loup for the chalet feel.
  • Book the O’dycéa spa ahead in French school holidays.
  • This is one of the more affordable areas in the Southern Alps: ideal for a first ski week.

Frequently asked questions

When does SuperDévoluy open in 2026?

On 12 December 2026, until 11 April 2027, depending on snow.

Are SuperDévoluy and La Joue du Loup linked?

Yes, the two resorts share the same ski area.

Is the Dévoluy good for families?

Yes, it holds the Famille Plus label and has plenty of easy runs.
