La Plagne: ten villages and a bobsleigh track

Ski-in ski-out villages, long easy blue runs and the only Olympic bobsleigh track in France. La Plagne is the classic family resort.

By Anthony, Cannes · Updated 12 November 2026

La Plagne brings together about ten villages, from Plagne Centre and Belle Plagne up high to Montchavin-Les Coches or Champagny lower down. Its ski area has about 225 kilometres of pistes between 1,250 and 3,250 metres, and joins Les Arcs via the Vanoise Express to form Paradiski.

For the 2026-2027 season, La Plagne opens on 12 December 2026 and closes at the end of April 2027, depending on snow. More than half of the runs are blue, which makes it a safe bet for families and intermediate skiers.

Things to do in La Plagne

The Olympic bobsleigh track

Built for the 1992 Albertville Games, it is the only Olympic track in France open to the public: bob-raft, piloted bobsleigh or fast luge, depending on age and nerve.

The Bellecôte glacier

The top of the area, above 3,000 metres, for more reliable snow and a view of Mont Blanc.

Belle Plagne

A car-free village of wood and stone, cosier than the big 1960s buildings.

Montchavin-Les Coches

On the forest side, an old Savoyard village next to the Vanoise Express, handy for skiing in Les Arcs.

Off the slopes

Snowshoeing, dog sledding, swimming pools and toboggan runs.

How to get to La Plagne

By train

To Aime-La Plagne station, then about 30 to 40 minutes by bus or taxi to the high villages. The bus station is right next to the train station.

By car

Via the A43 then the N90 to Aime, before the resort road.

From the airports

Geneva, Lyon or Chambéry, with transfers in season.

Tips for a ski trip to La Plagne

  • Book the bobsleigh several days ahead during school holidays: slots go fast.
  • Stay in a high village (Plagne Centre, Belle Plagne, Plagne Villages) for ski-in ski-out access.
  • In bad weather, stick to the tree-lined runs of Montchavin and Montalbert, which are more sheltered.

Frequently asked questions

When does La Plagne open in 2026?

On 12 December 2026, until the end of April 2027, depending on snow.

How old do you have to be to bobsleigh in La Plagne?

It depends on the option: the bob-raft is the most accessible, the others are for older riders. Check the minimum age and height when booking.

Which train station for La Plagne?

Aime-La Plagne station, then a bus or taxi up to the villages.
