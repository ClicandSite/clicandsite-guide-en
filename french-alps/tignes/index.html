Tignes: glacier skiing and reliable snow

Thanks to its glacier, Tignes is one of the first resorts in France to open: from 21 November 2026. A safe bet for early-season skiing.

Tignes is made up of several villages around a high-altitude lake: Tignes Le Lac, Val Claret, Le Lavachet, Tignes 1800 and Les Brévières, lower down in the forest. Above them, the Grande Motte glacier offers reliable snow from autumn.

For 2026-2027, Tignes announces a progressive opening from 21 November 2026, with the link to Val d’Isère from 28 November and a season running until 2 May 2027, depending on snow.

Things to do in Tignes

The Grande Motte glacier

An underground funicular climbs from Val Claret towards the glacier, with spectacular views of the Vanoise.

Lake Tignes

Frozen in winter, it hosts walks and ice activities in the middle of the resort.

The resort villages

Le Lac and Val Claret to be right on the slopes, Les Brévières for a village feel.

The link with Val d’Isère

The linked area offers days of skiing without repeating a run.

How to get to Tignes

By train

Train to Bourg-Saint-Maurice, then a bus or shuttle to the Tignes villages.

By plane

Chambéry, Geneva and Lyon airports, with shuttles and private transfers in season.

By car

Via Bourg-Saint-Maurice and the Tignes dam road. Winter equipment is compulsory.

Tips for skiing in Tignes

  • For skiing in November or April, Tignes is one of the best choices thanks to its glacier.
  • At this altitude the weather changes fast: bring goggles, warm layers and sunscreen.
  • Pick your village to suit you: Val Claret for skiing, Les Brévières for peace and quiet.

Frequently asked questions

When does Tignes open in 2026?

From 21 November 2026, with a progressive opening and the link to Val d’Isère from 28 November.

Can you ski on the glacier in Tignes?

Yes, the Grande Motte glacier offers high-altitude snow, reached by funicular from Val Claret.

How do you get to Tignes without a car?

By train to Bourg-Saint-Maurice, then by bus or shuttle to the resort.
