Montgenèvre: skiing between France and Italy

A village at 1,860 metres on a border pass, runs heading off into Italy and one of the earliest openings in the Southern Alps.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 12 November 2026

Montgenèvre sits on the pass of the same name at 1,860 metres, a few minutes from Italy. It is France’s oldest ski resort, now starting its 120th season. Its ski area of about 95 kilometres rises to 2,648 metres and continues on the Italian side into the Milky Way (Via Lattea), more than 400 kilometres of runs in all.

For 2026-2027 the ski area is open continuously from 28 November 2026 to 17 April 2027. The big news is the Chalvet sector, completely rebuilt with two new gondolas due for the Christmas holidays.

Things to do in Montgenèvre

The Milky Way

With the right pass you can ski to Claviere, Sestriere or Sauze d’Oulx on the Italian side.

Le Chalvet

The sunny side of the ski area, relaunched this season with two new gondolas.

Les Alberts

The hamlet below the resort, starting point for cross-country skiing in the forest.

The Italian touch

Montgenèvre celebrates its neighbour several times a season, starting with Sant’Ambrogio in early December.

How to get to Montgenèvre

By train

To Briançon (night train from Paris or regional train), then about 20 minutes by bus. On the Italian side, Oulx station is about 20 minutes away.

By car

Via Grenoble and the Lautaret pass, or via Gap and Briançon.

Getting around

Free shuttles run around the resort in winter.

Tips for a ski trip to Montgenèvre

  • Take advantage of cheaper passes early in the season, until mid-December.
  • Spend a Milky Way day and have lunch in Italy.
  • Carry ID if you cross the border on skis.

Frequently asked questions

When does Montgenèvre open in 2026?

On 28 November 2026, until 17 April 2027, depending on snow.

Can you ski to Italy from Montgenèvre?

Yes, the area is linked to the Italian Milky Way with the right pass.

Which train station for Montgenèvre?

Briançon in France or Oulx in Italy, then a bus or taxi.
