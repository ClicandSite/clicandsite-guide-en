Megève: a charming village and skiing facing Mont Blanc

A real Savoyard village, horse-drawn carriages in the streets and tree-lined slopes facing Mont Blanc. Megève is the classic chic, gentle ski resort.

Megève grew up around its medieval village, with its church and pedestrian streets. Its ski area is part of Évasion Mont-Blanc, which links Megève, Saint-Gervais, Combloux and other villages across more than 400 kilometres of pistes.

For 2026-2027, Megève plans a partial early opening in early December, then a full opening on 19 December 2026 until early April 2027, depending on snow. Its mid-altitude, often tree-lined runs make it pleasant even in bad weather.

Things to do in Megève

The village

Church square, pedestrian streets, shops and horse-drawn carriages: one of the prettiest resort villages in the Alps, especially when lit up in December.

Mont d’Arbois

The sector linked to Saint-Gervais, with long easy runs and a superb view of Mont Blanc.

Rochebrune

The area right above the village, reached by cable car, handy for half a day.

Le Jaillet and Combloux

Across the valley, sunny slopes facing the Mont Blanc range.

Off the slopes

Carriage rides, an ice rink, snowshoe walks and dog sledding.

How to get to Megève

By train

To Sallanches - Combloux - Megève station, then about thirty minutes by bus or taxi to the village.

From Geneva airport

About 1 hour 15 minutes by road, with shuttles and private transfers in season.

Getting around

Free shuttles serve the different lift departures.

Tips for a ski trip to Megève

  • Go up Mont d’Arbois on a clear day: that is where the view of Mont Blanc is most spectacular.
  • Enjoy the village at the end of the day, when the lights come on.
  • During the February holidays, book children’s ski lessons several weeks ahead.

Frequently asked questions

When does Megève open in 2026?

A partial early opening in early December, then a full opening on 19 December 2026, depending on snow.

Is Megève good for beginners?

Yes, the area has many blue and green runs, often sheltered by forest.

What is the nearest train station to Megève?

Sallanches - Combloux - Megève station, about thirty minutes from the village by bus or taxi.
