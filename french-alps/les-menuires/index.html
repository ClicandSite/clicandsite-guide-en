Les Menuires: the 3 Valleys at friendlier prices

The largest ski area in the world, accommodation right on the slopes and prices more reasonable than in Courchevel or Méribel.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 12 November 2026

Les Menuires is a resort in the Belleville valley, between Saint-Martin-de-Belleville and Val Thorens, in the heart of the 3 Valleys, one of the largest ski areas in the world with about 600 kilometres of pistes. Built in the 1960s, it focuses on ski-in ski-out neighbourhoods and prices more affordable than its neighbours.

For the 2026-2027 season, Les Menuires is open from 5 December 2026 to 18 April 2027, depending on snow.

Things to do in Les Menuires

The 3 Valleys

From Les Menuires you can ski to Val Thorens, Méribel and Courchevel: ideal for a different sector every day.

Pointe de la Masse

The top of the resort, above 2,800 metres, with views of Mont Blanc and the Vanoise peaks.

Ski-in ski-out neighbourhoods

Reberty, Les Bruyères or La Croisette: put your skis on as you leave your apartment.

Saint-Martin-de-Belleville

A real Savoyard village lower down, linked to the ski area, for a more authentic dinner.

How to get to Les Menuires

By train

To Moûtiers station, served by TGV trains in season, then about 45 minutes by bus or taxi.

From the airports

Geneva, Lyon or Chambéry, with transfers in season.

Getting around

Free shuttles link the resort’s neighbourhoods.

Practical info 2026: Les Menuires

  • Season: 5 December 2026 to 18 April 2027, with the 3 Vallées links open throughout.
  • Les Menuires–Saint-Martin-de-Belleville pass: €70 per adult day and €350 for 6 days; €63 and €315 from 5 to 18 December 2026 and from 10 to 18 April 2027.
  • 3 Vallées pass: €84.20 per adult day (18-74) and €421 for 6 days; €75.70 and €378.50 in the early and late season. €69 for ages 5-17, €21 from 75, free under 5.
  • On Saturdays: €41 for a Menuires–Saint-Martin day or €67.30 for a 3 Vallées day, online only, before midnight on Friday.
  • From Moûtiers station: coaches (Cars Région Savoie, Altibus) for about €12 per adult; a taxi costs about €85 for 1 to 4 people. In the resort, the shuttles between districts are free.

Prices and dates checked on 13 November 2026 on the official Les Menuires and 3 Vallées websites; they can change, so check before you go.

Tips for a ski trip to Les Menuires

  • Compare the Belleville valley pass and the 3 Valleys pass: the first is often enough for a week.
  • Stay in a ski-in ski-out neighbourhood to avoid shuttles with children.
  • Head over to Val Thorens on a clear day: it is only a few lifts away.

Frequently asked questions

When does Les Menuires open in 2026?

On 5 December 2026, until 18 April 2027, depending on snow.

Is Les Menuires part of the 3 Valleys?

Yes, the resort is linked to Val Thorens, Méribel and Courchevel.

Which train station for Les Menuires?

Moûtiers station, then a bus or taxi.

How much is a Les Menuires ski pass in 2026-2027?

The Les Menuires–Saint-Martin-de-Belleville pass costs €70 per adult day and €350 for 6 days (€63 and €315 in the early and late season). For the whole 3 Vallées area, allow €84.20 a day and €421 for 6 days.
