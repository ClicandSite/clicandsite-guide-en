Val Thorens: Europe’s highest ski resort

At 2,300 metres, Val Thorens is the highest ski resort in Europe. It opens first and closes last: from late November to early May.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 12 November 2026

Val Thorens sits at the head of the Belleville valley, at the heart of the 3 Valleys, the world’s largest linked ski area, together with Les Menuires, Méribel and Courchevel. Its altitude gives reliable snow from the start of the season to the end.

For winter 2026-2027 the resort plans to open on 21 November 2026 and close in early May 2027, depending on snow. The resort is almost entirely car-free: you can ski from the door of most accommodation.

Things to do in Val Thorens

Cime Caron

A cable car rises to 3,200 metres for one of the biggest panoramas in the Alps, from Mont Blanc to the Vanoise. The run down is for strong skiers.

The 3 Valleys

With a 3 Valleys pass you can ski to Les Menuires, Méribel and Courchevel in a day.

Orelle

On the Maurienne side, the Orelle sector is linked to Val Thorens, with sunny and often quieter slopes.

Après-ski

Val Thorens is known for its party atmosphere, from mountain terraces to bars in the resort.

How to get to Val Thorens

By train

Train to Moûtiers - Salins - Brides-les-Bains station, then a regional bus to the resort, about an hour’s drive up.

By plane

The nearest airports are Chambéry, Lyon and Geneva, with shuttles and private transfers in season.

By car

Through the Tarentaise valley then the Belleville road. Winter equipment is compulsory; the resort is car-free, so book parking.

Practical info 2026: Val Thorens

  • Season: the Val Thorens-Orelle area is open from 21 November 2026 to 2 May 2027; links to the rest of the 3 Valleys run from 5 December 2026 to 18 April 2027.
  • Val Thorens-Orelle lift pass: €66.10 per adult day (18-74) from 21 November to 18 December 2026, then €73.50 from 19 December to 2 May 2027; €54.20 then €60.20 for ages 5-17.
  • 3 Valleys lift pass: €84.20 per day for adults (18-74), €69 for ages 5-17 and €21 from age 75, from 19 December 2026 to 9 April 2027; €75.70 per adult day from 5 to 18 December and from 10 to 18 April 2027.
  • Saturdays: 20% off the adult 3 Valleys day pass, online purchase only.

Prices and dates checked on 12 November 2026 on the official resort and 3 Valleys websites; they can change, so check before you go.

Tips for skiing in Val Thorens

  • Early and late in the season, it is one of the few resorts where the whole area is open: ideal for a trip in November or April.
  • At 2,300 metres, cold and wind can be fierce: bring goggles, a balaclava and sunscreen.
  • Buy your lift pass online before arriving to skip the Saturday queue at the ticket office.

Frequently asked questions

When does Val Thorens open in 2026?

The resort plans to open on Saturday 21 November 2026, depending on snow, for a season running until early May 2027.

Why is Val Thorens the highest resort in Europe?

The village is built at 2,300 metres, and its lifts reach 3,200 metres at Cime Caron.

How do you get to Val Thorens without a car?

By train to Moûtiers, then by regional bus to the resort.

How much is a lift pass in Val Thorens in 2026-2027?

An adult day pass for Val Thorens-Orelle costs €66.10 until 18 December 2026, then €73.50. The 3 Valleys pass costs €84.20 per adult day from 19 December 2026 to 9 April 2027.
