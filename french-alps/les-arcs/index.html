Les Arcs: from the funicular to the Aiguille Rouge

A high-speed train station at the foot of the slopes, a funicular and a ski area rising to 3,226 metres. Les Arcs is big-mountain skiing without a car.

Les Arcs is made up of several high-altitude villages, Arc 1600, Arc 1800, Arc 1950 and Arc 2000, above Bourg-Saint-Maurice. Together with Peisey-Vallandry the area has about 200 kilometres of pistes, and the Vanoise Express cable car links it to La Plagne to form Paradiski, about 425 kilometres.

For the 2026-2027 season, Les Arcs is open from 12 December 2026 to 24 April 2027, depending on snow. The Vanoise Express stops on 17 April, so the last week can only be skied on the Les Arcs side.

Things to do in Les Arcs

The Aiguille Rouge

The top of the area at 3,226 metres, with a view of Mont Blanc and the Vanoise. The run down to Villaroger is one of the longest in the Alps, with more than 2,000 metres of vertical.

Arc 1950

A small car-free village of stone and timber at the foot of the slopes: the resort’s postcard view.

Arc 1800

The largest village, ideal for families, with plenty of blue runs and views of Mont Blanc.

Paradiski and the Vanoise Express

The double-decker cable car crosses the valley in a few minutes to reach La Plagne: worth at least one day with a Paradiski pass.

Off the slopes

Tobogganing, snowshoeing, swimming pools and walks between the villages.

How to get to Les Arcs

By train

To Bourg-Saint-Maurice station, served by direct TGV trains from Paris in season, then the funicular up to Arc 1600 in about seven minutes.

From the airports

Geneva, Lyon or Chambéry, with shuttles and private transfers in season; allow about 2 hours 30 minutes from Geneva.

Getting around

Free shuttles link the different villages.

Tips for a ski trip to Les Arcs

  • Come by train: the station is right next to the funicular, and you will not need a car in the resort.
  • Go up the Aiguille Rouge in the morning on a clear day, before clouds build around the summit.
  • Only buy the Paradiski pass if you really plan to ski La Plagne: Les Arcs alone is plenty for a week.

Frequently asked questions

When does Les Arcs open in 2026?

On 12 December 2026, until 24 April 2027, depending on snow.

Can you get to Les Arcs by train?

Yes: TGV to Bourg-Saint-Maurice, then the funicular up to Arc 1600.

What is the difference between Les Arcs and Paradiski?

Les Arcs and Peisey-Vallandry form an area of about 200 km; Paradiski adds La Plagne, for about 425 km.
