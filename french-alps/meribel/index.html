Méribel: at the heart of the 3 Valleys

At the centre of the 3 Valleys, Méribel lets you reach Courchevel or Val Thorens in a day, from a village built entirely of chalets.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 12 November 2026

Méribel was founded in the late 1930s with a rule still followed today: chalets in wood and stone, in the Savoyard style. The result is one of the most harmonious resorts in the Alps, in a wooded valley.

Its central position makes it the best base for exploring the 3 Valleys. For the 2026-2027 season, Méribel plans to open on 5 December 2026, depending on snow.

Things to do in Méribel

The 3 Valleys

From Méribel you can reach Courchevel on one side and Les Menuires and Val Thorens on the other.

Roc de Fer

The run used for the 2023 Alpine World Ski Championships, held with Courchevel.

The Olympic Park

An ice rink and aquatic centre, a legacy of the 1992 Albertville Olympics.

Méribel-Mottaret

The resort’s highest village, ski-in ski-out, with lifts heading towards Val Thorens.

How to get to Méribel

By train

Train to Moûtiers - Salins - Brides-les-Bains, then a regional bus to Méribel.

By plane

Chambéry, Geneva and Lyon airports, with shuttles and private transfers in season.

Getting around

Free shuttles run between Méribel, Mottaret and the valley villages.

Practical info 2026: Méribel

  • Season: the 3 Valleys ski area, which Méribel is part of, is open from 5 December 2026 to 18 April 2027.
  • 3 Valleys lift pass: €84.20 per day for adults (18-74), €69 for ages 5-17 and €21 from age 75, from 19 December 2026 to 9 April 2027; €75.70 per adult day from 5 to 18 December and from 10 to 18 April 2027.
  • 6-day 3 Valleys pass: €421 for adults and €345 for children in high season.
  • Saturdays: 20% off the adult 3 Valleys day pass, online purchase only.

Prices and dates checked on 12 November 2026 on the official 3 Valleys and Val Thorens websites; they can change, so check before you go.

Tips for skiing in Méribel

  • Stay in Mottaret to maximise ski time, or in Méribel centre for village atmosphere.
  • Check that your pass covers the 3 Valleys if you want to reach Val Thorens or Courchevel.
  • The home runs get busy at the end of the day: set off a little earlier.

Frequently asked questions

When does Méribel open in 2026?

The resort plans to open on 5 December 2026, depending on snow.

Is Méribel part of the 3 Valleys?

Yes, it sits at the centre of the area, between Courchevel and Les Menuires - Val Thorens.

How do you get to Méribel by train?

To Moûtiers station, then by regional bus to the resort.

How much is a 3 Valleys lift pass in 2026-2027?

€84.20 per adult day from 19 December 2026 to 9 April 2027, €75.70 early and late in the season, and €421 for 6 days in high season.
