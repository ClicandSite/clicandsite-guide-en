Auron: the village resort of the Alpes-Maritimes

A village of chalets on a sunny plateau, a big ski area and the Mediterranean less than two hours away. Auron turns 90 this winter.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 12 November 2026

Auron is a village resort at 1,600 metres above Saint-Étienne-de-Tinée, at the gates of the Mercantour. Its ski area has about 135 kilometres of runs, served by 19 lifts including two cable cars.

For 2026-2027 the ski area will open on 5 December 2026, depending on snow, until mid-April 2027. The resort celebrates its 90th anniversary on Saturday 30 January 2027.

Things to do in Auron

The ski area

Varied runs between larch forests and open slopes, with fine views of the Mercantour.

The village

Chalets, a small church, an ice rink: Auron has kept a village feel and holds the Famille Plus label.

Auron’s 90th birthday

A big celebration planned for Saturday 30 January 2027.

Saint-Étienne-de-Tinée

The old valley village below, with its painted chapels.

How to get to Auron

From Nice

Under 1 hour 30 minutes by road up the Tinée valley. In season, a bus links Nice to Auron.

From Cannes

Allow about 2 hours by road.

Good to know

The Bonette and Lombarde passes are closed in winter: the only way up is through the Tinée valley.

Tips for a ski trip to Auron

  • Plan a weekend around 30 January 2027 for the resort’s 90th birthday.
  • Combine with Isola 2000, less than an hour’s drive away, for a change of ski area.
  • Fit winter tyres or carry snow chains before leaving the coast.
