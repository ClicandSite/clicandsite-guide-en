Isola 2000: snow 90 minutes from the sea

Isola 2000 is a high-altitude resort in the Alpes-Maritimes, at the gates of the Mercantour National Park, about 90 kilometres from Nice. Its ski area of about 120 kilometres spreads across three sectors between 1,800 and 2,610 metres, with reliable snow thanks to the altitude and more than 400 snow cannons.

For 2026-2027, the tourist office announces the ski area open from 3 December 2026 to 19 April 2027, depending on snow.

Things to do in Isola 2000

The three sectors

Pélevos, Saint-Sauveur and the Lombarde, on the Italian border side, home to the black runs.

Ski-in ski-out

The whole resort sits at the foot of the slopes: put your skis on as you step outside.

The snowpark

A park for all levels, popular with young Riviera riders.

The closing party

The end-of-season party on 17 and 18 April 2027, at the foot of the slopes.

How to get to Isola 2000

From Nice

About 1 hour 30 minutes by road up the Tinée valley. Regular buses link Nice to the resorts in season.

From Cannes

Allow about 2 hours by road.

Good to know

The Lombarde pass to Italy is closed in winter: the only way up is through the Tinée valley.

Tips for a ski trip to Isola 2000

  • Leave the coast early at weekends: the Tinée road fills up fast on sunny Saturdays.
  • Fit winter tyres or carry snow chains, which are compulsory in the mountains in winter.
  • In April, enjoy spring skiing and end-of-season discounts.
