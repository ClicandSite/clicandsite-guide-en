Personal data and cookies

Data controller: iDream Lab, 34 rue Borniol, 06400 Cannes, France. For any question or request: contact@clicandsite.fr.

Audience measurement. This site sets no analytics or advertising cookies. It counts visits anonymously: page viewed, click to a partner, device type, referring site, and the approximate country and city, worked out from the IP address at the time of the visit. The IP address is neither recorded nor kept, and none of this information can identify you. This counting relies on the publisher’s legitimate interest in knowing how the site is used. The data is hosted by Supabase, on Amazon Web Services infrastructure in Europe; location is provided by DB-IP.

GetYourGuide module. On some pages, a live list of activities is provided by GetYourGuide. This module stores a session identifier and Clic&Site’s partner ID in your browser, used to attribute any booking. It only loads after you agree, by clicking “See available activities”. Your choice is kept in your browser, and you can withdraw it at any time with the button below. Plain links to the booking platforms work without this module.

Advertising. The site is enrolled in the Google AdSense programme (Google Ireland Limited). No ads are shown at the moment and no advertising cookies are set. If ads are shown in the future, they will only load after your consent, collected through a Google-certified consent banner, and this page will be updated. To learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com.

Emails. If you write to us, your message and address are used only to reply and are kept only as long as that exchange requires.

Your rights. You have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict and object to the processing of your data, by writing to the address above. You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (cnil.fr).
