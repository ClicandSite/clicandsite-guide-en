Visiting the Sainte-Chapelle

Fifteen windows, over a thousand stained-glass scenes and hardly any wall. On a sunny day, the upper chapel is one of the most beautiful interiors in the world.

Saint Louis built the Sainte-Chapelle in the 13th century to house the Crown of Thorns. Today it stands in the middle of the Palais de Justice on the Île de la Cité, which is why there is an airport-style security check at the entrance.

Booking a time slot is compulsory, even for visitors who get in free. Your slot is valid for half an hour. A combined ticket also covers the neighbouring Conciergerie, the former palace that became Marie-Antoinette’s prison.

Things to do at the Sainte-Chapelle

The upper chapel

The heart of the visit: fifteen huge windows telling the Bible from Genesis to the Passion. The later west rose shows the Apocalypse.

The lower chapel

Once used by the palace staff, with its blue vault scattered with fleurs-de-lis.

The Conciergerie

Next door, the great Gothic Hall of the Men-at-Arms and Marie-Antoinette’s reconstructed cell.

How to get to the Sainte-Chapelle

By metro and RER

Cité (line 4), two minutes away, or Saint-Michel - Notre-Dame (RER B and C), across the bridge.

Which entrance?

Visitors go in on Boulevard du Palais, through the Palais de Justice security check. Large suitcases are refused.

Tips for visiting the Sainte-Chapelle

  • Pick a sunny day if you can: the windows change completely with the light.
  • Arrive about twenty minutes before your slot to get through security without stress.
  • Classical concerts are held in the upper chapel in the evening: another way to see it.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to book the Sainte-Chapelle?

Yes, a time slot is compulsory for everyone, including free entry and Paris Museum Pass holders.

How long does the visit take?

Allow 45 minutes to an hour, plus the security check, and an extra hour with the Conciergerie.

Is the Sainte-Chapelle free on the first Sunday of the month?

Yes, from November to March, but you still have to book a slot.
