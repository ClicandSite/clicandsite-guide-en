Christmas in Paris: lights, markets and ice rinks

The Champs-Élysées lights go on on 22 November 2026, and its Christmas market returns after ten years. Here is where to find the magic of Christmas in Paris.

By Anthony, Cannes · Updated 12 November 2026

From late November to early January, Paris dresses up for the holidays: illuminated avenues, animated department store windows, chalet markets and ice rinks. It is a great time to visit, as long as you wrap up warm.

In 2026, the Champs-Élysées lights are switched on on Sunday 22 November and shine every evening until early January. The same day, the Champs-Élysées Christmas market makes its comeback, running until 4 January 2027.

Things to do at Christmas in Paris

The Champs-Élysées lights

Hundreds of illuminated trees from Concorde to the Arc de Triomphe. The best view is from the top of the Arc de Triomphe.

The Champs-Élysées Christmas market

Back after ten years, its chalets line the avenue between Place de la Concorde and the Rond-Point, from 22 November 2026 to 4 January 2027.

Department store windows

On Boulevard Haussmann, the animated windows of Galeries Lafayette and Printemps, and the giant tree under the Galeries Lafayette dome.

The Grand Palais ice rink

In December, a huge ice rink fills the glass-roofed nave of the Grand Palais. Book your slot ahead.

La Défense Christmas market

One of the largest in the region, open from 12 November to 31 December 2026.

A Seine cruise

To see the monuments lit up while staying warm in a glazed boat.

How to get to the Christmas sights

Champs-Élysées

Metro Concorde (lines 1, 8, 12), Champs-Élysées - Clemenceau (lines 1 and 13) or Charles de Gaulle - Étoile.

Department stores

Metro Chaussée d’Antin - La Fayette (lines 7 and 9) or Havre - Caumartin, RER A at Auber.

Practical info 2026: Christmas in Paris

  • Tuileries Christmas market: 14 November 2026 to 3 January 2027, daily 11am to 11:30pm.
  • Notre-Dame market: square René-Viviani, 26 November to 27 December 2026. La Défense: 12 November to 31 December 2026.
  • Champs-Élysées: lights from 22 November 2026, and the Christmas market is back on the avenue.
  • Department store windows: BHV 6 November, Printemps 7 November, Bon Marché and Samaritaine 8 November, Galeries Lafayette 14 November.

Prices and opening hours checked in November 2026 with the official sites and the Paris tourist office; they can change, so check before you go.

Tips for Christmas in Paris

  • Go on a weekday rather than at the weekend: markets and windows are packed on Saturdays.
  • Night falls around 5pm in December: visit museums in the afternoon and the lights afterwards.
  • Book ice rinks, dinner cruises and New Year’s Eve restaurants in advance.

Frequently asked questions

When are the Champs-Élysées Christmas lights switched on in 2026?

On Sunday 22 November 2026, in the early evening. They stay on every evening until early January.

Is there a Christmas market on the Champs-Élysées in 2026?

Yes, it returns after ten years, from 22 November 2026 to 4 January 2027, between Concorde and the Rond-Point.

Where can you ice skate in Paris at Christmas?

Under the glass roof of the Grand Palais in December, as well as on several rinks around the city and at La Défense.
