The Louis Vuitton Foundation and its glass sails

On the edge of the Bois de Boulogne, Frank Gehry’s glass ship hosts the biggest modern art exhibitions in Paris.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 12 November 2026

Opened in 2014 next to the Jardin d’Acclimatation, the Fondation Louis Vuitton is architecture first: twelve glass sails designed by Frank Gehry, set over a pool. Its terraces look out over the Bois de Boulogne and as far as La Défense.

Until 8 March 2027, the exhibition “Gustave Fayet, collector-creator” fills every gallery. It revisits this collector, one of the first buyers of Van Gogh, Gauguin and Odilon Redon, and includes Van Gogh’s Self-Portrait with Bandaged Ear and Pipe.

What to see at the Louis Vuitton Foundation

The Gustave Fayet exhibition

Several hundred works across all the galleries, from Van Gogh to Redon. Book tickets or a guided tour at the Louis Vuitton Foundation.

Frank Gehry’s building

The glass sails, the cascade and the play of reflections are as striking as the art.

The terraces

Several open-air terraces with views over the Bois de Boulogne and Paris.

The Jardin d’Acclimatation

Your ticket also covers the neighbouring park, great with children.

How to get to the Louis Vuitton Foundation

By metro

Line 1 to Les Sablons, then a 10 to 15-minute walk.

By shuttle

A paid shuttle runs from Place Charles-de-Gaulle – Étoile, near the Arc de Triomphe.

Address

8 Avenue du Mahatma-Gandhi, Bois de Boulogne (16th arrondissement).

Practical info 2026: The Louis Vuitton Foundation

  • Exhibition: “Gustave Fayet, collector-creator”, 9 October 2026 to 8 March 2027, including Van Gogh’s Self-Portrait with Bandaged Ear and Pipe.
  • Prices: €18 full price, €10 students, €5 under-18s; the ticket also covers the Jardin d’Acclimatation. Closed on Tuesdays.
  • Shuttle: from 44 avenue de Friedland (exit 2 of Charles-de-Gaulle–Étoile métro), about every 20 minutes. Otherwise métro Les Sablons (line 1).

Tips for visiting the Louis Vuitton Foundation

  • Book a timed slot online: big exhibitions often sell out at weekends.
  • The Foundation is closed on Tuesdays.
  • On Fridays it stays open until 9pm, which is often quieter.
  • Save time for the terraces, especially on a sunny day.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a ticket to the Louis Vuitton Foundation?

A full-price ticket costs €18 in 2026, €10 reduced (under 26, students) and €5 for under-18s. It includes the Jardin d’Acclimatation.

What are the opening hours?

It is open Monday, Wednesday and Thursday from 11am to 8pm, Friday from 11am to 9pm and at weekends from 10am to 8pm. It is closed on Tuesdays.

How long does the Gustave Fayet exhibition run?

“Gustave Fayet, collector-creator” runs from 9 October 2026 to 8 March 2027.
