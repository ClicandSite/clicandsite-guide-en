The Picasso Museum, in a Marais mansion

The world’s largest collection of works by Picasso, shown in the Hôtel Salé, a 17th-century mansion in the heart of the Marais.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 12 November 2026

The Musée national Picasso-Paris holds more than 5,000 works by the artist and tens of thousands of archive pieces. They are displayed in the Hôtel Salé, a 17th-century mansion on Rue de Thorigny, which reopened after renovation in 2014.

Paintings, sculptures, ceramics and drawings trace Picasso’s whole career. From 6 October 2026 to 7 February 2027, the museum also shows an exhibition on the avant-garde artist Kurt Schwitters.

What to see at the Picasso Museum

The permanent collection

Every period of Picasso’s work, from the Blue Period to Cubism and his last years. Book tickets or a guided tour at the Picasso Museum

The Hôtel Salé

The carved grand staircase and 17th-century salons are worth a look in themselves.

The Kurt Schwitters exhibition

“Le chaos-monde 1918–1944”, until 7 February 2027.

The rooftop café

A break above the rooftops of the Marais.

How to get to the Picasso Museum

By metro

Line 1 to Saint-Paul; or line 8 to Chemin Vert or Saint-Sébastien – Froissart.

On foot

Ten minutes from Place des Vosges through the Marais.

Address

5 Rue de Thorigny, in the 3rd arrondissement.

Practical info 2026: The Picasso Museum

  • Price: €16; free for under-18s and EU residents under 26, and for everyone on the first Sunday of the month.
  • Opening hours: Tuesday to Sunday, 9:30am–6pm (last entry 5:15pm); closed on Mondays, 1 January, 1 May and 25 December.
  • Address: Hôtel Salé, 5 rue de Thorigny, in the Marais.

Prices and opening hours checked in November 2026 with the official sites and the Paris tourist office; they can change, so check before you go.

Tips for visiting the Picasso Museum

  • The museum is closed on Mondays.
  • On the first Sunday of the month, entry is free for everyone.
  • Book a time slot online to skip the queue.
  • Combine your visit with a walk around the Marais and Place des Vosges.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a ticket to the Picasso Museum?

A full-price ticket costs €16 in 2026, €12 reduced. Entry is free for under-18s, EU residents under 26 and for everyone on the first Sunday of the month.

What are the opening hours?

The museum is open from 9.30am to 6pm every day except Mondays, 1 January, 1 May and 25 December.

How long does a visit take?

Allow about two hours for the collection and the temporary exhibition.

How long should you spend at the Picasso Museum?

Allow 2 hours.
