Visiting the Musée d’Orsay

Monet, Renoir, Van Gogh, Degas: the world’s largest Impressionist collection, in a former railway station. Thursday’s late opening is the best time to go.

Photo: Yomex Owo / Unsplash

Housed in the Gare d’Orsay, built for the 1900 World’s Fair, the museum covers art from 1848 to 1914. The great glass-roofed nave holds the sculpture; the Impressionists are on the 5th floor, under the roof.

The museum is closed on Mondays and stays open late on Thursday evenings. Booking a slot online is strongly recommended. The entrance areas are being renovated until 2028, without closing the collections.

Things to do at the Musée d’Orsay

The Impressionist gallery

On the 5th floor: Monet, Renoir, Pissarro, Sisley, Degas, Caillebotte. The heart of the museum.

Van Gogh and Gauguin

The Post-Impressionist rooms, with Starry Night Over the Rhône and Van Gogh’s self-portrait.

The clocks

The two great station clocks: from inside, you see Paris and the Sacré-Cœur through the clock face.

The nave

The view down the glass-roofed hall, with sculpture by Carpeaux and Rodin.

The restaurant

The former dining room of the station hotel, all gilding and chandeliers.

How to get to the Musée d’Orsay

By RER and metro

Musée d’Orsay station (RER C), right outside, or Solférino (line 12), five minutes away.

On foot

From the Louvre, cross the Léopold-Sédar-Senghor footbridge: ten minutes.

Tips for visiting the Musée d’Orsay

  • Go on Thursday evening: fewer people and a reduced price later in the day.
  • Start on the 5th floor before the Impressionist rooms fill up.
  • Carry on to the Musée de l’Orangerie and Monet’s Water Lilies across the Seine: a joint ticket is available.

Frequently asked questions

What day is the Musée d’Orsay closed?

Mondays, plus 1 May and 25 December.

How late is Orsay open on Thursdays?

On Thursdays the museum stays open into the evening, with last entry about 45 minutes before closing.

Do I need to book the Musée d’Orsay?

It is not compulsory but strongly recommended: tickets bought on the spot do not guarantee entry on busy days.
