Taking a Seine river cruise

An hour on the water and Paris glides by: Notre-Dame, the Louvre, Orsay, the Eiffel Tower. It is the best way to get your bearings on day one.

Most of the monuments of Paris line the Seine between the Île Saint-Louis and the Eiffel Tower. A sightseeing cruise of about an hour shows you all of them from your seat, with commentary in several languages.

Several companies sail from the centre: Bateaux-Mouches from Pont de l’Alma, Bateaux Parisiens and Vedettes de Paris from the foot of the Eiffel Tower, Vedettes du Pont-Neuf from the Île de la Cité. The Batobus works like a bus on the water, with a day ticket.

A bit of history

For centuries the Seine was Paris’s main trade route: timber, wine and stone arrived by boat, and the city’s coat of arms is even a ship. The first sightseeing bateaux-mouches were launched in 1949 by Jean Bruel; the name comes from the Mouche boatyards in Lyon, where steamboats were built in the 19th century.

Since 1991 the banks of the Seine, from Pont de Sully to Pont d’Iéna, have been a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Things to do on the Seine

The sightseeing cruise

About an hour, a loop between the Eiffel Tower and the islands. No need to book far ahead outside the high season. Book tickets or a guided tour on the Seine

The sunset cruise

Golden light on the façades, then the monuments lighting up: the best time slot.

The dinner cruise

For a special evening, with a menu on board. Book ahead, especially at weekends.

The Batobus

About ten stops, hop on and off as you like: handy for getting between museums without the metro.

How to get to the boarding points

Pont de l’Alma

Alma - Marceau metro (line 9) or Pont de l’Alma on RER C for the Bateaux-Mouches.

Eiffel Tower

Bir-Hakeim metro (line 6) or Champ de Mars - Tour Eiffel on RER C for the piers at Port de la Bourdonnais.

Pont-Neuf

Pont-Neuf metro (line 7) or Cité (line 4) for Square du Vert-Galant.

Practical info 2026: Your Seine cruise

  • Bateaux-Mouches: boarding at Port de la Conférence, at the foot of the Pont de l’Alma (right bank); sightseeing cruise of about 1h10.
  • Fares: adults €20 at the desk, €17 online; ages 4-12 €10 at the desk, €8 online; free under 4. Free parking on the quay during the cruise.
  • Getting there: métro Alma-Marceau (line 9) or Franklin-D.-Roosevelt (line 1), RER C Pont de l’Alma.

Prices and opening hours checked in November 2026 with the official sites and the Paris tourist office; they can change, so check before you go.

Tips for your Seine cruise

  • Do it on your first day: you will spot the monuments to visit afterwards.
  • Sit outside on the top deck and bring a jacket: it is always cooler on the water.
  • In winter, when the Seine floods, boats can be suspended: check the day before.

Frequently asked questions

Which Seine cruise company should I choose?

The big companies offer very similar routes. Choose the boarding point that suits you best: Eiffel Tower, Pont de l’Alma or Pont-Neuf.

How long is a Seine cruise?

About an hour for a sightseeing cruise, around two and a half hours for a dinner cruise.

Do the boats run in winter?

Yes, all year round, except when the Seine floods. The boats are heated and glazed.

What is there to see near the Seine?

In this guide, the closest places are Crazy Horse (about 5 min on foot), Eiffel Tower (about 10 min on foot) and Trocadéro (about 15 min on foot).
