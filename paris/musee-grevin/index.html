The Grévin Wax Museum and its wax stars

More than 200 wax celebrities, past and present, in a 19th-century theatrical setting on the Grands Boulevards.

By Anthony, Cannes · Updated 12 November 2026

Opened in 1882 on Boulevard Montmartre, the Musée Grévin stages more than 200 wax figures: actors, singers, athletes and historical figures. It is one of the favourite family outings in Paris.

The visit opens with a light show, then passes through the Palace of Mirages, a hall of mirrors from 1900, and the museum’s Italian-style theatre before the rooms on film, music and history.

What to see at the Grévin

The wax figures

French and international stars you can pose next to for photos.

The Palace of Mirages

A hall of mirrors and lights from the 1900 World’s Fair.

The theatre

A small gilded Italian-style theatre, a listed historic monument.

Historical scenes

Great figures from French history brought to life.

How to get to the Grévin

By metro

Lines 8 and 9 to Grands Boulevards, right outside the entrance.

On foot

Ten minutes from the Palais Garnier and Galeries Lafayette.

Address

10 Boulevard Montmartre, in the 9th arrondissement.

Practical info 2026: The Grévin

  • Prices: dated online ticket from €19 for adults and €16.50 for children (5-18); much more at the desk; free under 5.
  • Opening hours: open daily, 10am–6pm on weekdays, 9:30am–7pm at weekends, on public holidays and during school holidays.
  • New: the One Piece crew joined the museum in June 2026. Address: 10 boulevard Montmartre.

Prices and opening hours checked in November 2026 with the official sites and the Paris tourist office; they can change, so check before you go.

Tips for visiting the Grévin

  • Buy a dated ticket online: it is much cheaper than at the desk.
  • Come at opening time on a weekday to avoid holiday crowds.
  • Allow about 1.5 hours.
  • Leave through the Passage Jouffroy, a pretty covered arcade next door.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a ticket to the Grévin?

A dated ticket bought online costs about €19 for an adult, compared with about €28 at the desk.

What are the opening hours?

On weekdays it is open from 10am to 6pm; at weekends and during French school holidays, from 9.30am to 7pm.

Is the Grévin good for children?

Yes, it is a very family-friendly visit: kids love taking photos with their heroes.
