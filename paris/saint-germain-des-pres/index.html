Born around an abbey founded in the 6th century, Saint-Germain-des-Prés became the post-war home of the existentialists, jazz cellars and publishers. Sartre and Beauvoir wrote at Café de Flore, a few steps from Les Deux Magots.

Today people come to wander between the galleries of Rue de Seine, the boutiques and the pastry shops, before heading on to Saint-Sulpice or the Luxembourg Gardens. The walk is free, and food tours are one of the best ways to discover the area.

A bit of history

The district grew up around an abbey founded in the 6th century by King Childebert I. Its church, Saint-Germain-des-Prés, with its Romanesque bell tower, is the oldest in Paris.

After the Liberation it became the capital of existentialism: Sartre and Simone de Beauvoir wrote at the Café de Flore and Les Deux Magots, while jazz cellars and Juliette Gréco filled Saint-Germain’s nights.

The two literary cafés facing each other on Boulevard Saint-Germain.

Saint-Sulpice

A huge classical church, famous for its Delacroix murals and fountain square.

Rue de Buci and Rue de Seine

Food shops, café terraces and art galleries.

How to get to Saint-Germain-des-Prés

By metro

Line 4 to Saint-Germain-des-Prés; line 10 to Mabillon or Odéon.

On foot

15 minutes from the Louvre across the Pont des Arts.

Starting point

Place Saint-Germain-des-Prés, in front of the church, in the 6th arrondissement.

Tips for Saint-Germain-des-Prés

  • Have a coffee on a terrace in the morning, before the crowds arrive.
  • Carry on to the Luxembourg Gardens, ten minutes away on foot.
  • The galleries on Rue de Seine are free and open in the afternoon.
  • A guided food tour lets you taste chocolate, pastries and cheese.

Frequently asked questions

What is there to do in Saint-Germain-des-Prés?

Visit the church of Saint-Germain-des-Prés, have a coffee at the Flore or Les Deux Magots, stroll along Rue de Seine and Rue de Buci, then walk on to Saint-Sulpice and the Luxembourg.

Can you visit the church of Saint-Germain-des-Prés?

Yes, entry is free outside services.

How much does a food tour cost?

Expect around a hundred euros for two to three hours of tastings with a guide.

How long should you spend in Saint-Germain-des-Prés?

Allow half a day.

What is there to see near Saint-Germain-des-Prés?

In this guide, the closest places are Saint-Michel (about 15 min on foot), Louvre (about 15 min on foot) and Musée d’Orsay (about 15 min on foot).
