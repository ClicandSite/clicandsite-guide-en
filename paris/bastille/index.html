The Bastille, from Revolution to café terraces

The fortress vanished in 1789, but the square remains the symbol of the Revolution. Around it: a modern opera house, one of the biggest markets in Paris and a garden walk on an old viaduct.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 12 November 2026

Nothing remains of the prison stormed on 14 July 1789: its outline is simply marked out in paving stones. In the middle of the square stands the July Column, raised in memory of the 1830 revolution and topped by the gilded Spirit of Freedom.

The square, where the 4th, 11th and 12th arrondissements meet, is surrounded by lively neighbourhoods: Rue de Lappe and its bars, the Faubourg Saint-Antoine, historic home of the cabinet makers, and the Arsenal marina, where the Canal Saint-Martin begins.

Things to see around the Bastille

The July Column

A 50-metre bronze column in the middle of the square, crowned by the Spirit of Freedom. Book a guided tour or activity around the Bastille

The Opéra Bastille

The great modern opera house of Paris, opened in 1989 for the bicentenary of the Revolution. Guided tours and performances.

The Bastille market

One of the largest open-air markets in Paris, on Boulevard Richard-Lenoir, on Thursday and Sunday mornings.

The Coulée Verte René-Dumont

A garden walk on an old railway viaduct, with the craft workshops of the Viaduc des Arts below.

The Arsenal marina

The boat basin between the Bastille and the Seine, starting point for Canal Saint-Martin cruises.

Rue de Lappe

A little street of bars and dance halls, very lively in the evening.

How to get to the Bastille

By metro

Bastille station (lines 1, 5 and 8), right on the square and next to the Opéra.

On foot

From Le Marais, Place des Vosges is just five minutes from the Bastille.

Practical info 2026: Bastille

  • Bastille market: Boulevard Richard-Lenoir, Thursday and Sunday mornings.
  • Opéra Bastille: guided tours of the building on certain days, to be booked on the Opéra de Paris website.

Tips for visiting the Bastille

  • Come on Sunday morning for the market, then walk up to Place des Vosges and Le Marais.
  • Climb onto the Coulée Verte by the steps near the Opéra: the walk to the Jardin de Reuilly takes half an hour.
  • In the evening, Rue de Lappe and Rue de la Roquette are the liveliest streets in the area.

Frequently asked questions

Is anything left of the Bastille prison?

No, it was demolished after 1789. Its outline is marked in paving stones, and some remains can be seen in Bastille metro station, line 5.

Which days is the Bastille market on?

Thursday and Sunday mornings, on Boulevard Richard-Lenoir.

What is there to see around the Bastille?

The July Column, the Opéra Bastille, the Coulée Verte, the Arsenal marina and nearby Place des Vosges.

How long should you spend around the Bastille?

Allow half a day.

What is there to see near the Bastille?

In this guide, the closest places are Le Marais (about 5 min on foot), Picasso Museum (about 15 min on foot) and Île Saint-Louis (about 15 min on foot).
