La Défense, Paris of the skyscrapers

At the end of the Champs-Élysées axis, Europe’s leading business district is also an open-air art museum, dominated by the Grande Arche.

La Défense spreads across Puteaux, Courbevoie and Nanterre, just west of Paris. Begun in the late 1950s, it is Europe’s leading business district: dozens of office towers around a vast pedestrian esplanade.

It lies exactly in line with the historic axis that runs from the Louvre through the Champs-Élysées and the Arc de Triomphe to the Grande Arche. The esplanade is also scattered with monumental artworks you can see for free.

A bit of history

The district is named after a statue, The Defence of Paris, erected in 1883 to commemorate the fighting of the 1870 siege. The business-district project was launched in 1958, the year the CNIT opened with its record-breaking concrete vault.

The Grande Arche, designed by Dane Johan Otto von Spreckelsen, was inaugurated in 1989 for the bicentenary of the Revolution; it extends the historic axis that starts at the Louvre.

Things to see at La Défense

The Grande Arche

The hollow cube inaugurated on 14 July 1989 for the bicentenary of the Revolution. Admire it from the esplanade and its steps. Book a guided tour or activity at La Défense

The esplanade and the historic axis

From the steps of the Grande Arche, the view runs all the way to the Arc de Triomphe and, beyond, the Louvre.

Open-air art

Monumental sculptures by Calder, Miró and César, including his famous Thumb, scattered across the esplanade.

The CNIT

The oldest building in the district, with its shell-shaped concrete vault.

Paris La Défense Arena

The big concert and rugby venue just west of the Grande Arche.

How to get to La Défense

By metro and RER

Metro line 1 and RER A, La Défense (Grande Arche) station; tram T2 also serves the district.

From central Paris

Line 1 runs directly from the Champs-Élysées and the Arc de Triomphe to La Défense in about ten minutes.

Practical info 2026: La Défense

  • Grande Arche rooftop: permanently closed since 28 April 2023; the Grande Arche is now seen from the outside, for free.
  • Christmas market 2026: on the esplanade, with a 504 m² ice rink, from 12 November 2026 to 3 January 2027.

Prices and opening times checked in November 2026 with official websites and the Paris tourist office; they may change, so check before you go.

Tips for visiting La Défense

  • Come on a weekday at lunchtime for the business-district buzz, or at the weekend for peace and quiet.
  • Climb the steps of the Grande Arche at sunset, when the sun goes down in line with the Champs-Élysées.
  • Make the most of it and head to the Fondation Louis Vuitton, on the other side of the Bois de Boulogne.

Frequently asked questions

Can you go up on the roof of the Grande Arche?

No, the rooftop has been permanently closed since 28 April 2023; you can enjoy the arch from the esplanade.

When is the La Défense Christmas market in 2026?

From 12 November 2026 to 3 January 2027, with an ice rink on the esplanade.

How do I get to La Défense from Paris?

By metro line 1 or RER A to La Défense (Grande Arche).

How long should you spend at La Défense?

Allow two hours.

What is there to see near La Défense?

In this guide, the closest places are Louis Vuitton Foundation (about 3 km away), Arc de Triomphe (about 6 km away) and Trocadéro (about 6 km away).
