An evening at the Moulin Rouge

Feathers, rhinestones, French Cancan and a two-hour revue under the famous windmill in Pigalle. The Moulin Rouge is still the world’s most famous cabaret.

By Anthony, Cannes · Updated 12 November 2026

Opened in 1889 at the foot of Montmartre, the Moulin Rouge gave birth to the French Cancan and inspired Toulouse-Lautrec. Today its revue “Féerie” brings together about 80 performers, including the famous Doriss Girls, with handmade sets and costumes.

There are two shows every evening, at 9pm and 11pm, with half a bottle of champagne per person. You can also have dinner from 7pm before the 9pm show. Evenings sell out fast, especially at the end of the year, so book ahead.

What to expect at the Moulin Rouge

The Féerie revue

Two hours of dance tableaux, visual acts and French Cancan, in a Belle Époque setting.

The French Cancan

The most eagerly awaited moment of the evening, true to the venue’s tradition.

Dinner and show

Dinner served from 7pm with the orchestra, before the 9pm show.

Montmartre

The cabaret is at the foot of the hill: pair it with a walk up to the Sacré-Cœur before the show.

How to get to the Moulin Rouge

By metro

Line 2, Blanche station, right opposite the cabaret.

Address

82 Boulevard de Clichy, in the 18th arrondissement.

By taxi

Handy for getting back after the 11pm show, which ends around 1am.

Practical info 2026: The Moulin Rouge

  • Féerie revue: at 9pm and 11pm; show with half a bottle of champagne from €142, dinner and show (7pm) from €255.
  • In winter: until the end of March, the 11pm show only runs on Fridays and Saturdays.
  • Christmas: dinner and show on 24 December 2026 at €290, champagne included. Children admitted from age 6. Métro Blanche (line 2).

Prices and opening hours checked in November 2026 with the official sites and the Paris tourist office; they can change, so check before you go.

Tips for an evening at the Moulin Rouge

  • Book several weeks ahead for weekends and the Christmas period.
  • The 11pm show is often cheaper than the 9pm one.
  • Smart dress is required: no shorts, sportswear, trainers or flip-flops.

Frequently asked questions

How much does an evening at the Moulin Rouge cost?

In 2026, expect from about €115 to €150 for the show with champagne depending on the time, and from €255 for dinner and show.

What is the minimum age for the Moulin Rouge?

Children are admitted from age 6, but the show is really aimed at adults.

What should I wear to the Moulin Rouge?

Smart casual: shorts, sportswear, trainers and flip-flops are not allowed.
