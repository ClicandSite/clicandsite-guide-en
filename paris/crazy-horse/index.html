An evening at the Crazy Horse, Avenue George V

Opened in 1951 on Avenue George V, a short walk from the Champs-Élysées, the Crazy Horse made its name with a stylised art of the nude, sculpted by light. Its intimate room, set in former cellars, has welcomed guest stars such as Dita Von Teese.

The current show, “Totally Crazy”, lasts about 90 minutes. It runs twice a night on weekdays and three times on Saturdays, with or without champagne, and can be paired with dinner at the Ginger restaurant nearby.

A bit of history

The Crazy Horse opened in 1951 at 12 avenue George-V, created by Alain Bernardin, a lover of art and the American West. He invented a cabaret style of its own, where light projected straight onto the dancers matters as much as the choreography.

It has hosted many famous guest stars, from Dita Von Teese to Arielle Dombasle, and remains one of the emblematic cabarets of Paris.

What to expect at the Crazy Horse

The Totally Crazy show

A series of short tableaux, light effects and projections built around the Crazy dancers. Book an evening at the Crazy Horse

The room

A small, plush red room: you can see the stage well from almost every seat.

Champagne packages

A half bottle of champagne or drinks included, depending on the ticket.

The Crazy Experience

A backstage tour with one of the dancers, in a VIP package.

Practical info 2026: The Crazy Horse

  • “Totally Crazy!” show: Sunday to Friday at 8pm and 10:30pm; Saturday at 7pm, 9:30pm and 11:45pm.
  • Prices: €115 to €295 depending on the package (show only, champagne, dinner, backstage).
  • 75 years: the cabaret turns 75 in 2026. Address: 12 avenue George-V; Alma–George-V car park at no. 10.

Prices and opening hours checked in November 2026 with the official sites and the Paris tourist office; they can change, so check before you go.

Tips for the Crazy Horse

  • Book ahead, especially at weekends and over the holidays.
  • Arrive at least 30 minutes early: the show starts exactly on time.
  • Smart dress is expected: no shorts or flip-flops.
  • The show includes nudity: the minimum age is 10 with an adult, but it is really aimed at adults.

Frequently asked questions

How much does an evening at the Crazy Horse cost?

In 2026, expect from about €119 for the show only, €139 with champagne, and around €200 with dinner at Ginger.

What time are the shows?

Usually at 8pm and 10.30pm Sunday to Friday, and at 7pm, 9.30pm and 11.45pm on Saturdays. Check times when you book.

What is the minimum age?

10 with an adult, though some packages are for older guests only. The show includes artistic nudity.
