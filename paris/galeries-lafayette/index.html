Galeries Lafayette Haussmann, the dome and the rooftop

Under an Art Nouveau dome from 1912, Paris’s most famous department store also hides one of the best free views of the city.

By Anthony, Cannes · Updated 12 November 2026

Even if you don’t buy anything, Galeries Lafayette Haussmann is worth a visit. The department store, in the heart of the 9th arrondissement, is crowned by a glass and steel dome with colourful stained glass, completed in 1912.

Right at the top, the terrace offers a free view of the Palais Garnier, the Eiffel Tower and the rooftops of Paris. For Christmas 2026 the store presents “Au cœur de Noël”, designed by Jean-Charles de Castelbajac, from 12 November 2026 to 5 January 2027.

What to see at Galeries Lafayette

The dome

The Art Nouveau stained glass, best seen from the balconies or from the glass walkway suspended beneath it.

The rooftop

The free panoramic terrace at the top of the store, with views of the Opéra and the Eiffel Tower.

The Christmas tree

A giant tree set up under the dome every year, a tradition since 1976.

The Christmas windows

The animated windows on Boulevard Haussmann, a highlight for families.

How to get to Galeries Lafayette

By metro

Line 7 or 9 to Chaussée d’Antin – La Fayette, or Opéra station.

By RER

RER A to Auber, a few minutes away on foot.

Address

40 Boulevard Haussmann, in the 9th arrondissement.

Tips for visiting Galeries Lafayette

  • Come on a weekday at opening time to enjoy the dome without crowds.
  • The rooftop ice rink is open from 24 November 2026 to 3 January 2027.
  • Carry on to the Palais Garnier, just behind the store.
  • The Christmas windows look best after dark.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Galeries Lafayette rooftop free?

Yes, the panoramic terrace is free to visit during store opening hours.

What are the opening hours of Galeries Lafayette Haussmann?

The store is open Monday to Saturday from 10am to 8.30pm and on Sunday from 11am to 8pm. Hours may be extended in December.

When can I see the Galeries Lafayette Christmas tree?

The 2026 Christmas display, “Au cœur de Noël”, runs from 12 November 2026 to 5 January 2027.
