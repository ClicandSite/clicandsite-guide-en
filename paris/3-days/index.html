Paris in 3 days

Three days for the essentials of Paris, without rushing. Stops are grouped by neighbourhood, and each day takes the museums’ closing days into account.

  1. Day 1: Île de la Cité and the Marais

    • Notre-Dame: in the morning, Notre-Dame with a booked slot, then Square Jean-XXIII for the view of the apse.
    • Sainte-Chapelle: five minutes’ walk to the Sainte-Chapelle, ideally when the sun is high.
    • Le Marais: in the afternoon, cross the Île Saint-Louis to the Marais: Place des Vosges, the Musée Carnavalet and Rue des Rosiers.

  2. Day 2: the Louvre, the Opera and Montmartre

    • Louvre: the Louvre at opening time (not on a Tuesday), with a planned route.
    • Palais Garnier: walk up Avenue de l’Opéra to the Palais Garnier, then the Galeries Lafayette rooftop.
    • Montmartre: metro to Abbesses, walk up through the village and watch the sunset from the Sacré-Cœur.

  3. Day 3: Orsay, the Eiffel Tower and the Seine

    • Musée d’Orsay: the Musée d’Orsay in the morning (not on a Monday), starting on the 5th floor.
    • Eiffel Tower: walk along the river to the Eiffel Tower, with a booked slot in mid-afternoon.
    • Seine cruise: a sunset cruise from the foot of the tower, to watch Paris light up.

Going further

With a fourth day, spend it at Versailles (not on a Monday). For a quieter afternoon, swap the Eiffel Tower for the Arc de Triomphe and its rooftop, or for the Latin Quarter and the Luxembourg Gardens.

Guides: Versailles, Arc de Triomphe, Latin Quarter and Luxembourg.

Tips for these three days

  • Stay near a metro line 1 or line 4 station: between them they serve most of the stops on this itinerary.
  • Book the Louvre, the Sainte-Chapelle and the Eiffel Tower now: slots are compulsory or in high demand.
  • Remember the closing days: the Louvre on Tuesdays, Orsay and Versailles on Mondays.
