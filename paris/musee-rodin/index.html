The Rodin Museum and its sculpture garden

The Thinker, The Kiss, The Gates of Hell: Rodin’s masterpieces in an 18th-century mansion and a three-hectare garden.

By Anthony, Cannes · Updated 12 November 2026

Auguste Rodin lived and worked at the Hôtel Biron on Rue de Varenne late in life. He left his works to the French state on condition that a museum be created there; it opened in 1919.

The rooms display his marbles and bronzes, as well as sculptures by Camille Claudel, his pupil and partner. The garden, with its large outdoor sculptures, is a reason to come in itself.

What to see at the Rodin Museum

The Thinker

Rodin’s most famous bronze sits in the garden among the clipped yews.

The Gates of Hell

The monumental doorway inspired by Dante, which Rodin worked on for decades.

The Kiss

The marble of two entwined lovers, one of the most loved sculptures in the world.

Camille Claudel’s works

A room devoted to the sculptor, including The Waltz and The Age of Maturity.

How to get to the Rodin Museum

By metro

Line 13 to Varenne, right next to the entrance.

By RER

RER C to Invalides, ten minutes away on foot.

Address

77 Rue de Varenne, in the 7th arrondissement.

Practical info 2026: The Rodin Museum

  • Price: €15; closed on Mondays.
  • Combined ticket with the Army Museum (Invalides, next door): €26.
  • Getting there: 79 rue de Varenne, métro Varenne (line 13), two minutes on foot.

Tips for visiting the Rodin Museum

  • The museum is closed on Mondays: plan your visit for another day.
  • On a sunny day, save time for the garden and its café.
  • On the first Sunday of the month from October to March, entry is free for everyone.
  • Les Invalides is five minutes away: the two visits pair easily.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a ticket to the Rodin Museum?

Expect about €15 for an adult in 2026. Entry is free for under-18s and for EU residents under 26.

What are the Rodin Museum opening hours?

It is open Tuesday to Sunday from 10am to 6.30pm, with last entry 45 minutes before closing. It is closed on Mondays.

Can I visit just the garden?

The museum ticket includes the garden and its outdoor sculptures, which many visitors enjoy most.
