The Sacré-Cœur Basilica in Montmartre

Gleaming white on top of the hill, the basilica offers one of the best views of Paris, from its forecourt or from the top of its dome.

Begun in 1875 and consecrated in 1919, the Sacré-Cœur Basilica crowns the Butte Montmartre, the highest point in Paris. Its Château-Landon stone whitens in the rain, which keeps it so bright.

Entry to the basilica is free. For the 360-degree view you climb the 300 or so steps of the dome, which has a fee. The forecourt and steps are the big gathering place at sunset.

What to see at the Sacré-Cœur

The apse mosaic

A huge mosaic of Christ in glory above the altar, among the largest in France. Book tickets or a guided tour at the Sacré-Cœur.

The dome

About 300 spiral steps for a panoramic view over all of Paris on a clear day.

The forecourt

The free view over the rooftops of Paris, with musicians and crowds at all hours.

Square Louise-Michel

The terraced gardens and the carousel at the foot of the basilica.

How to get to the Sacré-Cœur

By metro

Line 2 to Anvers, then walk up or take the funicular; or line 12 to Abbesses.

By funicular

It runs from Square Louise-Michel to the foot of the basilica with a metro ticket.

Address

35 Rue du Chevalier-de-la-Barre, in the 18th arrondissement.

Practical info 2026: The Sacré-Cœur

  • Basilica: free entry, daily 6:30am to 10:30pm.
  • Dome: €8, €5 for under-16s; about 300 steps, no lift.
  • Funicular: one Métro-Train-RER ticket at €2.55; note that a ticket already validated in the métro no longer works for the funicular. Métro Anvers (line 2).

Prices and opening hours checked in November 2026 with the official sites and the Paris tourist office; they can change, so check before you go.

Tips for visiting the Sacré-Cœur

  • Come early in the morning to enjoy an almost empty forecourt.
  • Photos are not allowed inside the basilica, and modest dress is expected.
  • Watch out for bracelet sellers at the foot of the steps: politely say no and keep walking.
  • Carry on to Place du Tertre and the lanes of Montmartre, just behind.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Sacré-Cœur free to visit?

Yes, the basilica is free and open from 6.30am to 10.30pm. Only the dome climb has a fee, about €8 for an adult.

How many steps are there to the top of the dome?

About 300 steps, with no lift. The dome closes earlier than the basilica, around 5.30pm in winter.

How can I avoid climbing all the steps up the hill?

Take the funicular from Square Louise-Michel: it runs on a standard metro ticket.
