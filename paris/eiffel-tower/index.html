Visiting the Eiffel Tower

Three levels, over 300 metres and the most famous view in Paris. Plan ahead: official tickets, the right time slot and, if your legs are up to it, the stairs.

Built by Gustave Eiffel for the 1889 World’s Fair, the tower was meant to be taken down twenty years later. It has three levels: the 1st floor with its glass floor, the 2nd floor, where the view is easiest to read, and the top, reached by lift only.

You can walk up to the 2nd floor — 674 steps — then take the lift to the top. Either way, buy your tickets on the official website: fake ticketing sites imitate the tower and sell tickets that cannot be used.

Things to do at the Eiffel Tower

The 1st floor and its glass floor

The largest level, with a glass floor 57 metres above the ground and panels on how the tower was built. It is also the least crowded.

The 2nd floor

For many, the best view: high enough to take in all of Paris, low enough to pick out every monument from Montmartre to Les Invalides.

The top

Reached only by lift from the 2nd floor. You can see Gustave Eiffel’s reconstructed office and, on a clear day, for dozens of kilometres.

The evening sparkle

Every hour after dark, the tower sparkles for five minutes. It is best watched from the ground.

Trocadéro and the Champ de Mars

The two esplanades on either side give the classic photos: Trocadéro facing the tower, the Champ de Mars to see its full height.

How to get to the Eiffel Tower

By metro and RER

Bir-Hakeim (line 6) and Trocadéro (lines 6 and 9), or Champ de Mars - Tour Eiffel on RER C. Arriving via Trocadéro gives you the best first view.

By boat

Seine boats and the Batobus stop at the foot of the tower, so you can combine your visit with a cruise.

By taxi or with a driver

Traffic and drop-offs right by the tower are difficult; ask to be dropped on Quai Branly or at Trocadéro.

Tips for visiting the Eiffel Tower

  • Book as early as you can: summit slots sell out fast during school holidays.
  • On an overcast day, skip the top: the 2nd floor is enough and the view is better below the clouds.
  • Security checks take time; bring a small bag and arrive a little before your slot.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to book the Eiffel Tower?

It is strongly recommended, and climbing the stairs now has to be booked too. Buy only on the official website or from a recognised reseller.

Can you walk to the top of the Eiffel Tower?

No. The stairs go to the 2nd floor (674 steps); the top is reached by lift from there.

When does the Eiffel Tower sparkle?

Every hour after dark, for five minutes, until closing time.
