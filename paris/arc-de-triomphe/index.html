Visiting the Arc de Triomphe

At the top of the Champs-Élysées, 284 steps lead to a rooftop where twelve avenues fan out like a star. It is one of the most striking views in Paris.

By Anthony, Cannes · Updated 12 November 2026

Napoleon commissioned the Arc de Triomphe in 1806 to honour the Grande Armée. The Unknown Soldier has lain beneath the arch since 1921, and the flame of remembrance is rekindled every evening in a short ceremony.

The base of the monument and the tomb are free to visit. The rooftop, 50 metres up, is reached by 284 steps; the lift is for visitors with reduced mobility. Use the underground passage to get there: never cross the roundabout.

Things to do at the Arc de Triomphe

The rooftop

The view over the twelve avenues of Place de l’Étoile, the historic axis to the Louvre and, the other way, La Défense.

The Tomb of the Unknown Soldier

Under the arch, with the flame rekindled every evening at 6:30pm.

The sculptures

François Rude’s Departure of the Volunteers of 1792, known as La Marseillaise, on the Champs-Élysées side.

The Champs-Élysées

Two kilometres down to Concorde. In December, the Christmas lights make it one of the city’s most popular walks.

How to get to the Arc de Triomphe

By metro and RER

Charles de Gaulle - Étoile (lines 1, 2, 6 and RER A). Follow signs for the Arc de Triomphe exit to come out at the underground passage.

On foot

Walk up the Champs-Élysées from Concorde: about half an hour.

Practical info 2026: The Arc de Triomphe

  • Price: €16 online in low season (October to March); free for under-18s. The panoramic terrace is 50 m up.
  • Access: only through the underpasses from the Champs-Élysées or avenue de la Grande-Armée; never try to cross the roundabout.
  • Closed: 1 January, 11 November and 25 December. Last entry 45 minutes before closing.

Prices and opening hours checked in November 2026 with the official sites and the Paris tourist office; they can change, so check before you go.

Tips for visiting the Arc de Triomphe

  • Go up an hour before sunset and come down for the flame ceremony.
  • Buy your ticket online: the only wait is then the security check.
  • On 11 November, 8 May and 14 July, access is restricted during ceremonies: check before you go.

Frequently asked questions

How many steps to the top of the Arc de Triomphe?

284 steps. The lift is reserved for visitors with reduced mobility.

How do you get to the Arc de Triomphe?

Through the underground passage from the top of the Champs-Élysées or Avenue de la Grande-Armée. Do not cross the roundabout: it is dangerous and forbidden.

What time is the flame rekindled?

Every evening at 6:30pm, under the arch. The ceremony is public and free.
