The Île Saint-Louis, a village in the middle of the Seine

A quiet little island just behind Notre-Dame: 17th-century mansions, shady quays and the most famous ice cream shop in Paris.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 12 November 2026

The Île Saint-Louis is the smaller of the two natural islands in central Paris, just upstream from the Île de la Cité. It was entirely built up in the 17th century, which gives it a rare unity: straight streets, grand mansions and tree-lined quays.

You can walk all the way round in half an hour. Rue Saint-Louis-en-l’Île, running the length of the island, has the shops, cheesemongers and Berthillon; the quays are among the quietest in Paris.

Things to see on the Île Saint-Louis

Rue Saint-Louis-en-l’Île

The island’s main street, with its shops, delicatessens and tea rooms. Book a guided tour or activity on the Île Saint-Louis

Berthillon

The most famous ice cream maker in Paris, at 29-31 Rue Saint-Louis-en-l’Île, open since 1954. Several cafés on the island also sell its ice cream.

Quai d’Orléans

The most spectacular view of the back of Notre-Dame and its flying buttresses.

Saint-Louis-en-l’Île church

A Baroque church with a richly decorated interior, recognisable by its openwork spire.

Quai de Bourbon and Quai d’Anjou

17th-century mansions, including the Hôtel de Lauzun and the Hôtel Lambert, right on the water.

Pont Saint-Louis

The footbridge linking the island to Notre-Dame, often livened up by street musicians.

How to get to the Île Saint-Louis

By metro

Pont Marie (line 7) or Sully-Morland (line 7), two minutes’ walk from the island.

On foot

From Notre-Dame, cross the Pont Saint-Louis; from Le Marais, the Pont Marie.

Practical info 2026: Île Saint-Louis

  • Berthillon (29-31 Rue Saint-Louis-en-l’Île): open Wednesday to Sunday, 10am to 8pm; closed Monday and Tuesday.
  • Notre-Dame: entry to the cathedral, right opposite, is free, and booking a time slot is recommended.

Prices and opening times checked in November 2026 with official websites and the Paris tourist office; they may change, so check before you go.

Tips for visiting the Île Saint-Louis

  • Walk round the island along the lower quays by the water, then come back along Rue Saint-Louis-en-l’Île.
  • If Berthillon is closed (Monday and Tuesday), the cafés on the island selling its ice cream are often open.
  • Carry on to Le Marais, across the Pont Marie.

Frequently asked questions

When is Berthillon open?

Wednesday to Sunday, 10am to 8pm; the shop is closed on Mondays and Tuesdays.

Where can I see the back of Notre-Dame?

From Quai d’Orléans and the Pont Saint-Louis, on the Île Saint-Louis.

How long does it take to visit the Île Saint-Louis?

An hour is enough to walk round it, ice cream included.
