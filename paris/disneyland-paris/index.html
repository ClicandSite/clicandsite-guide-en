Disneyland Paris: visiting both parks

Two parks, a dining village and, since March 2026, World of Frozen. Disneyland Paris is a direct RER ride from central Paris.

Disneyland Paris has two parks in Marne-la-Vallée, east of Paris: Disneyland Park, with its castle and the great classics, and Disney Adventure World, the former Walt Disney Studios Park, renamed and transformed on 29 March 2026. Disney Village, with restaurants and shops, lies between them.

The big new arrival is World of Frozen, with the village of Arendelle on a large lake, next to Avengers Campus and the Pixar lands. Tickets are dated: buy them in advance, especially during school holidays.

Things to do at Disneyland Paris

Disneyland Park

Sleeping Beauty Castle, Main Street, Pirates of the Caribbean, Big Thunder Mountain and the afternoon parade.

Disney Adventure World

The second park, completely redesigned around a lake, with Disney, Pixar and Marvel lands and a night-time show.

World of Frozen

The village of Arendelle, a boat ride through the film’s story and character meetings. It is the busiest area: go early.

Avengers Campus

The Marvel land, with thrill rides for older children and adults.

Disney Village

Restaurants, shops and a cinema, free to enter, between the station and the parks.

How to get to Disneyland Paris

By RER A

To the Marne-la-Vallée - Chessy terminus, right outside the park entrance. Allow about 40 minutes from central Paris.

By TGV

Marne-la-Vallée - Chessy TGV station, in the same place, has direct trains from many French cities.

By car or with a driver

Via the A4 motorway. Park parking is paid; a private transfer saves changes with children.

Tips for Disneyland Paris

  • Arrive before opening and head first for the busiest rides, such as the Frozen attraction.
  • Download the official app: it shows live waiting times.
  • On weekdays outside school holidays, queues are much shorter than at weekends.

Frequently asked questions

How do you get to Disneyland Paris from Paris?

Take RER A to the Marne-la-Vallée - Chessy terminus, right outside the parks, in about 40 minutes from the centre.

What is Disney Adventure World?

It is the new name of the former Walt Disney Studios Park since 29 March 2026, now home to World of Frozen.

Should I book Disneyland tickets in advance?

Yes, tickets are dated and some days sell out during the holidays, so buy them before you go.
