Christmas markets in France 2026: the calendar

Alsace, Paris or the French Riviera: opening and closing dates of the main 2026 Christmas markets.

Photo: Pierre Blaché / Unsplash

  3. Christmas markets in France 2026

Christmas markets in France open between mid-November and early December 2026. Three regions stand out: Alsace for the traditional atmosphere, Paris for the lights and the department stores, and the French Riviera for Christmas in the sun. Here is the 2026 calendar, by opening date, with a link to our detailed guide for each destination.

Entry is free at all these markets.

The 2026 calendar

  • 12 Nov
    Paris – La Défense

    One of the largest Christmas markets in the Paris region, until 31 December 2026.

  • 14 Nov
    Paris – Tuileries

    Until 3 January 2027, every day from 11 am to 11.30 pm.

  • 22 Nov
    Paris – Champs-Élysées

    The lights are switched on on 22 November and the Christmas market returns to the avenue, from Concorde to the Rond-Point, until 4 January 2027.

  • 23 Nov
    Colmar

    Six markets in the old town until 29 December; the food market and the big wheel until 3 January 2027.

  • 26 Nov
    Paris – Notre-Dame

    Square René-Viviani, opposite the cathedral, until 27 December 2026.

  • 27 Nov
    Strasbourg

    The Christkindelsmärik on Place Broglie, France’s oldest, until 24 December 2026.

  • 27 Nov
    Riquewihr et Kaysersberg

    Until 20 December 2026; Kaysersberg from Friday to Sunday only. Christmas shuttles from Colmar station.

  • 27 Nov
    Nice et Cannes

    Until 3 January 2027: Jardin Albert-Ier and Place Masséna in Nice, Allées de la Liberté in Cannes.

  • 4 Dec
    Monaco

    The Christmas village on Port Hercule, Quai Albert-Ier, until 3 January 2027, with an ice rink next door.

Which region to choose?

Alsace

The most traditional: wooden chalets, mulled wine and half-timbered villages. Strasbourg and Colmar are linked by train, and shuttles serve the villages from Colmar.

Paris

For the Champs-Élysées lights, the department store windows and several markets, best combined with a Seine cruise.

The French Riviera

The mildest Christmas: Nice, Cannes and Monaco are less than an hour apart by train, and the markets stay open until 3 January 2027.

Our tips

  • Go on a weekday: Saturday is the busiest day everywhere.
  • Check the closing dates: Riquewihr and Kaysersberg close on 20 December, Strasbourg on the 24th; Paris and the Riviera stay open until early January.
  • Book your hotel early, especially in Strasbourg and Colmar for December weekends.

Frequently asked questions

When do Christmas markets open in France in 2026?

Between mid-November and early December: La Défense on 12 November, the Tuileries on the 14th, Colmar on the 23rd, Strasbourg, Riquewihr, Kaysersberg, Nice and Cannes on 27 November, and Monaco on 4 December 2026.

What is the oldest Christmas market in France?

Strasbourg’s Christkindelsmärik, which dates back to 1570. In 2026 it is open from 27 November to 24 December on Place Broglie.

Which Christmas markets are open after Christmas?

Paris (Tuileries and Champs-Élysées), Nice, Cannes and Monaco stay open until 3 or 4 January 2027, and Colmar until 29 December, with its food market until 3 January.
