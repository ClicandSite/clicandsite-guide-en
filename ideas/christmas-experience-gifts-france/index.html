Christmas gift ideas 2026: give an experience in France

Cruise, cabaret, palace, mountains or Riviera: our ideas for gifts to live, and how to give them.

Photo: Mathias Reding / Unsplash

  1. Home
  2. Ideas
  3. Experience gifts for Christmas

Give an outing rather than an object: dinner on the Seine, a night at the cabaret, a day at Versailles or Mont-Saint-Michel, a ride up the Aiguille du Midi… Here are our experience gift ideas in France for Christmas 2026, sorted by mood, each with our guide to help you choose.

For a couple

A Seine dinner cruise

Paris lit up from the river, from the Eiffel Tower to Notre-Dame. There are daytime, sunset and dinner cruises: our guide compares the companies and departure points.

A night at the Crazy Horse

The cabaret on Avenue George V stages “Totally Crazy”, a show of about 90 minutes, with or without champagne, which can be combined with dinner.

A Valentine’s weekend

14 February 2027 falls on a Sunday: a gift to open at Christmas and enjoy two months later. Our ideas for a weekend for two in Paris and on the Riviera.

For the whole family

A day at Disneyland Paris

The children’s classic, with or without transport from Paris.

The Palace of Versailles

The palace, the Hall of Mirrors and the gardens, on your own, with a guide or by bike around the estate.

Mont-Saint-Michel

A day trip from Paris or a guided tour on site, to discover the abbey and the bay.

For mountain or sunshine lovers

The Aiguille du Midi in Chamonix

The cable car climbs to 3,842 metres in about twenty minutes, facing Mont Blanc. Combine it with the Montenvers train and the Mer de Glace.

Monaco and the Riviera

The Rock, the Monte-Carlo casino and the hilltop village of Èze: a day trip in the winter sun.

How to give an activity

  • Choose the date together: give the idea at Christmas (a card, a photo of the place) and book the day that suits afterwards.
  • Check the cancellation terms: many online activities can be cancelled free of charge up to 24 hours before, but not all; check the offer page before you pay.
  • Think about the holidays: an outing for the Christmas holidays or the February 2027 holidays.
  • On the slopes, lessons and guided outings should be booked early for the holiday periods.

Frequently asked questions

What experience can I give for Christmas in France?

For a couple, a Seine dinner cruise or a night at the Crazy Horse; for a family, Disneyland Paris, Versailles or Mont-Saint-Michel; for mountain lovers, the Aiguille du Midi in Chamonix.

Can I give an activity without knowing the date?

The easiest way is to give the idea at Christmas and book the date together afterwards. If you book ahead, choose an offer with free cancellation and check its terms before you pay.

What outing can I give for Valentine’s Day 2027?

14 February 2027 falls on a Sunday: a weekend in Paris with dinner on the Seine, or on the French Riviera during the mimosa season.
