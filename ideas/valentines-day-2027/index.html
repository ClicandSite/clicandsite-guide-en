Valentine’s Day 2027: romantic weekends in France

14 February 2027 falls on a Sunday: our ideas for a weekend for two, from the Seine to the French Riviera.

Photo: Bastien Nvs / Unsplash

  1. Home
  2. Ideas
  3. Valentine’s Day 2027

In 2027, Valentine’s Day falls on a Sunday, so the weekend of Saturday 13 and Sunday 14 February is ideal for a trip together. Here are our favourite places in France for a romantic weekend, from Paris to the French Riviera, each with a link to our detailed guide.

Our romantic weekend ideas

Paris, dinner on the Seine

The great classic: a dinner cruise on the Seine, from a simple menu to a gourmet dinner, with the Eiffel Tower lit up. Boats run all year round, except when the river floods. In 2027, 14 February falls on a Sunday: book early, as Saturday 13 and Sunday slots go fast.

Paris, Montmartre

Cobbled lanes, the Place du Tertre and the view from the Sacré-Cœur: Montmartre is made for a long walk together, ideally early in the morning before the tour groups arrive.

Èze, the hilltop village

Medieval lanes, a cactus garden at the top and a sweeping view over the Mediterranean: Èze is one of the most romantic villages on the French Riviera, halfway between Nice and Monaco. In February it is far quieter than in summer.

Saint-Paul-de-Vence

The ramparts, the art galleries and the Fondation Maeght next door: a quiet day for two, 30 minutes from Nice.

Menton and the Lemon Festival

The Lemon Festival opens on Saturday 13 February 2027, the day before Valentine’s Day, and the Nice Carnival, themed “Vive l’Amour” (Long live love), is in full swing from 9 to 28 February: a chance to combine a romantic weekend with a big show.

Colmar and Little Venice

Half-timbered houses, the canals of Little Venice and Alsace wines: Colmar is a charming getaway, quieter in February than at Christmas. Eguisheim and Riquewihr are a few minutes away.

Étretat and Honfleur

The Étretat cliffs at sunset, then Honfleur’s Old Harbour an hour’s drive away: an easy Normandy weekend from Paris.

Saint-Malo

A walk round the ramparts facing the sea, the big winter tides and a crêperie inside the walls: Saint-Malo keeps all its charm out of season.

Our booking tips

  • Book early: dinner cruises and restaurants for Saturday 13 February fill up weeks ahead.
  • Watch out for the French school holidays: they start on 6 February 2027 for zone C (Paris), and mountain and Riviera hotels cost more.
  • Take the train: Paris–Nice, Paris–Colmar and Paris–Saint-Malo are all served by TGV, no car needed.

Frequently asked questions

What day is Valentine’s Day 2027?

Sunday 14 February 2027, so the weekend of 13 and 14 February is ideal for a trip together.

Where to go for a romantic weekend in France?

Paris for a Seine dinner cruise and Montmartre, the French Riviera for Èze, Saint-Paul-de-Vence and Menton, Alsace for Colmar, Normandy for Étretat and Honfleur, or Brittany for Saint-Malo.

Are there events on Valentine’s weekend 2027?

Yes: the Menton Lemon Festival opens on Saturday 13 February 2027 and the Nice Carnival runs from 9 to 28 February 2027.
