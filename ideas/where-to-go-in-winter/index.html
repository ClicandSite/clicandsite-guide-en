Where to go in France this winter

Reliable snow up high, Christmas lights in Paris or Mediterranean mildness: winter is a great season to travel in France, as long as you pick your dates well.

Here are our ideas to suit every taste, with the key dates for winter 2026-2027. Each link leads to a practical guide: what to see, how to get there and what to book.

Dates for your diary

  1. 21 Nov
    Val Thorens and Tignes open

    The first two major Alpine resorts start the season.

  2. 22 Nov
    Champs-Élysées Christmas lights

    Switched on that evening, with the return of the Champs-Élysées Christmas market until 4 January 2027.

  3. 28 Nov
    Val d’Isère and Chamonix open

    First areas open in Chamonix, full area in Val d’Isère.

  4. 4-5 Dec
    Courchevel, Méribel and Alpe d’Huez open

    The 3 Valleys and the Oisans start their season.

  5. 19 Dec
    Megève fully opens

    After a partial early opening in early December.

  6. 22-31 Jan
    Monte-Carlo International Circus Festival

    Under the Fontvieille big top in Monaco.

  7. 9-28 Feb
    Nice Carnival

    2027 theme: "Long Live Love", with parades and flower battles.

  8. 13-28 Feb
    Menton Lemon Festival

    Golden fruit parades on Sundays 14, 21 and 28 February.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I ski in France in November?

Val Thorens and Tignes open on 21 November 2026, then Val d’Isère and the first areas of Chamonix on 28 November, depending on snow.

Where can I find winter sun in France?

On the French Riviera: Nice, Menton and Monaco enjoy mild, bright days, with their big festivals in February.

When are the French Riviera’s big festivals in 2027?

Nice Carnival from 9 to 28 February 2027, Menton Lemon Festival from 13 to 28 February and the Mandelieu Mimosa Festival from 10 to 14 February.
