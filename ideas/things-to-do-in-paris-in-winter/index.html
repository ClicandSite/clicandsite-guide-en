Things to do in Paris in winter

Christmas lights, Christmas markets, major exhibitions and late-opening museums: winter is a great season to visit Paris, with fewer crowds at the monuments.

Photo: Pierre Blaché / Unsplash

  1. Home
  2. Ideas

Here is the calendar of events not to miss this winter, plus ideas for every kind of weather. Remember to book: most major sights require a time slot.

Dates for your diary

  1. Until 31 Dec
    La Défense Christmas market

    One of the largest in the region, open since 12 November.

  2. 22 Nov
    Champs-Élysées Christmas lights

    Switched on in the evening, then every night until early January.

  3. 22 Nov - 4 Jan
    Champs-Élysées Christmas market

    Back after ten years, between Concorde and the Rond-Point.

  4. December
    Grand Palais ice rink

    A huge rink under the glass roof; book your slot.

  5. Until 10 Jan
    "The Warhol Line" at the Musée du Luxembourg

    Drawing and image in Andy Warhol’s work.

  6. Until 17 Jan
    "Cezanne and Us" at the Grand Palais

    The painter’s work and his legacy.

  7. Until 24 Jan
    Sofia Coppola’s "Marie-Antoinette" at Versailles

    An exhibition for the film’s 20th anniversary, at the palace.

  8. Until 24 Jan
    Tintoretto at the Musée Jacquemart-André

    The Venetian master in a 8th-arrondissement mansion.

  9. Until 31 Jan
    Nadar in the Catacombs

    "Inventing Paris, from the depths to the sky": the first photographs of underground Paris.

Frequently asked questions

What to do in Paris when it rains?

The big museums: the Louvre (late openings Wednesday and Friday), Orsay (late on Thursday), the Catacombs or the Palais Garnier.

Which Paris monuments are free on the first Sunday of the month?

From November to March, notably the Arc de Triomphe, the Sainte-Chapelle and the Palace of Versailles, with a booked slot for the last two.

Which exhibitions are on in Paris this winter?

Cezanne at the Grand Palais until 17 January, Warhol at the Musée du Luxembourg until 10 January, Tintoretto at the Musée Jacquemart-André and Marie-Antoinette at Versailles until 24 January 2027.
