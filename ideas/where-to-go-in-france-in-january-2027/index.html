Where to go in France in January 2027: ideas and events

The quietest month of winter: uncrowded skiing, Monaco in party mode, Riviera sunshine and the last big exhibitions in Paris.

Photo: yelr / Unsplash

  1. Home
  2. Ideas
  3. Where to go in January 2027

January is the quiet month of winter: the holidays are over, the February school break has not started and trains and ski resorts are less busy. It is a good time to ski in peace, find the sun on the French Riviera or catch the big Paris exhibitions before they close.

January 2027 events

  • 3 Jan
    Last days of the Christmas markets

    Nice, Cannes, Monaco and the Tuileries close on 3 January 2027; the Champs-Élysées market runs until the 4th.

  • 4 Jan
    End of the Christmas school holidays

    School resumes across France. Until 6 February 2027, no zone is on school holiday.

  • 10 Jan
    Warhol exhibition ends

    At the Musée du Luxembourg in Paris. Cezanne at the Grand Palais runs until 17 January.

  • 18 Jan
    Monte-Carlo Rally week

    Reconnaissance and events in Monaco, then the race from Thursday 21 to Sunday 24 January 2027, finishing at Port Hercule.

  • 22 Jan
    Monte-Carlo International Circus Festival

    49th edition, from 22 to 31 January 2027 in Monaco.

  • 24 Jan
    Marie-Antoinette exhibition ends

    At the Palace of Versailles.

  • late Jan
    First mimosa blossom

    Mimosa blooms from January to March between Bormes-les-Mimosas and Grasse, peaking in February.

Our ideas for January

Ski outside the school holidays

From Monday 4 January to Saturday 6 February 2027, no French school zone is on holiday: it is the quietest part of the season in the Alps and the Pyrenees. To compare prices, see our 2026-2027 lift pass comparison: from €44 a day at Isola 2000 or Auron to €84.20 in the 3 Vallées.

The Rally and the circus in Monaco

Two big events follow each other in Monaco: the Monte-Carlo Rally from 21 to 24 January, then the circus festival from 22 to 31 January. The Nice–Ventimiglia TER train stops at Monaco-Monte-Carlo station.

Riviera sunshine

Seaside walks, hilltop villages without the crowds and the first mimosa on the Mimosa Route. The big festivals (Nice Carnival, Menton Lemon Festival) come in February.

Paris exhibitions

Last weeks for Warhol at the Musée du Luxembourg (10 January), Cezanne at the Grand Palais (17 January) and Marie-Antoinette at Versailles (24 January 2027).

Our tips

  • Check snow conditions before you go: early in the season, the highest resorts are the safest bet.
  • For skiing, go before 6 February: then the February school holidays start for zone C.
  • For Monaco during the Rally, come by train: car parks are in high demand.

Frequently asked questions

Where should I go in France in January?

Skiing outside the school holidays (4 January to 6 February 2027), the French Riviera for sunshine and the first mimosa, Monaco for the Monte-Carlo Rally (21-24 January) and the circus festival (22-31 January), or Paris for the big exhibitions.

When is the 2027 Monte-Carlo Circus Festival?

The 49th Monte-Carlo International Circus Festival runs from 22 to 31 January 2027, in Monaco.

Is January a good month for skiing in France?

Yes: between the end of the Christmas holidays (4 January 2027) and the start of the February holidays (6 February for zone C), no school zone is on holiday, so slopes and accommodation are less busy.
