Where to go in France in 2027: month-by-month ideas

From the Monte-Carlo Rally in January to the Alsace Christmas markets in December: a destination and an event for every month of 2027.

The easiest way to plan a holiday in France in 2027 is to start from the season. Here, month by month, are the destinations and events we recommend, with the dates already announced for 2027. Each heading links to our detailed guide.

Where to go in France in 2027, month by month

January: The Monte-Carlo Rally

The 95th edition runs from 21 to 24 January 2027, finishing on Port Hercule in Monaco. A good chance to see the French Riviera without the summer crowds.

February: The Nice Carnival and the Lemon Festival

The Nice Carnival runs from 9 to 28 February 2027 and the Menton Lemon Festival from 13 to 28 February 2027: two big events half an hour apart. It is also the month of the ski holidays.

April: Giverny and Monet’s gardens

Monet’s gardens reopen every spring, in early April, and close for the winter. April and May, with the tulips and then the wisteria, are the best months to go from Paris.

May: The Loire Valley châteaux

Before the summer crowds, May is ideal for Chenonceau, Chambord and the gardens of Villandry, by bike or by car, with the long bank-holiday weekends.

June: The Rouen Armada

From 17 to 27 June 2027, the 9th Armada brings tall ships from around the world to the banks of the Seine in Rouen. Entry is free and it is one of the big events of 2027.

July: Lavender in Provence

Lavender usually blooms from late June to mid-July around Gordes, Sénanque Abbey and the Valensole plateau. Come early in the morning for the light and the calm.

August: The Lorient Interceltic Festival and Brittany

The Lorient Interceltic Festival is scheduled from 30 July to 8 August 2027. Combine it with Belle-Île, the Gulf of Morbihan and Carnac, all close by.

September: Corsica

The sea is still warm, hiking becomes pleasant again and prices drop after mid-August: September is the best month for Corsica, from Bonifacio to Cap Corse.

October: The Dordogne and Périgord

Autumn colours over the Dordogne valley, the Sarlat markets and the castles of Beynac and Castelnaud, without the summer traffic jams.

December: The Alsace Christmas markets

Strasbourg, Colmar, Riquewihr, Kaysersberg and Eguisheim: Alsace remains France’s most popular Christmas destination.

Our booking tips

  • Book early for the big events: hotels in Nice, Menton and Rouen fill up months ahead for the Carnival, the Lemon Festival and the Armada.
  • Go in the shoulder seasons: May, June and September bring good weather without the crowds or the July-August prices.
  • Combine nearby regions: the French Riviera and Provence, Normandy and Brittany, or the Basque Country and the Pyrenees are easy to visit in one trip.

Frequently asked questions

What are the big events in France in 2027?

The Monte-Carlo Rally from 21 to 24 January, the Nice Carnival from 9 to 28 February, the Menton Lemon Festival from 13 to 28 February, the Rouen Armada from 17 to 27 June and the Lorient Interceltic Festival from 30 July to 8 August 2027.

What is the best month to visit France?

May, June and September: the weather is good in most regions, with fewer crowds and lower prices than in July and August.

Where to go in France in winter 2027?

The French Riviera for the Nice Carnival and the Lemon Festival in February, the mountains for skiing, or Alsace for the Christmas markets in December.
