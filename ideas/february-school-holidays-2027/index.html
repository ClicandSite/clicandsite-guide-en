February school holidays 2027 in France: dates by zone and where to go

From 6 February to 8 March 2027, the three zones go on holiday one after the other. Here is which week to choose for skiing or for Riviera sunshine.

Photo: Tomas Yates / Unsplash

  2. Ideas
  3. February holidays 2027

French winter school holidays in 2027 last two weeks in each zone and span a month. Zone C goes first, zone B last. The holidays start on Saturday after school and children go back on Monday morning. If you are travelling from abroad, these are the weeks when French families crowd the ski resorts.

Dates by zone

ZoneStartBack to school
Zone C Paris, Créteil, Versailles, Montpellier, ToulouseSaturday 6 February 2027Monday 22 February 2027
Zone A Lyon, Grenoble, Bordeaux, Clermont-Ferrand, Dijon, Besançon, Limoges, PoitiersSaturday 13 February 2027Monday 1 March 2027
Zone B Aix-Marseille, Nice, Lille, Nantes, Rennes, Strasbourg, Normandy, Nancy-Metz, Orléans-Tours, Reims, AmiensSaturday 20 February 2027Monday 8 March 2027

Corsica follows the zone A calendar.

The busiest week

From 13 to 22 February 2027, zones C and A are on holiday at the same time: it is the most expensive and crowded period in the ski resorts, with Saturday traffic jams on the Alpine roads. If you can choose, aim for the first week of zone C (6 to 13 February) or the last week of zone B (27 February to 8 March).

Where to go skiing

All resorts are open in February. To compare lift pass prices, see our 2026-2027 comparison: from €44 a day at Isola 2000 or Auron to €84.20 in the 3 Vallées. The Pyrenees, between €46 and €55, are often less crowded than the big Savoie resorts.

Or French Riviera sunshine

February is the month of the Riviera’s big festivals: the Nice Carnival, the Menton Lemon Festival from 13 to 28 February 2027, and the Mandelieu Mimosa Festival from 10 to 14 February. Zone A, on holiday from 13 February to 1 March, can see them all.

Frequently asked questions

When are the February school holidays in France in 2027?

Zone C: 6 to 22 February 2027. Zone A: 13 February to 1 March 2027. Zone B: 20 February to 8 March 2027.

Which zone goes first in February 2027?

Zone C (Paris, Créteil, Versailles, Montpellier, Toulouse), from Saturday 6 February 2027.

Which is the busiest ski week in February 2027?

13 to 22 February 2027, when zones C and A are on holiday at the same time.
