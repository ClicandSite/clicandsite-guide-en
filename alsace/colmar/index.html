Colmar: Little Venice, the old town and Christmas markets

Canals lined with colourful houses, a largely pedestrian old town and six Christmas markets: Colmar is Alsace’s picture postcard.

Colmar is the capital of Alsace wine, at the foot of the Vosges. Its old town has come through the centuries almost untouched: brightly painted half-timbered houses, Renaissance façades, cobbled lanes and the Little Venice district, where the River Lauch flows between the old market gardeners’ houses.

For Christmas 2026 the town sets up six Christmas markets from 23 November to 29 December, including one just for children. Every evening the lights bring out the old buildings, and Colmar makes an ideal base for the villages of the wine route.

Things to do in Colmar

Little Venice

Colmar’s most photographed spot: colourful houses along the Lauch, best seen from the bridge on Rue de Turenne. Flat-bottomed boats offer short trips on the river.

The Unterlinden Museum

Set in a former convent, it holds the famous Isenheim Altarpiece, one of the great masterpieces of early sixteenth-century painting.

Maison Pfister and Maison des Têtes

Two emblematic Renaissance façades: the first with its wooden gallery and painted panels, the second decorated with dozens of carved faces.

The Koïfhus and Place de l’Ancienne Douane

The old customs house, with its glazed tile roof, overlooks a lively square that hosts one of the Christmas markets.

The Tanners’ Quarter

Tall restored half-timbered houses with open lofts where hides were once dried, a few steps from Little Venice.

How to get to Colmar

By train

Colmar has TGV services from Paris and frequent regional trains from Strasbourg and Mulhouse. The old town is about fifteen minutes’ walk from the station.

By air

The nearest airports are EuroAirport Basel-Mulhouse and Strasbourg-Entzheim, both linked to Colmar by road or rail.

To the villages

During the Christmas season, shuttles link Colmar with nearby villages such as Eguisheim, Kaysersberg and Riquewihr: check the tourist office’s shuttle guide.

Tips for a trip to Colmar

  • On December Saturdays the old town is packed: the tourist office publishes a calendar of expected crowds, handy for choosing your day.
  • Go back to Little Venice after dark: the reflections of the lights on the water are worth a second visit.
  • Stay in Colmar rather than Strasbourg if you also want to see the wine-route villages: they are just a few kilometres away.

Frequently asked questions

When are the Colmar Christmas markets in 2026?

Colmar’s six Christmas markets are open from Monday 23 November to Tuesday 29 December 2026, in the old town.

Colmar or Strasbourg for the Christmas markets?

Strasbourg is bigger and busier; Colmar is more intimate and closer to the wine-route villages. Half an hour apart by train, the two are easy to combine.

How long do you need in Colmar?

One day covers the old town, Little Venice and the Unterlinden Museum. Add a day for the nearby villages.
