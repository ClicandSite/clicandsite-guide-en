Colmar at Christmas: Little Venice lit up

Half-timbered houses, canals and lights on every corner: at Christmas, Colmar looks like a storybook.

Colmar, the capital of Alsace wine, holds several Christmas markets in its old town: Place des Dominicains, Place de l’Ancienne Douane, Place Jeanne-d’Arc, the Koïfhus and Little Venice, plus a food market with a Ferris wheel on Place Rapp.

In 2026 the markets run from Monday 23 November to Tuesday 29 December, with free entry. The food market and Ferris wheel stay open until 3 January 2027. During the markets, the town centre is closed to traffic during opening hours.

Things to do at the Colmar Christmas markets

Little Venice

The children’s market by the River Lauch, and the colourful houses reflected in the water at night.

The Koïfhus

The old customs house hosts an indoor craft market, out of the cold.

Place des Dominicains

At the foot of the church, one of the prettiest markets after dark.

The food market and Ferris wheel

On Place Rapp, for Alsatian specialities and a view over the town.

The illuminations

Every evening the old town is lit up, monument by monument.

How to get to the Colmar Christmas markets

By train

About 30 minutes from Strasbourg by regional train. The station is 15 minutes on foot from the centre.

Christmas shuttles

From 27 November to 20 December 2026, shuttles leave Colmar station for the markets in nearby villages such as Kaysersberg and Riquewihr.

By car

The centre is closed to cars during the markets: use the car parks on the outskirts.

Tips for the Colmar Christmas markets

  • Come on a weekday: at weekends the old town is very crowded.
  • Stay after dark, around 5pm, for the Little Venice lights.
  • Combine with a nearby village, Kaysersberg or Riquewihr, using the Christmas shuttles.

Frequently asked questions

When are the Colmar Christmas markets in 2026?

From Monday 23 November to Tuesday 29 December 2026; the food market and Ferris wheel until 3 January 2027.

What are the opening hours?

Usually 11am to 7pm Monday to Thursday and 10am to 8pm Friday to Sunday. The food market is open 11am to 10pm.

Can you reach other Christmas markets from Colmar without a car?

Yes, Christmas shuttles leave Colmar station from 27 November to 20 December 2026.
