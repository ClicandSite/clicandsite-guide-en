Kaysersberg, the authentic Christmas market

A medieval village below its castle, about thirty craft stalls and a market open only on Advent weekends.

Tucked into the vineyards north-west of Colmar, Kaysersberg is one of the prettiest villages in Alsace, with its fortified bridge, half-timbered houses and castle ruins. Its Christmas market, founded in 1987, focuses on crafts and authenticity.

In 2026 it is open only on Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays over four weekends: 27 to 29 November, 4 to 6, 11 to 13 and 18 to 20 December, from 10am to 8pm. A farmers’ market runs on the same days on Place de la Mairie.

Things to do at the Kaysersberg Christmas market

The craft market

About thirty stalls in the Arsenal courtyard and around the Sainte-Croix church.

The farmers’ market

Local valley producers on Place de la Mairie, on the same days and hours.

The Christmas exhibition

A free exhibition in the Arsenal hall, also open on weekday afternoons until 3 January 2027.

The fortified bridge and castle

The view of the village from the castle ruins, a few minutes’ walk away.

How to get to the Kaysersberg Christmas market

From Colmar

About 20 minutes by car. Christmas shuttles also link Colmar station to the village markets from 27 November to 20 December 2026.

By car

On market weekends, park-and-ride car parks with free shuttles serve the centre.

By bus

Regular buses link Colmar and Kaysersberg.

Tips for the Kaysersberg Christmas market

  • Mind the days: the market only opens Friday to Sunday.
  • Come on Friday or Sunday morning; Saturday is the busiest day.
  • Combine with Riquewihr, about ten minutes away: the two markets complement each other well.

Frequently asked questions

When is the Kaysersberg Christmas market in 2026?

Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays from 27 to 29 November, 4 to 6, 11 to 13 and 18 to 20 December 2026.

What are the opening hours?

10am to 8pm on market days.

Is the Kaysersberg Christmas market open on weekdays?

No, only Friday to Sunday; the Christmas exhibition stays open in the afternoon on other days.
