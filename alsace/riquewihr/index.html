Riquewihr, a storybook village at Christmas

Ramparts, half-timbered houses and stalls all along the main street: at Christmas, Riquewihr is one of the prettiest villages in Alsace.

Surrounded by vineyards at the foot of the Vosges, Riquewihr has kept its ramparts, its Dolder tower and its 16th and 17th-century houses. At Christmas the whole village is decorated and about forty stalls set up in the historic centre.

In 2026 the Christmas market is open every day from Friday 27 November to Sunday 20 December, with free entry, from 10am. On weekdays it usually closes around 6.30pm.

Things to do at the Riquewihr Christmas market

Rue du Général-de-Gaulle

The main street, from the town hall to the Dolder tower, lined with decorated houses.

The Dolder tower

The village’s old fortified gate and its emblem.

Christmas shops

The village has year-round Christmas shops, ideal for decorations.

Wine cellars

Riesling, Gewurztraminer and Crémant d’Alsace: Riquewihr is a major wine village.

How to get to the Riquewihr Christmas market

From Colmar

About 20 minutes by car. From 27 November to 20 December 2026, Christmas shuttles leave Colmar station.

By car

Village car parks are paid. At weekends a park-and-ride with shuttle is available.

From Kaysersberg

About ten minutes by car: easy to do both markets in a day.

Tips for the Riquewihr Christmas market

  • Come on a weekday if you can: the village is small and fills up quickly at weekends.
  • Arrive early in the morning to enjoy the lanes before the coach groups.
  • Try local specialities at village winemakers rather than the busiest stalls.

Frequently asked questions

When is the Riquewihr Christmas market in 2026?

Every day from Friday 27 November to Sunday 20 December 2026.

What are the opening hours?

From 10am; on weekdays it closes around 6.30pm, a little later at weekends.

Is the Riquewihr Christmas market free?

Yes, entry is free. Only parking is paid.
