Eguisheim, the circular village

Half-timbered houses wrapped around a castle, in the middle of the vineyards: one of the most visited villages in Alsace.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 18 November 2026

Eguisheim lies on the Alsace Wine Route, a few kilometres south of Colmar. Its layout is unique: the streets run in rings around the central castle, so you can walk round the village without ever retracing your steps.

Listed among the most beautiful villages of France, it was voted France’s favourite village in 2013. The village is free to explore on foot, all year round.

A bit of history

Eguisheim was the seat of the Counts of Eguisheim. One of them, Bruno, born in 1002, became Pope Leo IX; his statue stands on the fountain in the square. The vineyards are old: two grands crus, Eichberg and Pfersigberg, ripen on the slopes around the village.

What to see in Eguisheim

The castle square

The heart of the village, with the Saint-Léon chapel and the fountain topped by the statue of Pope Leo IX.

The ramparts walk

A lane circles the village along the old walls, between flower-decked houses and winegrowers’ courtyards.

The three towers

On the hill above the village, the ruins of three keeps can be seen from across the plain.

Winegrowers’ cellars

Many estates open their cellars for tastings.

How to get to Eguisheim

By car

About fifteen minutes from Colmar; paid car parks are set up at the entrance to the village.

By train

The nearest station is Colmar, served by TGV and regional trains; finish by taxi or bike.

On a tour

Many day trips from Colmar and Strasbourg stop in Eguisheim.

Practical information 2026: Eguisheim

  • Access: free, all year round.
  • Crowds: heavy in summer and during the Christmas market; come early in the morning.
  • Time needed: one to two hours for the village, half a day with the towers and a tasting.

Our tips for visiting Eguisheim

  • Arrive before 10 am to enjoy the lanes without the crowds.
  • Walk up to the three towers through the vineyards for the view over the Alsace plain.
  • Combine the visit with Colmar, Riquewihr or Kaysersberg on the same day.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Eguisheim?

On the Alsace Wine Route, a few kilometres south of Colmar, in the Haut-Rhin.

Why is Eguisheim famous?

For its lanes laid out in rings around the castle, its flower-decked half-timbered houses and its title of France’s favourite village in 2013.

Do you have to pay to visit Eguisheim?

No, the village is free to explore; only the car parks charge.
