Strasbourg: Christmas market, cathedral and Petite France

A pink sandstone cathedral, half-timbered quaysides and France’s most famous Christmas market: Strasbourg is best explored on foot over one or two days.

Strasbourg’s historic centre, the Grande Île, is ringed by the arms of the River Ill. Within minutes you walk from the cathedral to the old tanners’ houses of Petite France, then on to the broad avenues of the Neustadt, the district built during the German period. The whole ensemble is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

For 2026 the Christmas market, the Christkindelsmärik, runs from 27 November to 24 December. The stalls are spread across several squares of the Grande Île, with the Great Christmas Tree on Place Kléber and an ice rink in the courtyard of the Palais Rohan.

Things to do in Strasbourg

Strasbourg Cathedral

A Gothic masterpiece in pink Vosges sandstone, with its single spire and astronomical clock. A platform, reached by a long staircase, looks out over the rooftops and, on a clear day, the Black Forest.

Petite France

The old millers’ and tanners’ quarter, with half-timbered houses on the water and swing bridges. It is the loveliest part of town for a walk at dusk.

The Ponts Couverts and the Vauban Dam

The medieval towers that once guarded the city’s entrance, then the Vauban Dam, whose rooftop terrace has a fine view over Petite France.

The Christkindelsmärik and the Great Tree

The historic market on Place Broglie and the huge decorated tree on Place Kléber, two essential stops between late November and Christmas.

Palais Rohan

The former palace of the prince-bishops, next to the cathedral, houses three museums: fine arts, decorative arts and archaeology.

The Neustadt

The grand buildings of Place de la République, the university and streets of neo-Renaissance and Art Nouveau houses show a very different side of Strasbourg.

How to get to Strasbourg

By train

The TGV links Paris Gare de l’Est with Strasbourg in under two hours. From the station, the cathedral is about twenty minutes on foot or a few stops by tram.

By air

Strasbourg-Entzheim airport has a regional train link to the main station. EuroAirport Basel-Mulhouse, further south, is another option.

By car

During the Christmas market, traffic is heavily restricted on the Grande Île: park at a Park and Ride (P+R) on the outskirts and finish by tram.

Tips for a trip to Strasbourg

  • December weekends are very busy in the centre: visit the markets on a weekday or late in the morning if you can.
  • Climb the cathedral platform early in the day and save Petite France for late afternoon, when the lights come on.
  • Book accommodation very early for Advent weekends, or stay in Colmar or in Kehl, across the Rhine, both linked by train or tram.

Frequently asked questions

When is the Strasbourg Christmas market in 2026?

From Friday 27 November to Thursday 24 December 2026, across several squares of the Grande Île around the cathedral and Place Kléber.

How long do you need in Strasbourg?

One day is enough for the cathedral, Petite France and the markets. Allow two days to add the museums, the Neustadt and a river cruise.

Can you visit Strasbourg without a car?

Yes, and it is the best way: the city is under two hours from Paris by TGV and the centre is easy to explore on foot or by tram.
