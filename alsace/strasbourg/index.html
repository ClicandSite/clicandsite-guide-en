Strasbourg, the capital of Christmas

Chalets on every square, the giant tree on Place Kléber and the illuminated cathedral: France’s oldest Christmas market draws visitors from all over the world.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 12 November 2026

The Christkindelsmärik on Place Broglie dates back to 1570, making it the oldest Christmas market in France. Today Strasbourg has about ten markets spread across the Grande Île, the UNESCO-listed historic centre, from the cathedral to the Petite France.

In 2026 the Christmas market opens on Friday 27 November and runs until Christmas Eve, with free entry. Stalls are usually open from 11.30am to 9pm, opening in the afternoon on the first day and closing in the late afternoon on 24 December.

Things to do at the Strasbourg Christmas market

The Christkindelsmärik, Place Broglie

The historic market and the largest in town, with decorations, bredele biscuits and mulled wine.

The giant tree on Place Kléber

A huge decorated tree in the heart of the city, lit until early January.

Notre-Dame Cathedral

The market at the foot of the Gothic façade, then the cathedral itself, lit up in the evening.

The Petite France

Half-timbered houses on the water, covered bridges and quieter stalls than in the centre.

Local treats

Bredele (Christmas biscuits), gingerbread, pretzels and white or red mulled wine, served in a returnable cup.

How to get to the Strasbourg Christmas market

By train

Direct TGV from Paris in about 1 hour 50 minutes. The station is 15 minutes on foot from Place Kléber.

By tram

Lines A and D to Homme de Fer, in the middle of the markets.

By car

The centre is closed to traffic during the markets: use a tram park-and-ride (P+R) on the outskirts.

Tips for the Strasbourg Christmas market

  • Come on a weekday and during the day: on Saturday afternoons the centre is packed.
  • Book your hotel very early; rooms for December weekends go from the summer.
  • Take a boat trip on the Ill to see the city lit up without the crowds.

Frequently asked questions

When does the Strasbourg Christmas market open in 2026?

On Friday 27 November 2026, until Christmas Eve.

What are the opening hours?

Usually 11.30am to 9pm; 2pm to 9pm on opening day and until about 6pm on 24 December.

Is the Strasbourg Christmas market free?

Yes, all the markets are free to enter.
