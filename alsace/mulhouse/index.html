Mulhouse, the city of technical museums

Mulhouse is the second city of Alsace, in the south of the region, close to Switzerland and Germany. It is best known for its large technical museums, among the most visited in eastern France.

The old centre, around Place de la Réunion, can be explored on foot in an hour or two.

A bit of history

For a long time a small independent republic allied to the Swiss cantons, Mulhouse only joined France in 1798. In the 19th century it became a major textile city, which explains its museums devoted to industry and transport.

What to see in Mulhouse

The National Automobile Museum

The Schlumpf collection, housed in a former spinning mill: one of the largest car collections in the world, including many Bugattis.

Cité du Train

The SNCF heritage museum, with steam locomotives, historic carriages and presidential coaches.

Place de la Réunion

The Renaissance town hall with its painted façades and the Saint-Étienne church.

The zoo and botanical park

A large zoo in a wooded park on the hills above the city.

How to get to Mulhouse

By train

Mulhouse is served by TGV from Paris and by regional trains from Strasbourg and Colmar.

By plane

EuroAirport Basel-Mulhouse is about thirty kilometres away.

Getting around

Tram line 3, stop “Musées”, serves the Cité du Train.

Practical information 2026: Mulhouse

  • Automobile museum: €19 full price; open every day except 25 December.
  • Cité du Train: €16 full price; 10 am to 6 pm from April to October, 10 am to 5 pm the rest of the year.
  • City centre: free.

Information checked in November 2026; prices and hours may change, check the museums’ websites before you go.

Our tips for visiting Mulhouse

  • Allow at least two hours for each museum.
  • Visit the Cité du Train in the morning and the car museum in the afternoon.
  • Combine Mulhouse with Colmar, about twenty minutes away by train.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the Mulhouse car museum?

€19 full price in 2026; the museum is open every day except 25 December.

How long should you spend at the Cité du Train?

Allow at least two hours to see the collections.

Can you visit Mulhouse in a day?

Yes: one museum in the morning, the city centre and a second museum in the afternoon.
