Haut-Kœnigsbourg Castle

A pink sandstone fortress perched 757 metres up, above the Alsace plain and the Wine Route.

Haut-Kœnigsbourg looks down on the Alsace plain from its rocky spur, 757 metres up, in the village of Orschwiller. It is one of the most visited monuments in the region.

Inside is a fully furnished fortress, with its kitchens, knights’ hall, armoury and, on a clear day, a view as far as the Black Forest.

A bit of history

Built in the 12th century, the castle was burned during the Thirty Years’ War and left in ruins. When Alsace was German, Emperor Wilhelm II had it rebuilt by the architect Bodo Ebhardt; the restored castle was inaugurated in 1908.

What to see at Haut-Kœnigsbourg

The great bastion

An artillery platform with the best view over the Alsace plain.

The Kaiser’s hall

The great hall decorated for Wilhelm II, with its paintings and ceiling.

The armoury

A collection of weapons and armour in the knights’ hall.

The medieval garden

A small garden of plants grown in the Middle Ages, below the ramparts.

How to get to Haut-Kœnigsbourg

By car

About 26 km from Colmar and 55 km from Strasbourg; the car parks along the access road fill up quickly in summer.

By shuttle

In season, a shuttle bus links Sélestat station with the castle.

On a tour

Tours from Strasbourg and Colmar combine the castle with the Wine Route villages.

Practical information 2026: Haut-Kœnigsbourg

  • Prices: €12 for adults, €8 for ages 6 to 17, free under 6.
  • Opening: all year except 1 January, 1 May and 25 December; hours vary with the season.
  • Tickets: buying online is recommended at busy times.
  • Time needed: about 1 hour 30 for a self-guided visit.

Information checked in November 2026 with the Sélestat and Colmar tourist offices; it may change, check before you go.

Our tips for visiting Haut-Kœnigsbourg

  • Come at opening time or late in the day to avoid the tour groups.
  • Bring a jacket: it is often cooler than down on the plain.
  • Combine the visit with the Volerie des Aigles or the Montagne des Singes nearby.
