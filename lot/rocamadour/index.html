Rocamadour, the holy town clinging to its cliff

A medieval village, sanctuaries carved into the rock and a castle on top: Rocamadour rises on three levels above the Alzou canyon.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 13 November 2026

Rocamadour clings to a limestone cliff on the Causse de Gramat, above the Alzou canyon in the Lot. A major pilgrimage site since the Middle Ages, it is one of the most visited places in south-west France.

The town spreads over three levels: the main street and its shops at the bottom, the sanctuaries in the middle and the castle with its ramparts at the top. Allow at least two hours for the town and sanctuaries.

A bit of history

According to tradition, in 1166 the intact body of a hermit, Saint Amadour, was found in the cliff, giving the place its name. Miracles attributed to the Black Madonna then made Rocamadour one of the great pilgrimages of Christendom, on the road to Santiago de Compostela.

Kings and pilgrims came in succession during the Middle Ages, and the town grew in tiers up the rock face. Looted during the Wars of Religion, the sanctuaries were restored in the 19th century, and the site is a UNESCO World Heritage Site as part of the Routes of Santiago de Compostela.

Things to see in Rocamadour

The Grand Staircase

Its 216 steps link the town to the sanctuaries; pilgrims once climbed them on their knees. See tours and trips in Rocamadour

The sanctuaries and the Black Madonna

On the square stand the Basilica of Saint-Sauveur, the crypt of Saint-Amadour and several chapels under the rock, including the Notre-Dame chapel and its Black Madonna.

The castle ramparts

The remains of a 14th-century fort, they offer the finest view over the site and the Alzou canyon.

L’Hospitalet

The hamlet on the edge of the plateau has the most famous view of Rocamadour, especially late in the day.

Rocher des Aigles and Forêt des Singes

Two family outings right next door: a birds of prey park and a park of free-roaming macaques.

How to get to Rocamadour

By car

About 1 hour from Sarlat and 25 minutes from the Gouffre de Padirac. Car parks at L’Hospitalet, by the castle and below the town.

By train

Rocamadour-Padirac station is a few kilometres away; you then need a taxi.

Lifts

To skip the steps, a lift links the town to the sanctuaries and an inclined lift climbs from the sanctuaries to the castle (both paid).

Practical info 2026: Rocamadour

  • Sanctuaries: free access to the square and chapels.
  • Castle ramparts: about €2 entry.
  • Lifts: paid, sometimes with queues in high season.

Tips for visiting Rocamadour

  • Come early in the morning or after 6 pm in summer to avoid the crowds.
  • Park at L’Hospitalet and walk down to enjoy the view.
  • Wear good shoes: the steps are slippery in the rain.

Frequently asked questions

How many steps does the Grand Staircase in Rocamadour have?

216 steps between the town and the sanctuary square; lifts let you avoid them.

How long does it take to visit Rocamadour?

Allow at least two hours for the town and sanctuaries, half a day with the ramparts and L’Hospitalet.
