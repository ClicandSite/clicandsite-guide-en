The Gouffre de Padirac, a river 103 metres underground

A giant shaft 35 metres wide, an underground river explored by boat and chambers with 94-metre vaults: the most spectacular visit in the Lot.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 13 November 2026

The Gouffre de Padirac opens onto the Causse de Gramat in the Lot, about twenty kilometres from Rocamadour. Its entrance shaft, some 35 metres wide, leads to an underground river flowing 103 metres below the surface.

The visit takes about 1 h 30 with an audio guide: down on foot or by lift, a walk through the galleries, a boat trip, then up to the Grand Dôme chamber. It is cool underground all year, so bring a jumper.

A bit of history

For centuries the people of the plateau feared this great hole, which legend said had been dug by the devil. On 9 July 1889 the explorer Édouard-Alfred Martel, seen as the father of modern caving, went down with his team and discovered the underground river, which he followed by canoe.

The chasm was opened to the public a few years later, at the end of the 19th century. More than 26 million visitors have explored it since, and cave divers are still mapping its galleries.

Things to see at the Gouffre de Padirac

The great shaft

The descent into this chasm open to the sky and covered in greenery is a sight in itself. See tickets and trips for Padirac

The Rivière Plane by boat

About 500 metres on crystal-clear water, steered by boatmen.

Lac de la Pluie and the Grande Pendeloque

A giant stalactite some 60 metres long hangs above the lake.

The Grand Dôme

An underground cathedral with a 94-metre vault, its rimstone pools and “stacks of plates”.

Rocamadour nearby

The holy town is about 25 minutes by car: both visits fit easily into one day.

How to get to the Gouffre de Padirac

By car

About 25 minutes from Rocamadour and 1 h 15 from Sarlat; large car park on site.

By train

Rocamadour-Padirac is the nearest station; you then need a taxi.

On a tour

Day trips combine Padirac and Rocamadour.

Tips for visiting the Gouffre de Padirac

  • Book your slot ahead in July and August.
  • Bring a jumper: it is cool underground, even in midsummer.
  • Combine with Rocamadour on the same day.
