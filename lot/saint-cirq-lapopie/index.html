Saint-Cirq-Lapopie overlooks the Lot valley about thirty kilometres east of Cahors. Listed among the Most Beautiful Villages of France, it was voted the French people’s favourite village in 2012.

The village takes one to two hours on foot, climbing to the castle ruins for the view of the river. It is at its best early in the morning or late in the day, once the coaches have left.

A bit of history

In the Middle Ages the village was shared between several noble families who controlled the river and collected tolls from boats. Wood turners, tanners and coppersmiths made it prosperous, using the Lot as a trade route.

After a long decline, Saint-Cirq-Lapopie was rediscovered in the 20th century by painters, then by André Breton, who stayed there from 1950. The village is now listed among the Most Beautiful Villages of France.

Things to see in Saint-Cirq-Lapopie

The medieval lanes

Steep cobbled streets, half-timbered houses and craft workshops. See tours around Saint-Cirq-Lapopie

The fortified church

Built on the rock, it towers over the village and has a fine view of the valley.

The Rocher de la Popie

At the top, the castle remains offer the widest view over the Lot.

André Breton’s house

The leader of the Surrealists fell in love with the village in 1950 and spent his summers there.

The towpath

A path cut into the cliff follows the river towards Bouziès, a lovely walk.

How to get to Saint-Cirq-Lapopie

By car

About 30 minutes from Cahors and 1 h 15 from Rocamadour; paid car parks at the entrance to the village.

By train

The nearest station is Cahors; you then need a car or taxi.

On foot

Along the towpath from Bouziès, by the Lot.

Tips for visiting Saint-Cirq-Lapopie

  • Arrive before 10 am or after 5 pm in summer.
  • Climb to the Rocher de la Popie for the view.
  • Wear comfortable shoes, the lanes are steep.

Frequently asked questions

Do you have to pay to visit Saint-Cirq-Lapopie?

No, the village is free to visit; only the car parks are paid.

How long does it take to visit Saint-Cirq-Lapopie?

Allow one to two hours for the lanes and the climb to the Rocher de la Popie.
