Mont-Saint-Michel and its abbey

A Benedictine abbey on a granite islet, surrounded by Europe’s biggest tides: the “wonder of the West” can be visited all year round.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 12 November 2026

Set on the border between Normandy and Brittany, Mont-Saint-Michel was one of the first sites listed by UNESCO. The village, its ramparts and its Grande Rue are free to visit; only the abbey at the top has a fee.

From October to April, the abbey is open daily from 9.30am to 6pm, with last entry an hour before closing. It is the best time to avoid the summer crowds, when the Mont welcomes tens of thousands of visitors a day.

What to see at Mont-Saint-Michel

The abbey

The abbey church, the hanging cloister and La Merveille, a Gothic complex on three levels.

The Grande Rue

The village’s main street, lined with old houses, shops and restaurants.

The ramparts

The wall walk gives the best views of the bay, especially as the tide comes in.

The bay

Guided walks across the sands at low tide let you discover the quicksand safely.

How to get to Mont-Saint-Michel

By car

Paid parking on the mainland, then the free “Le Passeur” shuttle or a 45-minute walk to the Mont.

By train

Train to Rennes or Pontorson, then a coach to the Mont.

On a day trip

Day tours leave from Paris, with transport and often the abbey ticket.

Tips for visiting Mont-Saint-Michel

  • Arrive before 10am or late in the afternoon to avoid the crowds in the Grande Rue.
  • Check the tide times: the incoming tide around the Mont is a sight to see.
  • Never venture into the bay alone: the quicksand and the tide are dangerous.
  • On the first Sunday of the month from November to March, the abbey is free.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a ticket to the abbey?

In 2026 a ticket costs €13 in low season and €16 in high season. The village and ramparts are free. The abbey is free for under-18s and for EU residents aged 18 to 25.

What are the abbey’s opening hours?

September to April, 9.30am to 6pm; May to August, 9am to 7pm. Last entry an hour before closing.

Can I visit Mont-Saint-Michel as a day trip from Paris?

Yes, but it is a long day: allow about 4.5 hours’ drive each way. An organised tour saves you the driving.
