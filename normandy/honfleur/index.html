Honfleur, the painters’ harbour

Narrow slate-fronted houses, sailing boats in the Vieux Bassin and France’s largest wooden church: Honfleur inspired the Impressionists.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 12 November 2026

At the mouth of the Seine, opposite Le Havre, Honfleur owes its fame to the Vieux Bassin, dug in 1681 under Colbert and lined with tall, narrow houses. Eugène Boudin, born here, taught a young Claude Monet to paint outdoors.

Behind the harbour, the church of Sainte-Catherine, built in the 15th century by shipwrights, is the largest wooden church in France. Its bell tower, which stands apart on the square, is included in the Eugène Boudin museum ticket.

What to see in Honfleur

The Vieux Bassin

The harbour, its sailing boats and Quai Sainte-Catherine, the most photographed quay in Normandy.

Sainte-Catherine church

A nave shaped like two upturned boat hulls, built entirely of wood.

The Eugène Boudin museum

Boudin, Monet and the painters of the estuary, with access to the Sainte-Catherine bell tower.

La Lieutenance and the salt stores

Remains of the old fortified town around the harbour.

How to get to Honfleur

By car

About 2.5 hours from Paris on the A13; paid car parks at the edge of town.

By coach

There is no station in Honfleur: coaches run from Le Havre or Lisieux.

On a day trip

Day tours from Paris combine Honfleur with Étretat or Rouen.

Tips for visiting Honfleur

  • Come early in the morning to photograph the Vieux Bassin without the crowds.
  • On Saturday mornings the market sets up around Sainte-Catherine church.
  • Walk up to Mont-Joli for a view over the estuary and the Pont de Normandie.
  • Combine it with Deauville or Étretat, less than an hour’s drive away.

Frequently asked questions

What can I see in Honfleur in a day?

The Vieux Bassin, Sainte-Catherine church and its bell tower, the Eugène Boudin museum, then a walk up to Mont-Joli.

Can I get to Honfleur by train?

No, there is no station. Take the train to Le Havre or Lisieux, then a coach.

Is Sainte-Catherine church free?

Yes, the church is free to visit. The bell tower is included in the Eugène Boudin museum ticket.
