Trouville-sur-Mer faces Deauville across the Touques river. Older and more down-to-earth than its neighbour, the resort has kept a working fishing port and a lively fish market.

Its long sandy beach, lined with villas, drew painters from the 19th century onwards, including Eugène Boudin and Claude Monet.

A bit of history

A simple fishing village, Trouville became one of the first seaside resorts in France in the mid-19th century. The Hôtel des Roches Noires, painted by Monet in 1870, was later home to the writer Marguerite Duras.

What to see in Trouville

The fish market

Built in 1936 and rebuilt to the same design after a fire in 2006; buy and eat fish and seafood here.

The beach and promenade

A long sandy beach lined with villas and beach huts.

The villas

Belle Époque houses in timber or brick on the hillside.

Pont des Belges

The bridge between Trouville and Deauville, a few minutes on foot.

How to get to Trouville

By train

Trouville-Deauville station is about two hours from Paris-Saint-Lazare.

By car

About two hours from Paris on the A13.

On foot

The station is on the Touques, a few minutes from the port.

Practical information 2026: Trouville

  • Fish market: the fishmongers are open every day of the year, 9 am to 7 pm.
  • Beach: free access.
  • Crowds: busy at weekends, especially in summer; parking is paid.

Information checked in November 2026 with the Trouville tourist office; it may change, check before you go.

Our tips for visiting Trouville

  • Come by train: the station is shared by Trouville and Deauville.
  • Have a seafood platter for lunch at the fish market.
  • Cross Pont des Belges to see Deauville on the same day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Trouville and Deauville?

Trouville is a real fishing port, older and more down-to-earth; Deauville is smarter, with its boardwalk and grand hotels. A bridge links the two.

Is the Trouville fish market open every day?

Yes, the fishmongers are open every day of the year, 9 am to 7 pm.

How do you get to Trouville without a car?

By train from Paris-Saint-Lazare to Trouville-Deauville station, in about two hours.
