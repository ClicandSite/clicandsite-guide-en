Rouen, capital of Normandy

Monet’s cathedral, the Gros-Horloge and hundreds of half-timbered houses, 1 hour 20 from Paris by train.

Rouen is Normandy’s finest city of art. Its Notre-Dame cathedral, a Gothic masterpiece, was painted some thirty times by Claude Monet, and its 151-metre spire is the tallest in France.

Around it, the historic centre is a maze of half-timbered streets, the Gros-Horloge, an astronomical clock spanning the street, and Place du Vieux-Marché, where Joan of Arc was burned in 1431. In 2026 the Christmas market fills Place de la Cathédrale from 25 November to 27 December.

What to see in Rouen

Notre-Dame cathedral

The Gothic façade painted by Monet and its cast-iron spire, the tallest in France.

The Gros-Horloge

A Renaissance clock face on an arch over the street; the belfry gives a view over the rooftops.

Place du Vieux-Marché

Where Joan of Arc was burned, with the modern church dedicated to her.

The Historial Jeanne d’Arc

An immersive visit on Joan’s trial, in the former archbishop’s palace.

How to get to Rouen

By train

From Paris Saint-Lazare, about 1 hour 20 to Rouen Rive-Droite.

On foot

The historic centre is easy to explore on foot from the station, 15 minutes from the cathedral.

By car

About 1.5 hours from Paris on the A13; underground car parks near the cathedral.

Tips for visiting Rouen

  • Come on a weekday for the Christmas market: December Saturdays are very busy.
  • In summer, don’t miss the evening light show projected onto the cathedral façade.
  • The Museum of Fine Arts has a fine Impressionist collection.
  • Rouen combines easily with Giverny, Honfleur or Étretat.

Frequently asked questions

When is the Rouen Christmas market in 2026?

From Wednesday 25 November to Sunday 27 December 2026, on Place de la Cathédrale, with about fifty stalls.

How do I get to Rouen from Paris?

By train from Gare Saint-Lazare, in about 1 hour 20.

What can I see in Rouen in a day?

The cathedral, the Gros-Horloge, Rue du Gros-Horloge, Place du Vieux-Marché and Saint-Maclou church.
