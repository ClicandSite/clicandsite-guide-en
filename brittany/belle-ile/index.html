Belle-Île-en-Mer is the largest of the Breton islands, off the Quiberon peninsula. You arrive at Le Palais, the small capital, beneath its citadel.

The island is best explored by car, bike or on foot along the coastal path: a wild coast to the west, sheltered beaches to the east and small harbours like Sauzon.

A bit of history

The citadel at Le Palais was strengthened by Vauban in the late 17th century. In 1886 Claude Monet spent several weeks on the island and painted a series of the Port-Coton needles. The actress Sarah Bernhardt later settled at Pointe des Poulains.

What to see on Belle-Île

The Port-Coton needles

Sharp rocks pounded by the waves on the wild coast, made famous by Monet.

Sauzon

A small harbour with colourful houses, in the north of the island.

The Vauban citadel

The fortress overlooking the port of Le Palais.

Pointe des Poulains

The lighthouse and Sarah Bernhardt’s house, facing the ocean.

How to get to Belle-Île

By ferry

BreizhGo Océane links Quiberon and Le Palais all year, in about 50 minutes, with up to 20 return trips a day in July and August.

In season

Shuttles also run to Sauzon from Quiberon in summer.

On the island

Hire a bike, car or scooter in Le Palais; there are also buses and coach tours.

Practical information 2026: Belle-Île-en-Mer

  • Crossing: about 50 minutes from Quiberon to Le Palais; book in summer, especially with a car.
  • Time needed: a day for the highlights, several days to walk round the island.
  • Safety: on the wild coast, stay on the path: the cliffs are dangerous.

Information checked in November 2026 with the Belle-Île tourist office; it may change, check before you go.

Our tips for visiting Belle-Île

  • Take the first morning ferry for a day trip.
  • Book a car crossing several weeks ahead in summer.
  • Go to Port-Coton late in the day, when the light is at its best.

Frequently asked questions

How do you get to Belle-Île-en-Mer?

By ferry from Quiberon to Le Palais, in about 50 minutes, all year round.

Can you visit Belle-Île in a day?

Yes, if you leave early and hire a car or e-bike on the island.

What is there to see on Belle-Île?

The Port-Coton needles, Sauzon, the citadel at Le Palais and Pointe des Poulains.
