The Quiberon peninsula

A wild coast battered by the Atlantic on one side, sheltered beaches on the other, and the boats to Belle-Île.

The Quiberon peninsula juts into the Atlantic in the south of the Morbihan, joined to the mainland by a narrow strip of sand, the Penthièvre isthmus.

Its western side, the wild coast (Côte sauvage), alternates cliffs, arches and coves under the waves. To the east, Quiberon bay has fine sandy beaches and calmer water.

What to see in Quiberon

The wild coast

A road and a footpath follow the west coast, among rocks, arches and coves. Swimming there is dangerous.

The bay beaches

Quiberon’s main beach and the sheltered eastern beaches, ideal for families.

Port-Maria and Port-Haliguen

The fishing and sailing harbours, where boats leave for the islands.

The canneries

Quiberon is the town of tinned sardines: several canneries have shops and offer visits.

Belle-Île, Houat and Hoëdic

The offshore islands, less than an hour away by boat.

How to get to Quiberon

By train

In summer, the “Tire-Bouchon” train links Auray and Quiberon; the rest of the year, coaches run the route.

By car

Along the peninsula road from Auray; it is very busy on summer weekends.

To the islands

Boats to Belle-Île all year from Port-Maria, and to Houat and Hoëdic.

Practical info 2026: Quiberon

  • Wild coast: free access; swimming is dangerous and often banned.
  • Bay beaches: lifeguards in summer.
  • Boats: book ahead in July and August.

Information checked in November 2026; it may change, so check before you go.

Tips for visiting Quiberon

  • Do not swim on the wild coast: the currents are violent.
  • Go and see the wild coast on a stormy day, from a safe distance.
  • Come by train in summer to avoid traffic on the isthmus.
  • Allow a day for Belle-Île.

Frequently asked questions

Can you swim on the Quiberon wild coast?

No, it is very dangerous: currents and waves are violent. Swim on the bay beaches to the east.

How do you get to Belle-Île from Quiberon?

By boat from Port-Maria, all year, in just under an hour.

How do you get to Quiberon without a car?

In summer, on the Tire-Bouchon train from Auray; the rest of the year, by coach.
