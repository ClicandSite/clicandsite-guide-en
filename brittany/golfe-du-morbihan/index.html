The Gulf of Morbihan, the little sea

An inland sea dotted with islands, harbours and megaliths, between Vannes and the Rhuys peninsula.

The Gulf of Morbihan is an inland sea joined to the ocean by a narrow channel between Locmariaquer and Port-Navalo. Its name comes from the Breton “mor bihan”, the little sea.

It has about forty islands and islets, including Île aux Moines and Île d’Arz, the two largest. You can explore it by boat, by bike or on foot along the coastal path.

A bit of history

The shores of the gulf are covered with Neolithic monuments. The most famous, the Gavrinis cairn on a small island, was built around 3500 BC; its stones are covered with carvings.

What to see in the Gulf of Morbihan

Île aux Moines

The largest island in the gulf, with its paths, beaches and flower-filled houses.

Île d’Arz

A quieter island to explore by bike or on foot.

The Gavrinis cairn

A carved megalithic tomb, reached by boat from Larmor-Baden, on guided tours only.

Vannes

The walled old town, its timber-framed houses and its harbour.

How to explore the gulf

On a cruise

Companies run gulf tours with stops from Vannes, Port-Navalo and Locmariaquer.

By shuttle boat

Regular boats serve Île aux Moines from Port-Blanc and Île d’Arz from Vannes.

By train

Vannes is about 2 hours 30 from Paris by TGV.

Practical information 2026: the Gulf of Morbihan

  • Gavrinis: a crossing of about 15 minutes from Larmor-Baden; guided tours only, booking advised.
  • Cruises: most run from April to September.
  • Islands: free access; visitors’ cars are not allowed on Île aux Moines.

Information checked in November 2026 with Morbihan Tourisme; it may change, check before you go.

Our tips for the Gulf of Morbihan

  • Book Gavrinis ahead: places are limited.
  • Choose a cruise with a stop on Île aux Moines so you can walk a little.
  • Check the tide times: the gulf’s currents are among the strongest in Europe.

Frequently asked questions

How many islands are there in the Gulf of Morbihan?

About forty islands and islets; the two largest are Île aux Moines and Île d’Arz.

How do you visit the Gavrinis cairn?

By boat from Larmor-Baden, in about 15 minutes, with a compulsory guided tour.

What is the best way to see the gulf?

A cruise with a stop on an island, then a walk on the coastal path.
