Rennes is the capital of Brittany and a big student city, about 1 hour 30 from Paris by TGV. Its largely pedestrian historic centre mixes medieval timber-framed houses and classical granite façades.

It is also a good gateway to Saint-Malo, Dinan and Mont-Saint-Michel, about an hour’s drive away.

A bit of history

In 1720 a huge fire destroyed much of the city; the north-western district that was spared has kept its timber-framed houses. The Brittany Parliament palace, completed in the 17th century, burned during a demonstration in 1994 and was then restored; it houses the court of appeal.

What to see in Rennes

The Brittany Parliament

A palace with painted and gilded ceilings, open only on guided tours.

The timber-framed houses

Around Place du Champ-Jacquet, Place Sainte-Anne and Rue du Chapitre.

The Lices market

Every Saturday morning, one of the largest markets in France, in the halls and on the square.

Thabor park

A large formal garden with a rose garden and a botanical garden.

How to get to Rennes

By train

About 1 hour 30 from Paris-Montparnasse by TGV.

Getting around

Two metro lines serve the station and the centre.

By car

Use the park-and-ride car parks at the ends of the metro lines.

Our tips for visiting Rennes

  • Come on a Saturday for the Lices market.
  • Book the Parliament tour several days ahead.
  • Use Rennes as a base for Saint-Malo and Mont-Saint-Michel.

Frequently asked questions

Can you visit the Brittany Parliament on your own?

No, only on a guided tour booked through the Rennes tourist office.

What day is the Lices market?

Saturday morning.

How do you get from Paris to Rennes?
