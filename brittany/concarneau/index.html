Concarneau, the walled town in the middle of the harbour

A little walled town set on an islet in the middle of a fishing port: Concarneau is one of the most striking sights in southern Finistère.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 12 November 2026

Concarneau, on the Baie de la Forêt, is one of France’s major fishing ports. Its historic heart, the Ville Close, occupies a rocky islet in the middle of the harbour, completely enclosed by ramparts remodelled by Vauban.

You enter by a bridge and a fortified gate, to wander the main street, walk the ramparts and learn about fishing at the dedicated museum.

Things to see in Concarneau

The Ville Close

The fortified islet in the middle of the harbour, with its lanes, shops and crêperies. See guided tours and things to do in Concarneau

The wall walk

A walk along the ramparts, with views over the harbour and the bay.

The Fishing Museum

The history of fishing in Concarneau, with a real trawler to visit at the quay.

The fishing port

Lively quays, trawlers and the fish auction, one of France’s major fishing ports.

The Marinarium

The marine biology station and its aquariums devoted to local sea life.

How to get to Concarneau

From Quimper

Half an hour by car or bus; the nearest TGV stations are Quimper and Rosporden.

Getting around

A small ferry crosses the harbour between the Ville Close and the opposite bank.

Practical info 2026: Concarneau

  • Ville Close: free access all year round.
  • Fête des Filets Bleus: a big Breton festival every August.

Tips for visiting Concarneau

  • Walk the ramparts along the wall walk for the best view of the harbour.
  • Take the little ferry to see the Ville Close from the other bank.
  • Combine with Quimper and Pont-Aven on the same day.

Frequently asked questions

What is there to see in Concarneau?

The Ville Close and its ramparts, the Fishing Museum, the fishing port and the Marinarium.

Is the Ville Close free?

Yes, you can enter the Ville Close freely all year.

How do I get to Concarneau?

By car or bus from Quimper, about half an hour away.
