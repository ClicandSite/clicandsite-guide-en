Cancale, the oyster capital

Oysters eaten by the sea, a fishing harbour and a wild headland, a quarter of an hour from Saint-Malo.

Cancale lies about fifteen kilometres east of Saint-Malo, facing the bay of Mont-Saint-Michel. The town is famous for its oysters, grown in beds that appear at low tide.

The town centre is on the hill; La Houle harbour below is lined with restaurants and the little oyster market.

A bit of history

Cancale oysters have been famous for centuries. The town has been listed as a “site remarquable du goût” for its oysters, and the oyster washers fountain in the town centre recalls the work of the harbour’s women.

What to see in Cancale

The oyster market

At the end of the harbour, below La Houle lighthouse: growers sell their oysters to take away or eat on the spot facing the sea.

La Houle harbour

The quays, fishing boats and sailors’ houses.

Pointe du Grouin

A wild headland with views of Île des Landes, Chausey and, on a clear day, Mont-Saint-Michel.

The GR34

The coastal path links Cancale with Pointe du Grouin and Saint-Malo.

How to get to Cancale

By car

About twenty minutes from Saint-Malo.

By bus

Buses link Saint-Malo and Cancale.

On foot

Along the GR34 from Pointe du Grouin, or from Saint-Malo for keen walkers.

Practical information 2026: Cancale

  • Oyster market: open every day of the year, during the day.
  • Tides: the oyster beds can only be seen at low tide.
  • Parking: hard at the harbour in summer; park in the town centre and walk down.

Information checked in November 2026 with Brittany Tourism and the Saint-Malo tourist office; it may change, check before you go.

Our tips for visiting Cancale

  • Eat your oysters on the harbour steps and throw the shells onto the shore, as tradition has it.
  • Come at low tide to see the oyster beds.
  • Combine Cancale with Saint-Malo on the same day.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Cancale oyster market open every day?

Yes, it is open every day of the year.

Where can you eat oysters in Cancale?

At the oyster market in La Houle harbour, or in the restaurants on the quays.

How far is Cancale from Saint-Malo?

About fifteen kilometres, or around twenty minutes by car.
