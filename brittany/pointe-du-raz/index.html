Pointe du Raz, Brittany’s end of the world

70-metre cliffs facing the Île de Sein, some of the strongest currents in Europe and lighthouses in the middle of the waves.

Pointe du Raz, at the end of Cap Sizun in Finistère, is one of the most famous natural sites in France. It juts into the Iroise sea facing the Île de Sein, above the Raz de Sein, a passage whose currents sailors fear.

With the Baie des Trépassés and Pointe du Van, it has been a Grand Site de France since 2004.

What to see at Pointe du Raz

The tip of the point

A path leads to the last rocks above the sea; the final section is steep.

The Île de Sein and the lighthouses

Offshore, the Île de Sein, La Vieille lighthouse and Tévennec lighthouse.

Notre-Dame-des-Naufragés

The white statue watching over sailors, near the signal station.

Baie des Trépassés

A large sandy beach between Pointe du Raz and Pointe du Van.

Pointe du Van

Wilder and quieter, with its small Saint-They chapel.

How to get to Pointe du Raz

By car

Via Audierne and Plogoff; about 1 hour from Quimper. The car park is about 20 minutes’ walk from the tip.

On foot

The GR34 passes Pointe du Raz, the Baie des Trépassés and Pointe du Van.

Practical info 2026: Pointe du Raz

  • Site access: free, all year.
  • Parking: paid in season, free in winter, from November to late March.
  • Visitor centre: open from 30 March to 1 November 2026, with exhibitions and guided walks.

Information checked in November 2026 with the Finistère department; it may change, so check before you go.

Tips for visiting Pointe du Raz

  • Come late in the day for sunset over the Île de Sein.
  • Bring a windproof jacket, even in summer.
  • Stay on the paths: the cliffs are sheer and the wind strong.
  • Carry on to Pointe du Van, which is quieter.

Frequently asked questions

Is Pointe du Raz free?

Access to the site is free; parking is paid in season and free from November to late March.

How long do you need at Pointe du Raz?

Allow 1 hour 30 minutes to 2 hours there and back from the car park; half a day with Pointe du Van.

When should you visit Pointe du Raz?

All year. The site is spectacular in rough weather and quieter in spring and autumn.
