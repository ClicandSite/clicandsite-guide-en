Dinan, the medieval town above the Rance

Half-timbered houses, over two kilometres of ramparts and a cobbled street tumbling down to the little port: Dinan is one of the best-preserved medieval towns in Brittany.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 12 November 2026

Dinan, in the Côtes-d’Armor, overlooks the Rance valley from its rocky spur. Its old town has kept its ramparts, fortified gates and dozens of medieval half-timbered houses.

Below, the port of Dinan, with its viaduct and boats, is reached via Rue du Jerzual and Rue du Petit-Fort, one of the loveliest streets in Brittany, lined with craft workshops.

Things to see in Dinan

Rue du Jerzual

The steep cobbled street running down from the old town to the port, lined with old houses and art studios. See guided tours and things to do in Dinan

The port of Dinan

The quays on the Rance, with their terraces and views of the great viaduct.

The ramparts

A circuit of about three kilometres, with the Promenade de la Duchesse-Anne and its viewpoints.

The clock tower

The old town’s belfry, with a panorama over the rooftops and countryside.

Saint-Sauveur basilica

The church holding the heart of Du Guesclin, above the English garden.

How to get to Dinan

From Saint-Malo

Half an hour by car, or by bus; in summer, boats sometimes sail up the Rance from Dinard and Saint-Malo.

By train

Dinan station, with connections via Dol-de-Bretagne or Rennes.

Practical info 2026: Dinan

  • Old town and ramparts: free access all year round.
  • Fête des Remparts: a big medieval festival held in July in even-numbered years.

Prices and opening times checked in November 2026 with official websites and local tourist offices; they may change, so check before you go.

Tips for visiting Dinan

  • Walk down Rue du Jerzual to the port, then come back along the towpath to see the town from below.
  • Park at the top of town: the central streets are narrow and often pedestrianised.
  • Combine with Saint-Malo on the same day.

Frequently asked questions

What is there to see in Dinan?

Rue du Jerzual, the port on the Rance, the ramparts, the clock tower and Saint-Sauveur basilica.

Is Dinan far from Saint-Malo?

No, about half an hour by car.

How long does it take to visit Dinan?

Half a day is enough for the old town and the port.
