Mandelieu-la-Napoule, the château by the sea

A red-stone château standing in the sea, remarkable gardens, mimosa-covered hills and the Estérel, right next to Cannes.

Mandelieu-la-Napoule lies west of Cannes, at the foot of the Estérel and Tanneron hills. The town combines the seafront of La Napoule, with its marinas and beaches, and Mandelieu, further inland.

Its landmark is the Château de la Napoule, an old fortress restored by an American artist couple. In February the town celebrates the mimosa that blooms on the surrounding hills.

A bit of history

The Château de la Napoule, an old fortress of the local lords, gradually fell into ruin over the centuries. In 1917 the American sculptor Henry Clews and his wife Marie bought it, then restored it from 1919 in a very personal neo-medieval style.

In the 20th century the town became a seaside resort and the mimosa capital, celebrated every winter since the 1930s. The château, run by an art foundation, now hosts exhibitions, concerts and artist residencies.

Things to see in Mandelieu-la-Napoule

The Château de la Napoule

A medieval red-stone fortress restored by Henry and Marie Clews, with a museum devoted to Henry Clews’s work. See activities around Mandelieu

The château gardens

Designed by Marie Clews and listed as a Remarkable Garden, they open onto the sea.

The Mimosa Festival

Every February the town celebrates the mimosa with flower parades and events.

Beaches and marinas

Sandy beaches along the bay, and the marinas of La Napoule and Cannes-Marina.

The Estérel and Théoule

The red Estérel rocks and the coves of Théoule-sur-Mer begin just past the château.

How to get to Mandelieu-la-Napoule

By car

About 15 minutes from Cannes; A8 exit 40 (Mandelieu).

By train

Mandelieu-la-Napoule station, on the Cannes – Saint-Raphaël line, is a short walk from the château.

With a chauffeur

From Nice airport, allow about 40 minutes to an hour depending on traffic.

Practical info 2026: Mandelieu-la-Napoule

  • Château de la Napoule: €4 for the gardens only, €8 for the guided tour of the château and gardens; free for under-10s.
  • 2026 season: 1 April to 20 September, Tuesday to Sunday; also open during the Mimosa Festival in February.
  • Families: free treasure hunt in the gardens.

Tips for visiting Mandelieu-la-Napoule

  • Come in February for the mimosa in bloom.
  • Allow about an hour and a half for the château and gardens.
  • Continue along the Corniche d’Or to Théoule and the Estérel.
