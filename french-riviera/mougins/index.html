Visit Mougins

A spiral-shaped village above Cannes, where Picasso spent his last years and where people come as much for the art as for the food.

Mougins winds around its hill a few kilometres from Cannes. Its flowery lanes are lined with galleries and restaurants: the village has been known for its cuisine for decades.

Picasso lived in Mougins from 1961 until his death in 1973. Today the village is home to FAMM, Europe’s first private museum devoted to women artists.

Things to do in Mougins

The village lanes

A circular layout, stone houses covered in flowers and art galleries at every step.

FAMM

Opened in 2024 in the former classical art museum, it shows works by women artists from the 19th century to today.

The wash house and town hall square

The heart of the village, with shady terraces.

Mougins’ restaurants

The village has a long gastronomic tradition: plenty of restaurants, from the simplest to the most renowned.

Notre-Dame-de-Vie chapel

Away from the village at the end of a cypress avenue, with a view over the countryside. Picasso lived in the farmhouse next door.

How to get to Mougins

By bus from Cannes

Buses link Cannes with Mougins village.

By car or with a driver

The village is pedestrianised, with car parks at the entrance. Mougins combines well with Grasse or Valbonne.

Tips for visiting Mougins

  • Come in the late afternoon, browse the galleries, then stay for dinner.
  • Book restaurants ahead in season and during events in Cannes.
  • Combine Mougins with Grasse, about twenty minutes away.

Frequently asked questions

How do you get to Mougins from Cannes?

By bus or by car: the village is about twenty minutes from central Cannes.

What is FAMM?

The Femmes Artistes du Musée de Mougins, opened in 2024 and devoted to works by women artists.

Did Picasso live in Mougins?

Yes, from 1961 until his death in 1973, in a farmhouse near Notre-Dame-de-Vie chapel.
