The French Riviera in 3 days

Three days, a coastal train and the best of the Riviera. This itinerary works entirely without a car, with Nice as your base.

  1. Day 1: Nice and Villefranche-sur-Mer

    • Nice: in the morning, the Cours Saleya market and the lanes of Old Nice, then up Castle Hill for the view over the bay.
    • Villefranche-sur-Mer: in the afternoon, ten minutes by train to Villefranche: the Cocteau chapel, Rue Obscure and a swim at Marinières beach.

  2. Day 2: Èze and Monaco

    • Èze: leave early for Èze, before the coaches arrive: the village lanes and the garden at the top.
    • Monaco: head down to Monaco for the changing of the guard at 11:55, then the Oceanographic Museum, Port Hercule and Casino Square at the end of the day.

  3. Day 3: Cannes and the Lérins Islands

    • Cannes: train to Cannes, the Forville market and a climb up Le Suquet in the morning.
    • Lérins Islands: boat from Quai Laubeuf to Sainte-Marguerite: Fort Royal, the coastal path and a swim in the coves. Back for the Croisette at sunset.

With a car

With a car or a driver, swap day 3 for the hinterland: Grasse and its perfumeries in the morning, Gourdon and its views at lunchtime, then Saint-Paul-de-Vence in the late afternoon.

Guides: Grasse, Gourdon, Saint-Paul-de-Vence.

Tips for these three days

  • Stay in Nice: the station has direct regional trains to Villefranche, Monaco, Antibes and Cannes.
  • Avoid driving in July and August; the coastal train is faster.
  • Dress modestly for Monaco Cathedral and the casino.
