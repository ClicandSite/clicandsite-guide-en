Mimosa Route and Mimosa Festival 2027

From January to March, mimosa blooms along 130 km between Bormes-les-Mimosas and Grasse. The stops, the parades and the Mandelieu Mimosa Festival, 10 to 14 February 2027.

In midwinter, while the rest of Europe is grey, the hills between the Var and the Alpes-Maritimes turn yellow with mimosa. The Mimosa Route follows the blossom for 130 kilometres, from Bormes-les-Mimosas to Grasse. The best time is January to March, peaking in February.

The 8 stops on the route

  1. km 0
    Bormes-les-Mimosas

    The starting point: a hilltop medieval village, the Var’s mimosa capital, with more than 90 species. Its flower parade weekend is 20-21 February 2027: free mimosa market on Saturday, float parade on Sunday (paid entry, tickets sold on site).

  2. km 17
    Rayol-Canadel-sur-Mer

    A village on the Corniche des Maures, towards Saint-Tropez.

  3. Sainte-Maxime

    Its mimosa parade takes place on Sunday 7 February 2027 at 2.30pm; free standing, paid grandstand seats.

  4. km 59
    Saint-Raphaël

    At the foot of the Esterel massif and its red rocks.

  5. Mandelieu-La Napoule

    The mimosa capital, with its big February festival.

  6. km 122
    Tanneron

    The massif where the mimosa-covered hills are most spectacular.

  7. Pégomas

    A village among mimosa and eucalyptus, on the river Siagne.

  8. km 130
    Grasse

    The finish, in the world capital of perfume.

The 2027 Mandelieu Mimosa Festival

It runs from Wednesday 10 to Sunday 14 February 2027, on the theme “Le mimosa entre en jeu”. The programme includes parades, street entertainment, mimosa giveaways and fireworks. The highlight is the big flower parade on Sunday afternoon, with floats covered in mimosa, between the beaches, the port and the Château de La Napoule. The festival dates back to 1931.

Our tips

  • Take two or three days: the route is done by car, stopping in each village.
  • Finish before dark: in February, the sun sets early behind the Esterel; plan your last stops before 5.30pm.
  • Combine it with the other festivals: the Nice Carnival and the Menton Lemon Festival take place at the same time.

Frequently asked questions

When is the 2027 Mimosa Festival in Mandelieu?

From Wednesday 10 to Sunday 14 February 2027, with the big flower parade on Sunday afternoon.

When is the Sainte-Maxime mimosa parade in 2027?

Sunday 7 February 2027 at 2.30pm; free standing, paid grandstand seats.

When is the Bormes-les-Mimosas mimosa festival in 2027?

The Bormes-les-Mimosas flower parade weekend is 20-21 February 2027: mimosa market on Saturday (free), float parade on Sunday (paid entry, tickets sold on site). Dates checked in November 2026.

When is the best time for the Mimosa Route?

January to March, with the strongest blossom in February.

How long is the Mimosa Route?

130 kilometres, from Bormes-les-Mimosas to Grasse, in eight stops.
