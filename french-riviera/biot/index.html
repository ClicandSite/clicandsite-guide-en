Visit Biot

The glassblowers’ village. Watch bubble glass being blown, wander between workshops and discover Fernand Léger’s colours.

Biot is a hilltop village between Antibes and Valbonne, known worldwide for its bubble glass, born here in the mid-20th century. Glassblowers’ and potters’ workshops made its name.

At the foot of the village, the Fernand Léger National Museum, its façade covered by a huge colourful mosaic, is one of the most unusual museums on the coast.

Things to do in Biot

The Biot glassworks

Watch the glassblowers at work and learn the bubble-glass technique. Shop and gallery on site. Book a guided tour or activity in Biot.

The Fernand Léger National Museum

A museum devoted entirely to the painter, with its great mosaic façade and gardens.

Place des Arcades

The heart of the village, with medieval arcades and terraces.

Craft workshops

Glassmakers, potters, goldsmiths: Biot’s lanes are full of workshops open to the public.

How to get to Biot

By train, then bus

Biot station is by the sea; buses then climb up to the village.

By car or with a driver

Biot is about fifteen minutes from Antibes. Car parks at the village entrance.

Practical info 2026: Biot

  • Fernand Léger National Museum: €8 (€10 during exhibitions), free for EU citizens under 26. Closed on Tuesdays; in winter, 10am–5pm.
  • Exhibition: “Léger – Morellet, De-figuring Painting”, 21 November 2026 to 26 April 2027.
  • Access: free parking at the museum; TER trains stop at Biot station, at the bottom of the town.

Tips for visiting Biot

  • Start with the glassworks below the village to see the glassblowers at work.
  • The Fernand Léger museum combines easily with a visit to the village.
  • Biot pairs well with Antibes or Saint-Paul-de-Vence on the same day.

Frequently asked questions

What is Biot bubble glass?

Glass with air bubbles deliberately trapped inside, which became the signature of Biot’s glassworks.

Can you watch glassblowers in Biot?

Yes, the glassworks lets you watch the glassmakers at work in the workshop.

How do you get to Biot without a car?

By train to Biot station, then by bus up to the village.
