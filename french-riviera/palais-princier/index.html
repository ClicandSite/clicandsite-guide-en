The Prince’s Palace looks over Monaco from the Rock. It is the prince’s official residence, built on the site of a 13th-century Genoese fortress and embellished in the Renaissance.

Part of the palace, the State Apartments, is open from April to October. All year round, Place du Palais hosts the daily changing of the guard by the Prince’s Carabiniers.

A bit of history

The Prince’s Palace stands on the site of a Genoese fortress built in 1215. The Grimaldis made it their residence at the end of the 13th century, and it still is today.

In the Renaissance the fortress was turned into a palace, with galleries, frescoes and a double-revolution staircase. The Company of the Prince’s Carabiniers, who perform the changing of the guard, was created in 1817.

Things to see at the Prince’s Palace

The changing of the guard

Every day at 11.55 am the Prince’s Carabiniers change the guard on Place du Palais. Arrive a little early for a good spot. See tickets and tours for the Prince’s Palace

The State Apartments

The Hercules Gallery and its 16th-century frescoes, the Hall of Mirrors, the Throne Room and the Palatine Chapel.

The Court of Honour

With its double-revolution marble staircase.

Place du Palais

Its old cannons and views over the port of Monaco and Fontvieille.

The Rock

The lanes of Monaco-Ville, the cathedral and the Oceanographic Museum are a few minutes’ walk away.

How to get to the Prince’s Palace

On foot

From Port Hercule, a ramp climbs to the Rock in about fifteen minutes.

By bus

Monaco’s buses run up to Monaco-Ville.

With a chauffeur

From Nice airport, allow about 30 to 45 minutes depending on traffic.

Practical info 2026: The Prince’s Palace of Monaco

  • Changing of the guard: every day at 11.55 am on Place du Palais.
  • State Apartments: open April to October; in July and August, daily 10 am to 7 pm. Closed during the Monaco Grand Prix. Current prices on the Palace website.
  • Accessibility: the State Apartments are not accessible to people with reduced mobility.

Prices and opening times checked in November 2026 with official websites and local tourist offices; they may change, so check before you go.

Tips for visiting the Prince’s Palace

  • Arrive around 11.30 am for the changing of the guard, then visit the State Apartments.
  • Combine with the Oceanographic Museum, a 10-minute walk away.
  • Avoid the Rock at midday in summer, when the tour groups arrive.

Frequently asked questions

What time is the changing of the guard in Monaco?

Every day at 11.55 am on Place du Palais.

When can you visit the Prince’s Palace?

The State Apartments are open from April to October; the rest of the year only the outside and the changing of the guard can be seen.

How long should you spend at the Prince’s Palace?

Allow about 1 hour.

What is there to see near the Prince’s Palace?

In this guide, the closest places are The Rock of Monaco (about 5 min on foot), Port Hercule (about 5 min on foot), Monaco Exotic Garden (about 10 min on foot).
