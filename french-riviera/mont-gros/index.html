The Mont Gros hike above Nice

A 5 km loop from the Saint-Roch district, through the Parc du Vinaigrier, below the great dome of the Nice Observatory.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 15 November 2026

Mont Gros is a 375-metre hill on the east side of Nice. On top stands the Nice Observatory, whose great white dome can be seen from all over the city.

Just below, the Parc du Vinaigrier has trails through real countryside, a few minutes from the centre. It is one of the best hikes you can start right from Nice.

A bit of history

The Nice Observatory was built on top of Mont Gros from 1881, at the initiative of the banker Raphaël Bischoffsheim. The buildings were designed by Charles Garnier, the architect of the Paris Opera.

The dome of the great telescope was made by Gustave Eiffel’s workshops and the work was completed in 1887. Today the site belongs to the Observatoire de la Côte d’Azur.

The route

  1. Start at the Saint-Roch church. Walk up Avenue Giacobi and its steps to Boulevard Bischoffsheim, on the Grande Corniche.
  2. Take the Chemin du Cal du Mont Gros, which climbs between villas, then follow the yellow and red waymarks of the Nice long-distance trail (GRP) to the entrance of the Parc du Vinaigrier.
  3. In the park, turn right on the upper Vinaigrier trail to the summit plateau and its wooden tower.
  4. Come down past the water treatment station, then along the level path back to the park entrance, and return to Saint-Roch the way you came.

Allow about 2 hours 30 minutes for 5.2 km and 330 metres of climb. The trail is of medium difficulty: the climb from Saint-Roch is steep in places.

What to see on the hike

The Parc du Vinaigrier

A 31-hectare natural area owned by the Conservatoire du littoral, on the slopes of Mont Gros. The trails wind between olive groves, pines and old terraced fields.

The Mont Vinaigrier viewpoint

On the summit plateau, at about 370 metres, a wooden tower serves as a viewpoint. The view takes in Nice, the bay of Villefranche, Cap Ferrat and the peaks of the Alps.

Traces of the past

The park keeps the remains of a cyclopean wall from an ancient fortified settlement, and an old irrigation system from the farms of the past.

The Nice Observatory

The great white dome towers over the hike. The Observatory site can only be visited with a guide, on booking.

How to get to the start

On foot

The start is in front of the Saint-Roch church, in the district of the same name, east of central Nice.

By bus

Lignes d’Azur bus 84 serves the top of the park, at the “Sainte-Anne” stop. Handy for a shorter walk without the climb from town.

By car

Take the Grande Corniche and then Boulevard de l’Observatoire: there is a small car park at the upper entrance of the park, about 500 metres past the Observatory.

Practical info 2026: Mont Gros

  • Parc du Vinaigrier: free, open every day, 7.30 am to 8 pm from April to October and 8 am to 6 pm from November to March.
  • Nice Observatory: guided visits only, booked online on oca.eu: €12 full price, €10 reduced, €6 children and students, free under 6.
  • Visit: allow about 2 hours for the guided visit of the Observatory.

Prices and opening times checked in November 2026 with the Observatoire de la Côte d’Azur and the tourist office; they may change, so check before you go.

Tips for the hike

  • Wear good shoes: some stretches are steep.
  • Take water: there are no shops or restaurants around the Observatory.
  • Go early in the morning or late in the day for the light over the bay and less heat.
  • To visit the Observatory, book your slot in advance on the Observatoire de la Côte d’Azur website.
  • For a longer hike, carry on past the Col de Villefranche to Mont Alban and Mont Boron.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the Mont Gros hike?

Allow about 2 hours 30 minutes for the 5.2 km loop from the Saint-Roch church, with 330 metres of climb.

Is the hike difficult?

It is of medium difficulty: the climb from Saint-Roch is steep in places, the rest is easy.

Can you visit the Nice Observatory?

Yes, but only on a guided visit, booked online with the Observatoire de la Côte d’Azur. Full price is €12.

Is the Parc du Vinaigrier free?

Yes, entry is free. The park is open every day, until 8 pm from April to October and until 6 pm from November to March.

What is there to see near Mont Gros?

In this guide, the closest places are Port Lympia (about 10 min by car), Vieux-Nice (about 15 min by car) and Villefranche-sur-Mer (about 15 min by car).
