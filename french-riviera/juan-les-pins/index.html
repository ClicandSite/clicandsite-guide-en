Juan-les-Pins, beaches and jazz

Fine sandy beaches facing sunsets over the Esterel and a pine grove that hosts one of Europe’s oldest jazz festivals: Antibes’ seaside resort lives for summer.

On the west side of Cap d’Antibes, Juan-les-Pins is the town’s seaside resort. Launched in the 1920s, it drew Scott Fitzgerald and the gilded youth of the Jazz Age, who invented the Riviera summer season here.

Its sandy beaches, rare on this coast, face west, so the sun sets over the Esterel. Every July the Pinède Gould hosts Jazz à Juan, one of the oldest jazz festivals in Europe.

A bit of history

Juan-les-Pins grew up in the late 19th century in a pine wood by the sea. In the 1920s American millionaire Frank Jay Gould built a casino and hotels here and launched the fashion for sunbathing and the summer season on the Riviera.

F. Scott Fitzgerald stayed here at the same time. The Jazz à Juan festival, founded in 1960, has hosted the greatest musicians, from Ray Charles to Miles Davis.

How to get to Juan-les-Pins

By train

Juan-les-Pins station is a few minutes from the beaches, with regional trains from Nice and Cannes.

From Old Antibes

About 2 km: on foot, by bus or by bike around the cape.

  • Stay on the beach for sunset over the Esterel.
  • In July, book your hotel very early for Jazz à Juan.

Frequently asked questions

Are the beaches in Juan-les-Pins sandy?

Yes, they are among the few fine sandy beaches on the French Riviera.

When is Jazz à Juan?

Every July, in the Pinède Gould facing the sea.

Is Juan-les-Pins part of Antibes?

Yes, it is a district of Antibes, on the west side of the cape.

How long should you spend in Juan-les-Pins?

Allow a beach day.

What is there to see near Juan-les-Pins?

In this guide, the closest places are Antibes (about 30 min on foot), Old Antibes (about 2 km away) and Port Vauban (about 3 km away).
