Visit Grasse

The world capital of perfume. Tour the factories, create your own fragrance, and see why jasmine and rose fields are worth their weight in gold.

Photo: Everyday Paris / Unsplash

Perched above Cannes, Grasse has specialised in perfume for centuries. The perfume-making skills of the Pays de Grasse are on UNESCO’s list of intangible cultural heritage.

The old town, with its lanes and squares, has stayed authentic. People come to understand how a perfume is born, from the flowers to the bottle.

Things to do in Grasse

The International Perfume Museum

The history of perfume around the world, from antiquity to today, with bottles, stills and gardens of perfume plants a few kilometres away.

Fragonard, Molinard and Galimard

The big houses open their workshops: factory tours, often free, and shops.

Create your own perfume

Several houses run one- to two-hour workshops where you compose your fragrance with a perfumer. A unique souvenir.

The old town and cathedral

Steep lanes, arcaded squares and a cathedral with paintings by Rubens and by Fragonard, a native of the town.

Flower festivals

In May, Expo Rose celebrates the May rose; in August, the Jasmine Festival fills the town.

How to get to Grasse

By train from Cannes

A TER line links Cannes and Grasse in about thirty minutes. The station is at the bottom of the town: allow for a climb or take a bus to the centre.

By bus

Buses link Grasse with Cannes and Nice.

By car or with a driver

Grasse combines easily with Gourdon, Mougins or Saint-Paul-de-Vence.

Tips for visiting Grasse

  • Book ahead if you want a perfume-making workshop, especially in season.
  • Factory tours are short: you can easily do two in a row.
  • Combine Grasse with the hilltop villages of Gourdon or Mougins.

Frequently asked questions

Are the perfumery tours in Grasse free?

Guided factory tours at the big houses are generally free. Perfume-making workshops are paid.

How do you get to Grasse from Cannes?

By TER train, about half an hour, or by bus. By car, allow half an hour without traffic.

How long is a perfume-making workshop?

Usually one to two hours, and you leave with your own bottle.
