Fort Carré, Antibes’ star-shaped fortress

A 16th-century fortress with four bastions, on its peninsula above Port Vauban.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 13 November 2026

Fort Carré looks over Port Vauban from its small peninsula at the entrance to Antibes. It is a 16th-century star-shaped fortress with four arrowhead bastions.

Built to watch the border with the County of Nice, it has largely kept its original shape: Vauban, who came later, barely changed its outline. The visit gives one of the best views of Antibes and the bay.

A bit of history

In the mid-16th century a round tower was built on the peninsula, on the site of the Saint-Laurent chapel, to watch the border with the County of Nice. It was then surrounded by four bastions that give the fort its star shape.

Vauban visited Antibes in the late 17th century. He mainly strengthened the town and harbour defences but barely altered the outline of Fort Carré, which keeps most of its 16th-century form.

Things to see at Fort Carré

The four bastions

The points of the star, from which the sea and the border were watched. See tours of Antibes

The view

Over Port Vauban and its yachts, the old town, the Baie des Anges and the Alps on a clear day.

The park

A protected 4-hectare park of Mediterranean vegetation around the fort.

The coastal path

A waterside walk at the foot of the fort.

Port Vauban

One of the Mediterranean’s great marinas, just below.

How to get to Fort Carré

On foot

From Antibes station or Port Vauban, allow 15 to 20 minutes.

By car

Parking near the entrance, Avenue du 11-Novembre.

With a chauffeur

From Nice airport, allow about 20 to 30 minutes depending on traffic.

Practical info 2026: Fort Carré, Antibes

  • Opening times: closed Mondays; 10 am to 6 pm in July and August, 10 am to 5 pm the rest of the year. Closed 1 January, 1 May, 1 November, 25 December and in bad weather.
  • Prices: €5 full, €3 reduced. €15 combined ticket with the municipal museums (including the Picasso Museum), valid 7 days.
  • Access: Avenue du 11-Novembre. Not wheelchair accessible; sloping, stony path.

Prices and opening times checked in November 2026 with official websites and local tourist offices; they may change, so check before you go.

Tips for visiting Fort Carré

  • Come on a clear day for the view to the Alps.
  • Wear good shoes: the path is stony.
  • Get the combined ticket with the Picasso Museum.
