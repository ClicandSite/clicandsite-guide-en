Vieux-Nice, lanes and socca

Narrow ochre lanes, a flower market, baroque churches and piping-hot socca: Nice’s old town is best explored with an appetite.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 12 November 2026

Squeezed between Castle Hill, the sea and Place Masséna, Vieux-Nice is a tangle of lanes with ochre and red façades and washing drying at the windows. It has kept its baroque churches, small shops and down-to-earth cooking.

It is the best place to taste socca, pissaladière or petits farcis. In the morning the Cours Saleya flower market comes alive; late in the day, people climb Castle Hill to watch the Baie des Anges glow.

A bit of history

The old town grew up at the foot of Castle Hill, where a citadel stood until Louis XIV had it razed in 1706. Its lanes still bear the stamp of Nice’s Savoyard and Italian past.

Sainte-Réparate cathedral and the Palais Lascaris, an aristocratic mansion, date from the 17th century. The flower market on Cours Saleya has been held since the late 19th century.

Things to see in Vieux-Nice

Cours Saleya

The morning flower and vegetable market, then restaurant terraces in the evening. Book a food tour of Vieux-Nice and the Cours Saleya

Sainte-Réparate cathedral

The baroque cathedral on Place Rossetti, also known for its ice-cream parlours.

Palais Lascaris

A 17th-century baroque palace with a collection of antique musical instruments.

Castle Hill

The park at the top, its waterfall and the best view over the Baie des Anges and the port.

Niçois food

Socca, pissaladière, pan bagnat and petits farcis from the old-town stalls.

How to get to Vieux-Nice

By tram

Line 1, Opéra-Vieille Ville or Cathédrale-Vieille Ville stops; from the airport, line 2 serves the centre.

On foot

The district is pedestrianised and compact, a few minutes from the Promenade des Anglais and Place Masséna.

Practical info 2026: Vieux-Nice

  • Cours Saleya: flower and fresh produce market Tuesday to Sunday mornings; flea market on Mondays.
  • Castle Hill: free park; a free lift goes up from the bottom of the hill on the sea side.
  • Palais Lascaris: €5 entry, or €15 for the Nice municipal museums pass; closed on Tuesdays.

Tips for visiting Vieux-Nice

  • Eat socca hot from the oven, ideally late morning.
  • Climb Castle Hill at sunset.
  • Carry on to Port Lympia, on the other side of the hill.

Frequently asked questions

What should I eat in Vieux-Nice?

Socca, a chickpea-flour pancake, pissaladière, pan bagnat and petits farcis.

When is the Cours Saleya market?

Tuesday to Sunday mornings for flowers and fresh produce; Monday is flea market day.

How do you get up Castle Hill?

On foot by the steps from Vieux-Nice or the port, or with the free lift on the sea side.

How long should you spend in Vieux-Nice?

Allow half a day.

What is there to see near Vieux-Nice?
