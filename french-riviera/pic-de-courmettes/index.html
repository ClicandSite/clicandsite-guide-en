The Pic de Courmettes hike

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 15 November 2026

The Pic de Courmettes rises to 1,248 metres above Tourrettes-sur-Loup and Vence, in the Préalpes d’Azur regional nature park. From the top, the view stretches from the Esterel to Italy.

The hike starts from the Domaine des Courmettes. On the nearby ridge, between Puy de Naouri and Puy de Tourrettes, lie the remains of a light aircraft that crashed in November 2010.

A bit of history

The Domaine des Courmettes is an old mountain farm estate surrounded by pastures. On the plateau there is a dolmen, a sign of very ancient settlement.

The wreck is that of a small tourist plane that crashed on the ridge in November 2010. The debris was left in place: it is a place of remembrance, to be respected.

The route

  1. Start from the Domaine des Courmettes car park, above Tourrettes-sur-Loup.
  2. Climb through the pastures to the top of the Pic de Courmettes (1,248 m).
  3. For a longer loop, follow the ridge to Puy de Tourrettes (1,268 m), where the wreck lies, then come back down to the estate.

The out-and-back to the peak is about 6.2 km with 430 metres of climb. The loop via Puy de Tourrettes is about 11.6 km and 645 metres of climb, or 4 hours on foot. Beyond the peak the trail is no longer waymarked.

What to see on the hike

The view from the top

The whole coast, from the Esterel to Cap d’Antibes and on to Italy, with the snowy Alps behind.

The plane wreck

The remains of a light aircraft on the Puy de Tourrettes ridge. Touch nothing and take nothing.

The dolmen and the vultures

A dolmen hides near the estate, and griffon vultures often soar above the ridges.

How to get to the start

By car

From Tourrettes-sur-Loup, take the road to the Domaine des Courmettes up to the car park. The road is narrow.

Without a car

No public transport goes up to the estate: a car or a driver is the easiest option.

Practical info 2026: Pic de Courmettes

  • Access: free, in the Préalpes d’Azur regional nature park.
  • Water: no water on the route.
  • Waymarks: marked trail up to the peak, not beyond; a map or GPS track is advised for the loop.

Information checked in November 2026 with the Alpes-Maritimes department (Randoxygène); it may change, so check before you go.

Tips for the hike

  • Take plenty of water: there is none on the way.
  • Avoid very hot days: the trail is very exposed.
  • Bring a map or GPS track if you do the loop via Puy de Tourrettes.
  • Respect the wreck: it is the site of a fatal accident.

Frequently asked questions

How high is the Pic de Courmettes?

The Pic de Courmettes rises to 1,248 metres above Tourrettes-sur-Loup and Vence.

Where is the plane wreck?

On the ridge between Puy de Naouri and Puy de Tourrettes, on the loop that extends the climb to the peak.

How long is the hike?

About 2 hours 30 minutes for the out-and-back to the peak (6.2 km), and 4 hours for the 11.6 km loop via Puy de Tourrettes.

What is there to see near the Pic de Courmettes?

In this guide, the closest places are Saint-Paul-de-Vence (about 25 min by car), Gourdon (about 35 min by car) and Cascade de Vescagne (about 35 min by car).
