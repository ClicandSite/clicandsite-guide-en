The Cime de la Forna loop

A 6.8 km loop from La Turbie, along the ridges of the Grande Corniche departmental park.

Cime de la Forna is one of the summits of the Grande Corniche departmental park, above La Turbie and Èze. The loop goes around it from the village.

The trail passes the Simboula viewpoint table, with a view over the coast, Monaco and Mont Agel. An easy-going hike through scrubland and pine woods.

A bit of history

The Grande Corniche departmental park protects the ridges between Èze and La Turbie. It runs along the Grande Corniche, a road built under Napoleon I on the line of the old Roman road.

The route

  1. Start at Place Detras in La Turbie.
  2. Climb to the ridges of the departmental park and Cime de la Forna.
  3. Pass the Simboula viewpoint table, then come back down to La Turbie to close the loop.

Allow about 2 hours 30 minutes for 6.8 km and 290 metres of climb. The high point is at 670 metres. The difficulty is easy to medium.

What to see on the hike

The Simboula viewpoint

A viewpoint with a table naming the peaks and capes of the coast.

The view over Monaco and Mont Agel

The ridges overlook the Principality and face Mont Agel.

The Trophy of Augustus

At the start, in La Turbie, the Roman monument built in the 1st century BC.

How to get to the start

By bus

ZOU bus 603 from Nice and 604 or 605 from Monaco stop at “La Turbie Mairie”, a short walk from Place Detras.

By car

La Turbie is on the Grande Corniche and has its own exit on the A8 motorway. Park in the village.

Practical info 2026: Cime de la Forna

  • Access: free, open all year.
  • Departmental park: follow the park rules: no fires, dogs on a lead.
  • Water: no water on the ridges.

Information checked in November 2026 with the Alpes-Maritimes department; it may change, so check before you go.

Tips for the hike

  • Take water and a hat, the ridges are exposed.
  • Come on a clear day to make the most of the viewpoint table.
  • Combine the hike with a visit to the Trophy of Augustus.
  • For a full day, add the Tête de Chien loop.
