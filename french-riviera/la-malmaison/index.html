A bit of history

La Malmaison is the only remnant of the Grand Hôtel de Cannes, built on the Croisette in 1863, when the town was becoming a fashionable winter resort.

From 1970 the villa hosted the town’s art exhibitions. Since 2018 the whole building has been devoted to the art centre, which reopened in 2025 with larger rooms and a rooftop terrace.

Things to see at La Malmaison

The exhibitions

Major temporary exhibitions of modern and contemporary art, changed several times a year. See tours and activities in Cannes

The villa

A Belle Époque façade with columns, wrought-iron balconies and balustrades among the palm trees.

The rooftop terrace

Café Olympe, on the roof, looks out over the bay of Cannes.

The Croisette

Outside, the promenade runs to the Palais des Festivals one way and Pointe Croisette the other.

The Palais des Festivals

A few minutes’ walk away, for the photo on the steps.

How to get to La Malmaison

On foot

From Cannes station, allow about 10 minutes’ walk to the Croisette.

By train

TER trains link Cannes with Nice and Antibes; the station is right in the centre.

With a chauffeur

From Nice airport, allow about 35 to 45 minutes depending on traffic.

Practical info 2026: La Malmaison art centre

  • Address: 47 boulevard de la Croisette, Cannes.
  • Prices: €6.50 full price; €3.50 for 18–25s and groups; free for under-18s, students and jobseekers.
  • Opening times: Tuesday to Sunday 10 am–1 pm and 2–6 pm; daily 10 am–7 pm in July and August. The centre sometimes closes between exhibitions: check before you go.

Prices and opening times checked in November 2026 with official websites and local tourist offices; they may change, so check before you go.

Tips for visiting La Malmaison

  • Check the programme first: the venue is all about its temporary exhibitions.
  • Go up to the rooftop terrace for the view over the bay.
  • Combine with the Croisette, the Palais des Festivals and Le Suquet on the same day.
