Visiting the Verdon Gorge

Europe’s largest canyon, emerald-green water and dizzying cliffs, two hours from the Riviera.

The Verdon Gorge is the essential nature trip from the French Riviera. Over some twenty kilometres the river has carved a canyon with cliffs several hundred metres high.

Where the gorge ends, Lake Sainte-Croix and its turquoise water are perfect for swimming and pedal boats. Nearby, Moustiers-Sainte-Marie is one of the most beautiful villages in Provence.

Things to do in the Verdon Gorge

The Route des Crêtes

A loop from La Palud-sur-Verdon with viewpoints on the edge of the drop. The most impressive views of the canyon.

Lake Sainte-Croix

A huge reservoir with turquoise water, beaches, pedal boats and canoes.

Pont du Galetas

At the mouth of the gorge, where the canyon opens onto the lake. Starting point for electric boat and canoe trips into the gorge.

Moustiers-Sainte-Marie

A village at the foot of a cliff, famous for its faience and the star hanging above it.

The Blanc-Martel trail

The great hike along the bottom of the gorge, for experienced walkers: a full day, with tunnel sections.

How to get to the Verdon Gorge

By car or with a driver

Allow about two to two and a half hours from Nice or Cannes. You need a full day.

On an organised tour

Without a car it is the simplest option: many tours leave from Nice and Cannes.

Tips for visiting the Verdon Gorge

  • Leave very early to enjoy the viewpoints before the crowds and find a space at Pont du Galetas.
  • Pedal boat and canoe hire is in high demand in summer: arrive when they open.
  • Swimming in the Verdon can be dangerous because of water releases from the dams: follow the signs.

Frequently asked questions

Can you do the Verdon Gorge as a day trip from Nice?

Yes, but it is a long day: about two hours’ drive each way. An organised tour saves you the driving.

Can you swim in the Verdon Gorge?

Yes, especially in Lake Sainte-Croix. In the gorge, watch out for dam water releases that suddenly raise the water level.

When is the best time to visit the Verdon?

June and September: good weather, warm water and fewer people than in July-August.
