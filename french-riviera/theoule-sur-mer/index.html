Théoule-sur-Mer lies west of Cannes, where the last foothills of the Estérel massif drop into the Mediterranean. It is the last town in the Alpes-Maritimes before the Var.

People come for the coves, the Pointe de l’Aiguille and the Corniche d’Or, the scenic road along the Estérel to Agay. A day is enough to enjoy it.

A bit of history

Long a simple fishing hamlet belonging to Mandelieu, Théoule became an independent town in 1929. By the harbour, a former soap factory that looks like a castle recalls its craft past; it is now a hotel.

With the arrival of the railway and the opening of the Corniche d’Or in the early 20th century, the village turned to holidaymaking. Villas, including the famous Bubble Palace, dot its hills, and its coastline is now protected by a departmental marine park.

Things to see in Théoule-sur-Mer

Pointe de l’Aiguille

A 7-hectare park where the red Estérel rocks plunge into the sea, 800 metres on foot from the centre. See activities in the Estérel

The underwater trail

From mid-June to mid-September, a buoyed route lets you explore the seabed with mask and snorkel.

The harbour and château

The marina is overlooked by a castle-like building: a former soap factory, now a hotel.

Beaches and coves

Plage du Château, Vallon de l’Autel, Petite Fontaine: mostly small coves rather than long beaches.

The Corniche d’Or

A road of about twenty kilometres between Théoule and Agay, with views of the red rocks and the sea.

How to get to Théoule-sur-Mer

By car

About 20 minutes from Cannes along the coast; A8 exit 40 (Mandelieu).

By train

TER trains on the Cannes – Saint-Raphaël line stop at Théoule-sur-Mer station.

With a chauffeur

From Nice airport, allow about 45 minutes to an hour depending on traffic.

Practical info 2026: Théoule-sur-Mer

  • Pointe de l’Aiguille: free access, parking on the Corniche d’Or.
  • Underwater trail: free, from mid-June to mid-September.
  • Château de Théoule: private hotel, visible from the harbour and beach.

Prices and opening times checked in November 2026 with official websites and local tourist offices; they may change, so check before you go.

Tips for visiting Théoule-sur-Mer

  • Park early in summer: spaces are scarce on the Corniche.
  • Bring a mask and snorkel for the underwater trail.
  • Bring water: the Estérel trails have no shade.

Frequently asked questions

What is there to do in Théoule-sur-Mer?

Swim in the coves, walk to the Pointe de l’Aiguille, try the underwater trail and drive the Corniche d’Or.

How do you get to Théoule-sur-Mer from Cannes?

By car along the coast in about twenty minutes, or by TER train to Théoule-sur-Mer station.

How long should you spend in Théoule-sur-Mer?

Allow one day.

What is there to see near Théoule-sur-Mer?

In this guide, the closest places are Mandelieu-la-Napoule (about 30 min on foot), Esterel Massif (about 5 km away), Le Suquet (about 9 km away).
