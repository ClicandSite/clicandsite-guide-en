The Picasso Museum in the Château Grimaldi, Antibes

The first museum devoted to Picasso, in a seafront castle where the painter worked in 1946.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 13 November 2026

The Picasso Museum occupies the Château Grimaldi, on the ramparts of Antibes’ old town, facing the sea. It was the world’s first museum devoted entirely to the artist.

In autumn 1946 Picasso set up his studio in the castle and made many works there, which he left to the town. The collection has grown since, with ceramics, drawings and works by other artists.

A bit of history

The Château Grimaldi stands on the site of the acropolis of ancient Antipolis. Bought by the town in 1925, it became the Grimaldi Museum, devoted to history and archaeology.

In 1946 the curator offered Picasso a studio there. From mid-September to mid-November the painter created many works and left 23 paintings and 44 drawings to the town. The castle became the Picasso Museum on 27 December 1966.

Things to see at the Picasso Museum

The 1946 works

Paintings and drawings made by Picasso in the castle itself in autumn 1946. See tours of Antibes

The ceramics

Pieces made in Vallauris and given to the museum.

The terrace

Sculptures by Germaine Richier facing the Mediterranean, with views of Cap d’Antibes.

The Château Grimaldi

A listed monument built on the site of the ancient Greek acropolis of Antipolis.

The old town

The ramparts, the cathedral and the Provençal market, right next door.

How to get to the Picasso Museum

On foot

From Antibes station, allow about fifteen minutes via Port Vauban.

By train

TER trains from Nice or Cannes to Antibes station.

With a chauffeur

From Nice airport, allow about 20 to 30 minutes depending on traffic.

Practical info 2026: Picasso Museum, Antibes

  • Opening times: closed Mondays; 10 am–1 pm and 2–6 pm, continuously 10 am–6 pm from mid-June to mid-September. Closed 1 January, 1 May, 1 November and 25 December.
  • Prices: €12 full, €8 reduced; free for under-18s. €15 combined ticket for the municipal museums and Fort Carré, valid 7 days.
  • Address: Château Grimaldi, Place Mariéjol, in the old town.

Prices and opening times checked in November 2026 with official websites and local tourist offices; they may change, so check before you go.

Tips for visiting the Picasso Museum

  • Leave time for the terrace, the view is worth it.
  • Get the combined ticket if you are also visiting Fort Carré.
  • Do the Provençal market in the morning, right next door.
