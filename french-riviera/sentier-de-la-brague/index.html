The Brague trail, from Biot to Valbonne

An easy, shady 10 km hike along the Brague river, between the villages of Biot and Valbonne.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 15 November 2026

The Brague trail follows the river of the same name between Biot and Valbonne, a few kilometres from Antibes. It crosses the Brague departmental park, a wooded valley in the middle of the Sophia Antipolis technology park.

The path is almost flat, in the shade of oaks and alders. An easy hike, ideal in summer or with children.

A bit of history

The Brague rises in the Grasse hinterland and reaches the sea between Antibes and Biot. Its valley, long used for farming, is now protected by the Alpes-Maritimes department.

The route

  1. Start from Biot town hall or, the other way, from the church and cemetery of Valbonne (waymark 362).
  2. The trail follows the Brague through the departmental park, crossing the river several times.
  3. Finish in the other village. Come back on Envibus bus 10. You can also cut the walk short at the Pont des Tamarins.

Allow about 3 hours 30 minutes for 10 km one way. There is little climb and the difficulty is easy.

What to see on the hike

The river

The Brague flows between rocks and trees, with small waterfalls and pools.

Biot village

At the start, the hilltop village of glassmakers, with its arcades and workshops.

Valbonne village

At the finish, a village laid out on a grid, with its arcaded square.

How to get to the start

By bus

Envibus buses serve Biot and Valbonne; bus 10 takes you back to the start.

By car

Park in Biot or Valbonne, then come back by bus.

Practical info 2026: Brague trail

  • Access: free, open all year.
  • Brague departmental park: no fires, dogs on a lead.
  • Floods: the trail may be closed after heavy rain.

Information checked in November 2026 with the Alpes-Maritimes department; it may change, so check before you go.

Tips for the hike

  • Avoid the trail after heavy rain: the Brague floods quickly.
  • Wear shoes with good grip, some stretches are damp.
  • Do it in summer: it is almost entirely in the shade.
  • Check the bus 10 timetable for the way back.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the Brague trail?

About 10 km between Biot and Valbonne, or 3 hours 30 minutes one way.

How do you get back to the start?

On Envibus bus 10 between Valbonne and Biot. You can also cut it short at the Pont des Tamarins.

Is the trail easy?

Yes, it is almost flat, but some stretches are damp and stony.

What is there to see near the Brague trail?

In this guide, the closest places are Biot (at the start), Antibes (about 15 min by car) and Mougins (about 20 min by car).
