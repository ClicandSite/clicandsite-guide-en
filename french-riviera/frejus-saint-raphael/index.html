Fréjus and Saint-Raphaël, between Rome and the Esterel

A town founded by Julius Caesar, a marina below a basilica and the red rocks of the Esterel: two neighbouring towns at the gateway to the French Riviera.

Fréjus and Saint-Raphaël sit side by side on the Gulf of Fréjus, halfway between Cannes and Saint-Tropez. Fréjus, ancient Forum Julii founded by Julius Caesar, still has an amphitheatre, aqueduct arches and one of the oldest Christian complexes in France.

Saint-Raphaël, a seaside resort born in the 19th century, stretches from the Old Port to the red coves of the Esterel. The Corniche d’Or follows the coast to Agay and Cap Dramont, with some of the finest coves and trails on the Riviera.

Things to see in Fréjus and Saint-Raphaël

The episcopal complex of Fréjus

The amphitheatre and Roman remains

The arena, the Lanterne d’Auguste and the aqueduct arches, traces of Forum Julii.

Saint-Raphaël Old Port

Boats in front of the basilica of Notre-Dame-de-la-Victoire and the seafront promenade.

The Esterel corniche

The red-rock coast road to Agay, with its coves and sea views.

Cap Dramont

A coastal trail facing the Île d’Or and its little tower, one of the area’s classic walks.

How to get to Fréjus and Saint-Raphaël

By train

Saint-Raphaël-Valescure station is served by TGVs from Paris and by regional trains along the coast from Cannes and Nice.

By car

On the A8, allow about 40 minutes from Cannes and an hour from Nice airport, traffic permitting.

Tips for visiting Fréjus and Saint-Raphaël

  • Do Fréjus in the morning and the Esterel corniche in the afternoon.
  • Set off early for the Cap Dramont trail in summer, as it gets hot quickly.
  • Carry on into the Esterel massif for a hike.

Frequently asked questions

What is there to see in Fréjus?

The episcopal complex and its baptistery, the Roman amphitheatre, the Lanterne d’Auguste and the aqueduct arches.

How do you get to Saint-Raphaël?

By TGV or regional train to Saint-Raphaël-Valescure station, or on the A8.

Where can you swim near Saint-Raphaël?

In the coves along the Esterel corniche, at Agay or around Cap Dramont.

How long should you spend in Fréjus and Saint-Raphaël?

Allow a day.
