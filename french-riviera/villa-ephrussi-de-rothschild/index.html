Villa Ephrussi de Rothschild, the pink palace of Cap Ferrat

A Belle Époque palace between two bays, its art collections and nine themed gardens overlooking the Mediterranean.

The Villa Ephrussi de Rothschild stands on the narrowest part of the Cap Ferrat peninsula, between the bay of Villefranche and the bay of Beaulieu-sur-Mer. It now belongs to the Académie des beaux-arts.

You visit the ground and first floors of the villa, with their 18th-century art collections, then the gardens, the highlight of the visit. Allow about two hours.

A bit of history

Béatrice de Rothschild, daughter of the banker Alphonse de Rothschild and wife of Maurice Ephrussi, bought land on the Cap Ferrat isthmus in the early 20th century. Between 1905 and 1912 she built an Italian-style palace there to house her art collections.

She also created a series of themed gardens, which she had reshaped as she pleased. On her death in 1934 she bequeathed the villa and its collections to the Académie des beaux-arts, which opened it to the public.

Things to see at the Villa Ephrussi de Rothschild

The villa

An Italian-style Belle Époque palace, with its salons, 18th-century furniture and porcelain collections and the upstairs bedrooms. See tickets for the Villa Ephrussi de Rothschild

The French garden

The great central garden, with its pool and fountains, runs towards the sea like the deck of a ship.

The nine themed gardens

Spanish, Florentine, Japanese, exotic, rose garden… designed by Béatrice de Rothschild and listed as a Remarkable Garden.

Sea views

From the gardens you can see both the bay of Villefranche and the bay of Beaulieu.

The tea room

In season you can sit under the orange trees on the terrace.

How to get to the Villa Ephrussi de Rothschild

By bus

Bus no. 15 from Nice stops in front of the villa (Passable/Rothschild stop).

By car

About 25 minutes from Nice and 15 minutes from Villefranche; limited parking.

With a chauffeur

From Nice airport, allow about 30 to 40 minutes depending on traffic.

Practical info 2026: Villa Ephrussi de Rothschild

  • Prices: €18 full price, €12 reduced; free for under-7s.
  • Opening times: 1 February to 1 November, daily 10 am to 6 pm (7 pm in July and August); November to January, 10 am to 4.30 pm.
  • Evening openings: in July and August, candlelit evenings on Mondays and Tuesdays, €40 per adult.

Prices and opening times checked in November 2026 with official websites and local tourist offices; they may change, so check before you go.

Tips for visiting the Villa Ephrussi de Rothschild

  • Arrive at opening time to enjoy the gardens in peace.
  • Spend most of your time in the gardens.
  • Combine with the Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat coastal path or with Villefranche.
