Cap d’Antibes, a wild coastal path and Billionaires’ Bay

A peninsula of pines, rocks and villas between Antibes and Juan-les-Pins, best explored on foot along the coastal path.

Cap d’Antibes is the wooded peninsula jutting into the sea between Antibes and Juan-les-Pins. Behind the pines lie some of the finest estates on the Riviera.

The best way to see it is on foot, along the Tire-Poil coastal path, which follows the rocks between Garoupe beach and Billionaires’ Bay.

A bit of history

The Cap d’Antibes coastal path follows the old customs officers’ trail, once used to watch the coast. At the top of the cape, the Garoupe sanctuary houses Notre-Dame-de-Bon-Port, protector of Antibes’ sailors, its walls covered with ex-votos.

In the 19th century the cape filled with grand villas. Villa Eilenroc, completed in 1867, takes its name from an anagram of the first name of its owner’s wife, Cornélie.

Things to see on Cap d’Antibes

The Tire-Poil path

The old customs officers’ path runs along the southern tip of the cape above the rocks; its name is said to come from the wind that ruffles walkers’ hair. See activities on Cap d’Antibes

Billionaires’ Bay

The Anse de l’Argent Faux, with yachts at anchor, turquoise coves and villas in the pines.

Garoupe beach

A sandy beach in a sheltered cove, where the path begins.

The Garoupe plateau

At the highest point of the cape, the lighthouse and the Notre-Dame-de-Bon-Port chapel look out over Antibes and the coast.

Villa Eilenroc

A 19th-century villa and its 11-hectare park, open to the public on Saturdays.

How to get to Cap d’Antibes

By car

The Boulevard du Cap loops round the peninsula; park at Garoupe beach, where the path starts.

On foot

From Juan-les-Pins or central Antibes, allow about 45 minutes to an hour to walk to La Garoupe.

With a chauffeur

From Cannes or Nice airport, allow about 25 to 35 minutes depending on traffic.

Practical info 2026: Cap d’Antibes

  • Tire-Poil path: a loop of about 5 km, about 2 hours, from the Garoupe beach car park. It may be closed in bad weather or heavy swell.
  • Villa Eilenroc: open on Saturdays 10 am to 5 pm (last entry 4.30 pm), €2, free for under-12s; closed in August because of fire risk.
  • Path: not wheelchair accessible; wear good shoes and bring water.

Prices and opening times checked in November 2026 with official websites and local tourist offices; they may change, so check before you go.

Tips for visiting Cap d’Antibes

  • Start early in summer: the path is in full sun.
  • Walk from La Garoupe towards Billionaires’ Bay to finish facing the finest coves.
  • On Saturdays, combine the path with a visit to Villa Eilenroc.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the Cap d’Antibes coastal path?

The Tire-Poil loop is about 5 km and takes 1.5 to 2 hours.

Where do you park for the Cap d’Antibes path?

At the Garoupe beach car park, where the path starts.

How long should you spend on Cap d’Antibes?

Allow half a day.

What is there to see near Cap d’Antibes?
