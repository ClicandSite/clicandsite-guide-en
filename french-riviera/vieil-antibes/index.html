Old Antibes, between ramparts and Picasso

Ramparts lashed by the waves, a castle where Picasso painted facing the sea and a Provençal market under a covered hall: Antibes’ old town is best explored on foot.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 12 November 2026

Wrapped in its ramparts facing the Baie des Anges, Old Antibes is one of the best-preserved historic centres on the coast. Its flower-filled lanes lead from Port Vauban to the Château Grimaldi, perched above the waves.

Picasso set up his studio here in autumn 1946; the castle now houses the museum named after him. In the morning the Provençal market on Cours Masséna smells of olives and herbs, and the ramparts walk looks out as far as the snowy Alps in winter.

Things to see in Old Antibes

The Picasso Museum

The Château Grimaldi and the works Picasso painted in Antibes, with a terrace overlooking the sea. Book a guided tour of Old Antibes and its ramparts.

The ramparts walk

The path along the fortifications, facing the sea and, on clear days, the peaks of the Alps.

The Provençal market

The covered hall on Cours Masséna: fruit, olives, cheese and flowers in the morning.

The cathedral

The cathedral of Notre-Dame-de-l’Immaculée-Conception and its ochre façade, next to the castle.

La Gravette beach

A small sandy beach sheltered inside the ramparts, right in the old town.

How to get to Old Antibes

By train

Antibes station is about ten minutes’ walk from the old town, with frequent regional trains from Nice (about 20 minutes) and Cannes (about 10 minutes).

From Nice airport

About twenty kilometres; allow 25 to 45 minutes by car depending on traffic.

Practical info 2026: Old Antibes

  • Picasso Museum: closed on Mondays; check opening times before you go.
  • Provençal market: mornings, daily in season, and except Mondays the rest of the year.

Tips for visiting Old Antibes

  • Come in the morning for the market, then go up to the Picasso Museum.
  • Walk the ramparts at sunset.
  • Then head down to Port Vauban and its superyachts.

Frequently asked questions

What is there to see in Old Antibes?

The Picasso Museum, the ramparts walk, the Provençal market, the cathedral and La Gravette beach.

When did Picasso live in Antibes?

He worked at the Château Grimaldi in autumn 1946, painting many works there.

When is the Provençal market held?

In the morning, daily in season, and except Mondays the rest of the year.
