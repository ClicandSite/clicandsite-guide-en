Cours Saleya, the flower market of Old Nice

Flowers, fruit and vegetables under striped awnings, a baroque chapel and a flea market every Monday.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 13 November 2026

In the heart of Old Nice, Cours Saleya is one of the liveliest squares in town. Lined with cafés and restaurants, it is famous for its flower market.

From Tuesday to Sunday, flowers, fruit and growers’ vegetables set the rhythm of the mornings. On Mondays the square hosts a big flea market.

A bit of history

Cours Saleya runs along the seafront edge of Old Nice, at the foot of Castle Hill. In 1861, just after Nice became part of France, the flower, fruit and vegetable market was created here.

In the middle of the market stands the Miséricorde chapel, a listed baroque masterpiece. The flower market is now one of the best known in France.

Things to see at Cours Saleya

The flower market

One of France’s best-known markets, under colourful striped awnings. See food tours of Old Nice

Fruit and vegetables

Produce from growers in the hills behind Nice, next to the flowers.

The Monday flea market

Antique and second-hand dealers set out their wares along the whole square.

The Miséricorde chapel

A listed baroque chapel in the middle of the market.

The terraces

Cafés and restaurants all along the square, with Castle Hill as a backdrop.

How to get to Cours Saleya

On foot

From Place Masséna, allow 5 minutes; the Promenade des Anglais is just behind.

By tram

Line 2, Opéra-Vieille Ville stop, a short walk away.

With a chauffeur

From Nice airport, allow about 20 minutes depending on traffic.

Practical info 2026: Cours Saleya and the flower market

  • Flower and fresh produce market: Tuesday to Sunday, mornings; closed on Mondays.
  • Flea market: every Monday 7 am to 6 pm, and also on Place Garibaldi on the third Saturday of the month.
  • Entry: free; the square is pedestrianised.

Prices and opening times checked in November 2026 with official websites and local tourist offices; they may change, so check before you go.

Tips for visiting Cours Saleya

  • Come in the morning for the market, then climb Castle Hill.
  • Try socca, Nice’s chickpea pancake, from the stalls or nearby streets.
  • On Mondays, come for the flea market rather than the flowers.

Frequently asked questions

When is the Cours Saleya flower market?

Tuesday to Sunday, in the morning. On Mondays it is replaced by the flea market.

When is the Cours Saleya flea market?

Every Monday from 7 am to 6 pm.

How long should you spend at Cours Saleya?

Allow about 1 hour.

What is there to see near Cours Saleya?

In this guide, the closest places are Vieux-Nice (about 5 min on foot), Castle Hill (Colline du Château) (about 5 min on foot), Place Masséna and the Carré d’Or (about 10 min on foot).
