Forville is Cannes’ big covered market, in a 1930s hall at the foot of Le Suquet, a short walk from the Old Port.

This is where locals and the town’s chefs shop: growers from the hinterland, fishmongers, cheese sellers and flower stalls. On Mondays the food stalls give way to a flea market.

A bit of history

Forville market replaced Cannes’ open-air market in the 19th century. The current pink hall with its tiled roofs dates from the 1930s.

It has remained the town’s food hub, at the foot of Le Suquet, and hosts a flea market every Monday.

Things to see at Forville market

The growers’ stalls

Seasonal fruit and vegetables, herbs, olive oil and flowers, often sold by the people who grow them. See food tours in Cannes

The fish stalls

Mediterranean fish and shellfish, one of the market’s highlights.

The Monday flea market

Furniture, lamps, crockery and old books replace the food stalls.

The hall

A pink 1930s market hall, with the Porte du Poussiat on the Suquet side.

The neighbourhood

Café terraces around the market, Rue Meynadier and the climb up to Le Suquet.

How to get to Forville market

On foot

From Cannes station, allow about ten minutes; the Old Port is right next door.

By bus

Palm Bus services stop at the Old Port and the town hall, close by.

With a chauffeur

From Nice airport, allow about 35 to 45 minutes depending on traffic.

Practical info 2026: Forville market

  • Food market: Tuesday to Friday 7 am to 1 pm; Saturday, Sunday and in summer 7 am to 1.30 pm (Cannes town hall times).
  • Flea market: every Monday, in the hall.
  • Address: Rue du Marché Forville, at the foot of Le Suquet.

Prices and opening times checked in November 2026 with official websites and local tourist offices; they may change, so check before you go.

Tips for visiting Forville market

  • Come early, before 10 am: the best time, before the crowds and the heat.
  • Then climb Le Suquet for the view over the bay.
  • On Mondays, don’t come for fresh produce: it’s flea-market day.

Frequently asked questions

What are Forville market’s opening times?

Tuesday to Friday 7 am to 1 pm, weekends and summer 7 am to 1.30 pm. On Mondays it’s a flea market.

Where is Forville market?

Rue du Marché Forville, at the foot of Le Suquet, near Cannes’ Old Port.

How long should you spend at Forville market?

Allow 45 minutes to 1 hour.

What is there to see near Forville market?
