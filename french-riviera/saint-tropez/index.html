Visit Saint-Tropez

A former fishing village turned legend. Out of season Saint-Tropez is a little Provençal port again; in summer, arrive by sea.

By Anthony, Cannes · Updated 12 November 2026

Saint-Tropez is first and foremost a harbour: colourful façades, terraces and yachts at the foot of the old town. Behind it, the lanes of La Ponche, the fishermen’s quarter, lead down to a small beach.

The peninsula is famous for its beaches, especially Pampelonne, in the neighbouring town of Ramatuelle. In July and August the access road is one of the most congested in France: the boat changes everything.

Things to do in Saint-Tropez

The Old Port

Saint-Tropez’s non-stop show. Go early in the morning to see it before the crowds, or at aperitif time for the atmosphere.

The Citadel

The 17th-century fortress on the hill houses the maritime history museum. The view over the gulf and the town is the best in Saint-Tropez.

Place des Lices market

Tuesday and Saturday mornings under the plane trees: Provençal produce, flowers, clothes. The locals’ meeting place.

The Annonciade museum

A former chapel on the harbour, housing paintings by the artists who loved Saint-Tropez, from Signac to Matisse.

La Ponche

The old fishermen’s quarter with its lanes and small beach, a stone’s throw from the harbour.

Pampelonne beaches

Several kilometres of sand in Ramatuelle, with public stretches and private beach clubs.

How to get to Saint-Tropez

By boat

The best option in season. Shuttles link Saint-Tropez with Sainte-Maxime all year round, and in summer with Saint-Raphaël, Cannes and Nice. You land right in the harbour.

By bus

There is no train station in Saint-Tropez. Regional buses leave from Saint-Raphaël and Toulon, but get stuck in summer traffic.

By car or with a driver

Out of season the drive is pleasant. In July-August expect hours of traffic at the entrance to the peninsula: leave very early or come back late.

Tips for visiting Saint-Tropez

  • Come on a Tuesday or Saturday to enjoy the Place des Lices market.
  • In summer, take the boat from Sainte-Maxime or Cannes: faster and far more pleasant.
  • Climb to the Citadel late in the afternoon for the light over the gulf.

Frequently asked questions

How do you get to Saint-Tropez without a car?

The easiest way is the boat, from Sainte-Maxime all year round or from Cannes, Nice and Saint-Raphaël in season. There is no train station.

What day is the Saint-Tropez market?

The Place des Lices market is held on Tuesday and Saturday mornings.

Is Pampelonne beach in Saint-Tropez?

It is in the neighbouring town of Ramatuelle, a few kilometres from the centre of Saint-Tropez.
