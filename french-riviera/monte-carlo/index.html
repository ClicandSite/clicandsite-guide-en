Monte-Carlo, the casino district

A Belle Époque casino, luxury cars outside the Hôtel de Paris and gardens facing the sea: Monte-Carlo is Monaco’s most famous showcase.

By Anthony, Cannes · Updated 12 November 2026

Monte-Carlo grew up around the casino the princely family opened in the 19th century to rescue Monaco’s finances. Today’s building, with the opera house designed by Charles Garnier, overlooks a square full of grand hotels, luxury shops and classic cars.

You can enjoy the district without gambling: admire the casino atrium, have a coffee on a terrace facing the Hôtel de Paris, stroll through the gardens down to the sea, then head for the Japanese garden and Larvotto beach.

Things to see in Monte-Carlo

The casino

The atrium and Belle Époque gaming rooms, open to visitors during the day. Book a tour of Monte-Carlo and its casino.

Place du Casino

The Hôtel de Paris, the Café de Paris and the parade of luxury cars.

The Monte-Carlo Opera

The Salle Garnier, a red-and-gold jewel built into the casino.

The gardens

The casino gardens and their mirrored sphere, then terraces with sea views.

The Japanese garden

A peaceful Zen garden by the sea, near the Grimaldi Forum.

Larvotto

Monaco’s redeveloped public beach, with its promenade and restaurants.

How to get to Monte-Carlo

By train

Regional trains from Nice take about twenty minutes to Monaco-Monte-Carlo station, then free public lifts and escalators take over.

By car

There are plenty of public car parks but they are expensive; a chauffeur transfer saves looking for a space.

Practical info 2026: Monte-Carlo

  • Monaco is a sovereign state: there are no border checks from France and the euro is the currency, but carry photo ID.
  • Casino: paid visits during the day; the gaming rooms are adults-only with photo ID and smart dress in the evening.

Tips for visiting Monte-Carlo

  • Visit the casino in the morning, before gaming starts.
  • Have a coffee at the Café de Paris to watch the square.
  • Then go up to the Rock for the changing of the guard.

Frequently asked questions

Can you visit the Monte-Carlo casino without gambling?

Yes, the atrium and salons can be visited during the day with a ticket.

How old do you have to be to enter the casino?

You must be an adult and show photo ID.

How do you get to Monte-Carlo from Nice?

By regional train in about twenty minutes to Monaco station, by bus or with a private transfer.
