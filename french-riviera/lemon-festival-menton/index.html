Menton Lemon Festival 2027

Floats and giant sculptures made of lemons and oranges, at the foot of the mountains: the 93rd Lemon Festival runs from 13 to 28 February 2027 on the theme “Douce France”.

By Anthony, Cannes · Updated 12 November 2026

Every winter Menton covers its gardens and floats with lemons and oranges. The Lemon Festival draws over 200,000 visitors and was added to France’s inventory of intangible cultural heritage in 2019.

The 93rd edition, “Douce France”, celebrates France’s regions and traditions. It runs from Saturday 13 to Sunday 28 February 2027, with three highlights: the golden fruit parades on Sundays, the night parades on Thursday evenings and the citrus displays in the Biovès gardens.

The 2027 Lemon Festival programme

The golden fruit parades

The citrus floats parade along the Promenade du Soleil on Sundays 14, 21 and 28 February 2027, from 2:30 to 4pm. Seats cost €30 in the stands, €16 standing.

The night parades

The floats parade after dark on Thursdays 18 and 25 February 2027, from 9pm, with music and lights.

The citrus displays

The giant sculptures of the Biovès gardens, open daily from 13 to 28 February, 9am to 6pm. Entry €12 for adults, €7 for children.

Menton around the festival

The stepped old town, the Basilica of Saint-Michel and the seafront, a few minutes’ walk from the gardens.

How to get to the Lemon Festival

By train

TER trains on the Nice–Ventimiglia line stop in Menton, a few minutes’ walk from the Biovès gardens. The easiest option on parade days.

By car

Traffic is very heavy on parade days. The organisers advise arriving in Menton before 11am.

With a driver

A driver drops you off and picks you up near the route, with no parking to find: handy for families or from Nice, Cannes or Monaco.

Tips for the Lemon Festival

  • Book grandstand seats early: the best Sunday seats go quickly.
  • Visit the Biovès gardens on a weekday morning, before the groups arrive.
  • Bring a jacket: February night parades are chilly.
  • Dogs are not allowed in the parade area.

Frequently asked questions

When is the 2027 Lemon Festival?

From Saturday 13 to Sunday 28 February 2027 in Menton, on the theme “Douce France”.

How much is a parade ticket?

For the golden fruit parades, allow €30 in the stands and €16 standing. The Biovès gardens display costs €12 for adults and €7 for children.

Should I drive?

Better not: traffic is very heavy on parade days. The train to Menton station is the easiest way.
