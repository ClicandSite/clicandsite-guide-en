Visit Villefranche-sur-Mer

One of the most beautiful bays in the Mediterranean, a village of ochre and pink façades, and a fishermen’s chapel decorated entirely by Cocteau.

A few minutes from Nice, Villefranche-sur-Mer rises above a deep natural harbour sheltered by Cap Ferrat. The colourful houses run down to the fishing port, where you can have a coffee by the water.

The town is small and can be seen on foot in a few hours, which makes it an ideal stop to combine with Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat or Monaco.

Things to do in Villefranche-sur-Mer

The Saint-Pierre chapel

A small fishermen’s chapel on the harbour, decorated entirely by Jean Cocteau in the late 1950s: frescoes, colours and symbols. Book a guided tour or activity in Villefranche-sur-Mer

Rue Obscure

A street entirely covered by medieval vaults. Cool and mysterious, it runs through the old town.

The Saint-Elme citadel

A 16th-century fortress now home to the town hall, gardens and several small museums.

The harbour and seafront

Terraces and colourful boats at the foot of the old town, the most photographed spot in Villefranche.

Marinières beach

A fine sandy beach, rare on this part of the coast, right below the station.

How to get to Villefranche-sur-Mer

By train

Villefranche-sur-Mer station is on the beach, about ten minutes from Nice by TER. By far the easiest option.

By bus

Buses link Nice with Villefranche and continue to Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat.

Practical info 2026: Villefranche-sur-Mer

  • By train: TER Nice–Menton, under half an hour from Nice; the station is by the water, a short walk from the beach and the old town.
  • Citadelle Saint-Elme: free entry; gardens and ramparts open on weekdays 10am–5pm in winter. The museum rooms are closed for renovation.

Prices and opening hours checked in November 2026 with tourist offices and the sites themselves; they can change, so check before you go.

Tips for visiting Villefranche-sur-Mer

  • Cruise ships anchor in the bay: on those days the town is busy around midday.
  • Combine Villefranche in the morning with Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat in the afternoon.
  • Take the train rather than the car: parking is scarce and expensive.

Frequently asked questions

How do you get from Nice to Villefranche-sur-Mer?

The TER train takes about ten minutes and the station is on the beach. Buses run too.

Is there a sandy beach in Villefranche?

Yes, Marinières beach, right below the station, has fine sand.

Can you visit the Cocteau chapel?

Yes, it is open for much of the year for a small fee. Check opening days before you go.

How long should you spend in Villefranche-sur-Mer?

Allow half a day.

What is there to see near Villefranche-sur-Mer?

In this guide, the closest places are Port Lympia (about 3 km away), Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat (about 3 km away) and Vieux-Nice (about 4 km away).
