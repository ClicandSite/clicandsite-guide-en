The Val de Siagne loop at Le Tignet

A loop of about 7 km through the woods, along the meanders of the Siagne, on the border with the Var.

The Val de Siagne loop is a hiking trail at Le Tignet, in the Grasse area. It goes down to the Siagne, the river that here separates the Alpes-Maritimes from the Var.

The trail follows the old Saint-Cassien country path in the shade of oak trees and runs along the river’s meanders. A cool walk, ideal in warm weather.

A bit of history

The Siagne flows down from the hills above Grasse and reaches the sea at Mandelieu-la-Napoule. For part of its course, it marks the border between the Alpes-Maritimes and the Var.

The route

  1. Start from the hikers’ car park of the Val du Tignet.
  2. Go down the old Saint-Cassien country path to the banks of the Siagne.
  3. Follow the river’s meanders, pass near the Pont de Lignière, then climb back to the car park to close the loop.

Allow 2 hours 30 minutes to 3 hours for a loop of about 7 km. It is route no. 38 in the Randoxygène guide for the Grasse area.

What to see on the hike

The Siagne meanders

The river winds through a wooded valley, with pebble banks and green water.

The old Saint-Cassien path

An old country path lined with stone walls, which once linked the hamlets of the valley.

The Pont de Lignière

A bridge over the Siagne, between the Alpes-Maritimes and the Var.

How to get to the start

By car

Le Tignet is about 15 minutes from Grasse and 30 minutes from Cannes on the Draguignan road (D2562). The walk starts at the Val du Tignet hikers’ car park.

By bus

Sillages buses serve Le Tignet from Grasse; you then walk to the start.

Practical info 2026: Val de Siagne

  • Access: free trail, open all year.
  • River: watch the level of the Siagne: it can rise quickly after rain or water releases.
  • Map: route no. 38 in the Randoxygène guide for the Grasse area (Alpes-Maritimes department).

Information checked in November 2026 with the Alpes-Maritimes department and the Pays de Grasse tourist office; it may change, so check before you go.

Tips for the hike

  • Keep away from the river when it is in flood.
  • Wear good shoes: the way down to the Siagne is steep in places.
  • It is a good summer walk: the trail is mostly shaded.
  • Bring a picnic for a break by the water.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the Val de Siagne loop?

Allow 2 hours 30 minutes to 3 hours for a loop of about 7 km.

Where does the loop start?

At the Val du Tignet hikers’ car park, in Le Tignet near Grasse.

Can you swim in the Siagne?

The trail runs along the river, but be careful: the level can rise quickly after rain.

What is there to see near the Val de Siagne?

In this guide, the closest places are Grasse (about 15 min by car), Mougins (about 25 min by car) and Cannes (about 30 min by car).
