Port-Cros, the heart of the national park

A car-free island covered in forest, surrounded by waters protected since 1963: the wildest of the Golden Isles.

Port-Cros, off Hyères, is the heart of the Port-Cros National Park, created on 14 December 1963. It is the first marine national park in Europe: protection extends to the waters around the island.

No cars, no bikes: you land in a small harbour and set off on foot along waymarked trails to beaches, forts and wooded valleys.

What to see on Port-Cros

La Palud beach

The island’s best-known beach, about 45 minutes’ walk from the harbour, with a marked underwater trail for snorkelling in summer.

The forts

Fort de l’Estissac, which hosts exhibitions in season, and Fort du Moulin above the harbour.

Vallon de la Solitude

A shady trail through the heart of the island, among holm oaks and pines.

The village and harbour

A few colourful houses, a church and the National Park house.

How to get to Port-Cros

From Hyères

TLV-TVM boats all year from the port of Hyères (Port Saint-Pierre), in about an hour. Note that La Tour Fondue only serves Porquerolles.

From Le Lavandou

In season, a crossing of about 30 minutes.

On the island

On foot only: wear good shoes.

Practical info 2026: Port-Cros

  • Island access: free; only the boat crossing is paid.
  • Park rules: no fires, no camping, no picking or collecting; smoking is banned outside the village.
  • Services: a few restaurants and a shop near the harbour, often closed out of season.

Information checked in November 2026 with the Port-Cros National Park and the ferry companies; it may change, so check before you go.

Tips for visiting Port-Cros

  • Bring water and a picnic, especially out of season.
  • Pack a mask and snorkel for the La Palud underwater trail in summer.
  • Check the time of the last boat back before setting off on a walk.
  • In a strong mistral, crossings may be cancelled.

Frequently asked questions

How do you get to Port-Cros?

By boat, all year from the port of Hyères (about 1 hour) and in season from Le Lavandou (about 30 minutes).

Can you swim on Port-Cros?

Yes, especially at La Palud beach, where an underwater trail is marked out in summer.

Can you smoke on Port-Cros?

No, smoking is banned outside the village to prevent fires.
