Nice Carnival 2027

The French Riviera’s biggest winter event returns to Place Masséna from 9 to 28 February 2027, on the theme “Vive l’Amour”.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 12 November 2026

The Nice Carnival is France’s first international carnival. In 2026 it drew over 420,000 people, including 226,000 spectators for the parades and flower battles.

The 2027 edition runs from 9 to 28 February on the theme “Vive l’Amour” (Long Live Love). It all happens around Place Masséna, with two main events: the flower parades in the afternoon and the illuminated carnival parades at night.

A bit of history

Traces of a carnival in Nice go back to the late 13th century. Its modern form was born in 1873, when a festival committee organised the first parade of floats led by His Majesty Carnival.

The flower battles, launched soon after, drew the wealthy winter visitors of the Riviera. Today it is one of the biggest carnivals in the world.

The 2027 Nice Carnival programme

The flower parades

Flower-covered floats parade past while performers throw flowers to the crowd, on Wednesdays 10, 17 and 24 and Saturdays 13, 20 and 27 February 2027, from 2:30 to 4pm. Free for under-6s.

The illuminated parades

The giant floats of the King, the Queen and the big heads parade at night on Place Masséna, with music and lights. The first is on Saturday 13 February at 9pm.

The “Vive l’Amour” theme

Floats, costumes and street shows explore love in all its forms, in the centre and the neighbourhoods.

Old Nice

A short walk from Place Masséna, Cours Saleya and its lanes are perfect for a break between parades.

How to get to the Nice Carnival

By train

Nice-Ville station is about 15 minutes’ walk from Place Masséna. Regional trains run frequently between Cannes and Menton, with extra trains on night-parade evenings.

By tram

Masséna stop, line 1, right on the route.

With a driver

From Cannes, Antibes or Monaco, a driver drops you near the centre and waits for you after the night parade.

Tips for the Nice Carnival

  • For grandstand seats, book as soon as ticket sales open: the best seats go fast.
  • The flower parades are ideal with children; the night parades are livelier and more crowded.
  • Arrive early: access to the route is through controlled entrances, with bag checks.
  • Parades can be cancelled in bad weather.
