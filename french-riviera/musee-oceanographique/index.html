Founded by Prince Albert I, a great ocean explorer, the Oceanographic Museum clings to the cliff of the Rock, a short walk from the Prince’s Palace.

It combines an aquarium of about a hundred tanks, including a large shark tank, with rooms on the exploration and protection of the sea. Allow at least two hours.

A bit of history

Prince Albert I of Monaco, passionate about oceanography, led many scientific expeditions at sea. He had this “palace of the sea” built on the cliff of the Rock to display his collections.

The museum, opened in 1910, belongs to the Oceanographic Institute, a foundation created by the prince. It remains a place for discovering and protecting the ocean.

Things to see at the Oceanographic Museum

The aquarium

About a hundred tanks, from Mediterranean species to tropical fish. See tickets for the Oceanographic Museum

The shark lagoon

The big tank where sharks swim, one of the highlights of the visit.

The museum rooms

Prince Albert I’s collections and the history of ocean exploration.

The rooftop terrace

Views over the sea, Monaco and the coast, with a restaurant.

The sea façade

The palace drops straight into the cliff above the Mediterranean.

How to get to the Oceanographic Museum

On foot

From the Prince’s Palace, allow about ten minutes through the lanes of the Rock.

By car

The Pêcheurs car park, below the museum, is linked by lift.

With a chauffeur

From Nice airport, allow about 30 to 45 minutes depending on traffic.

Practical info 2026: Oceanographic Museum of Monaco

  • Opening times: open daily except the Grand Prix weekend and 25 December; 10 am to 6 pm January to March and October to December, later in spring and summer.
  • 2026 prices: adult ticket about €19 to €22 depending on the season; free for under-4s. Combined tickets with the Prince’s Palace or the Exotic Garden.
  • Address: Avenue Saint-Martin, on the Rock.

Prices and opening times checked in November 2026 with official websites and local tourist offices; they may change, so check before you go.

Tips for visiting the Oceanographic Museum

  • Buy tickets online in summer to skip the queue.
  • Combine with the changing of the guard at the Prince’s Palace at 11.55 am.
  • Go up to the rooftop terrace before you leave.
