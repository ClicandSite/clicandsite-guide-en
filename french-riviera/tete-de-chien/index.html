La Turbie

The Tête de Chien loop above Monaco

A 7.5 km loop from La Turbie, along the white cliffs that tower over the Principality.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 15 November 2026

Tête de Chien is the rocky headland that towers over Monaco, more than 500 metres above the sea. Its white limestone cliffs can be seen from all over the Rock.

The trail starts from the village of La Turbie and loops around the headland. The view drops onto Port Hercule, Fontvieille and Cap d’Ail, and on a clear day reaches Saint-Tropez.

A bit of history

The summit is occupied by the Fort de la Tête de Chien, a military site closed to the public. The trail goes around it without entering.

La Turbie, at the start, is known for the Trophy of Augustus, a Roman monument built above the sea in the 1st century BC.

The route

  1. Start on Avenue de la Pinède in La Turbie. Follow the waymarks for the Tête de Chien loop.
  2. The trail follows the edge of the cliffs, with Monaco below, then goes round the fort on its south side.
  3. The way back crosses the plateau through the scrubland to La Turbie.

Allow about 2 hours for 7.5 km and nearly 480 metres of total climb. The high point is at 548 metres. The difficulty is medium: the town recommends it for fit walkers.

What to see on the hike

The view over Monaco

The trail overlooks the whole Principality, from the Rock to Monte-Carlo. It is one of the best viewpoints over Monaco.

The Tête de Chien cliffs

Tall walls of white limestone straight above Cap d’Ail and Monaco.

The coast to Cap Ferrat

To the west, the view runs to Èze, Cap Ferrat and, on a clear day, the Maures hills.

How to get to the start

By bus

ZOU bus 603 from Nice (Vauban bus station) and 604 or 605 from Monaco (Place d’Armes) stop at “La Turbie Mairie”.

By car

La Turbie is on the Grande Corniche and has its own exit on the A8 motorway. Park in the village and walk to Avenue de la Pinède.

Practical info 2026: Tête de Chien

  • Access: free trail, open all year.
  • Warning: part of the trail, between waymarks 605 and 606, has been reported closed by the Alpes-Maritimes department; check randoxygene.departement06.fr before you go.
  • Fort de la Tête de Chien: military site, closed to the public.

Information checked in November 2026 with the town of La Turbie and the Alpes-Maritimes department; it may change, so check before you go.

Tips for the hike

  • Wear good shoes: the trail is stony.
  • Take water and a hat: there is very little shade.
  • Avoid very windy days: the trail runs along the cliff edge.
  • Go late in the day for the light over Monaco.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the Tête de Chien loop?

Allow about 2 hours for the 7.5 km loop from La Turbie, with nearly 480 metres of total climb.

Can you visit the Fort de la Tête de Chien?

No, it is a military site closed to the public. The trail goes around it.

How do you get to La Turbie without a car?

By ZOU bus: 603 from Nice, 604 and 605 from Monaco, stop “La Turbie Mairie”.

What is there to see near Tête de Chien?

In this guide, the closest places are Monaco (about 15 min by car), Cime de la Forna (starts from La Turbie) and Èze (about 15 min by car).
