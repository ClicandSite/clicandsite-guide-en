Clinging to the cliff above Fontvieille, the Exotic Garden is one of Monaco’s best-known sights. Opened in 1933, it displays succulents and cacti from all over the world.

A bit of history

Conceived at the end of the 19th century, Monaco’s Exotic Garden was opened in 1933 on the cliff above Fontvieille.

Closed in 2020 for safety and modernisation works, it reopened on 30 March 2026, after an inauguration attended by Prince Albert II.

Things to see at the Exotic Garden

Cacti and succulents

One of the world’s great collections of succulents, with astonishing shapes. See Monaco tours

The panorama

A plunging view over the Rock, Fontvieille harbour, Monaco and the coast.

Cliffside paths

Paths and steps cut into the rock.

The Observatory Cave

A cave next to the garden; check on site, as it was closed for a long time.

The Rock opposite

The Prince’s Palace and the Oceanographic Museum, across the harbour.

How to get to the Exotic Garden

By bus

Monaco buses serve Boulevard du Jardin-Exotique.

On foot

From the Rock or Fontvieille, allow about twenty minutes uphill.

With a chauffeur

From Nice airport, allow about 30 to 45 minutes depending on traffic.

Practical info 2026: Monaco Exotic Garden

  • Reopening: the garden reopened to the public on 30 March 2026, after six years of works.
  • Opening times: 9 am to 6 pm at reopening; current times and prices on the garden’s official website.
  • Access: Boulevard du Jardin-Exotique, on the heights of Monaco.

Prices and opening times checked in November 2026 with official websites and local tourist offices; they may change, so check before you go.

Tips for visiting the Exotic Garden

  • Come on a clear day to see as far as Italy.
  • Wear good shoes: the garden is all slopes and steps.
  • Combine with the Rock and the Prince’s Palace on the same day.
