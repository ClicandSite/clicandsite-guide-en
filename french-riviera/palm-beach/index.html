Palm Beach, at the end of the Croisette

An Art Deco and Hispano-Moorish façade, palm trees and views of the Lérins Islands: Cannes’ old summer casino shines again on Pointe Croisette.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 12 November 2026

Opened in 1929 on Pointe Croisette, Palm Beach was Cannes’ summer casino, a magnet for the jet set and royalty. After five years of work the building reopened in 2024, with its Art Deco and Hispano-Moorish façade restored and its seawater pool recreated.

Today the complex brings together restaurants, a private beach, a casino, a members’ club and a large event hall. Even without going in, the point is worth the walk: it is the end of the Croisette, facing the Lérins Islands, next to Port Pierre-Canto and its sailing boats.

A bit of history

The Palm Beach opened in 1929 at the tip of La Croisette as Cannes’ first summer casino, for the smart set, who were then starting to come to the Riviera in summer and not only in winter.

With its pool and parties it became one of Cannes’ society spots. After several years of renovation it has reopened with a beach, pool, restaurants and casino.

Things to see at Palm Beach

The façade

The steps, palm trees and white spires of the old casino, restored to their original look. Book a boat trip in the bay of Cannes

Pointe Croisette

Views from Cap d’Antibes to the Esterel, with Sainte-Marguerite and Saint-Honorat islands just opposite.

Port Pierre-Canto

The neighbouring marina, quieter than the Old Port, ideal for a stroll along the jetties.

The beaches at the point

Private and public beaches between Palm Beach and Port Canto.

Restaurants and casino

Several chef-led restaurants and big international names, plus the casino, open in the evening.

How to get to Palm Beach

On foot

About 35 to 40 minutes’ walk from the Palais des Festivals along the whole Croisette.

By bus

Town buses link the centre and the station with Pointe Croisette.

Practical info 2026: Palm Beach

  • Works on the Croisette: the boulevard is being redeveloped in stages from 2026 until around 2030. Traffic and parking may be disrupted: come by train (the station is a five-minute walk away) or use the car parks in the town centre. The easiest option is to book a private chauffeur transfer, which drops you at your hotel door with no parking to find.
  • Access: book the restaurants and beach ahead, especially in season and during the Festival; the casino is adults-only with photo ID.

Tips for visiting Palm Beach

  • Come at sunset, when the Esterel stands out opposite the point.
  • Combine with a boat trip to the Lérins Islands.
  • Walk back along the Croisette to the Palais des Festivals.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Palm Beach in Cannes?

On Pointe Croisette, at the very end of the Boulevard de la Croisette, facing the Lérins Islands.

When did Palm Beach open?

The casino opened in 1929; the renovated complex reopened in 2024.

What is at Palm Beach today?

Restaurants, a private beach, a casino, a seawater pool, a members’ club and an event hall.

How long should you spend at Palm Beach?

Allow one to two hours.
