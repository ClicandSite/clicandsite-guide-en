The Cannes Film Festival 2027, 80th edition

From 11 to 22 May 2027, Cannes celebrates 80 years of its festival. It is for professionals, but you can still enjoy it from the Croisette, on the beach and in the streets.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 12 November 2026

The 80th Cannes Film Festival will be held from 11 to 22 May 2027 at the Palais des Festivals, slightly earlier than usual, for an anniversary edition. Official selection screenings are reserved for accredited professionals and guests.

The public is not left out: you can watch the red carpet from the barriers on the Croisette, the Cinéma de la Plage shows open-air films every evening, and the whole town lives to the rhythm of the festival for twelve days.

Things to see during the Festival

The red carpet

Film teams arrive in the late afternoon and evening, best seen from around the Palais. Book a tour of Cannes and the Croisette

The Cinéma de la Plage

Free open-air screenings on Macé beach, subject to available seats.

The Croisette in full swing

Decorated grand hotels, busy terraces and a lively atmosphere late into the night.

Invitations for the public

Seats are sometimes handed out for certain screenings; young film fans can apply for the “3 jours à Cannes” programme.

How to get to the Cannes Film Festival

By train

Cannes station is a five-minute walk from the Palais des Festivals, the easiest option during the festival.

From Nice airport

About 30 km; allow plenty of time, as traffic is very heavy during the festival.

Practical info 2026: Cannes Film Festival 2027

  • Traffic: the area around the Palais is closed and the Croisette is gridlocked. A private chauffeur for the Cannes Film Festival saves looking for parking.
  • Accommodation: hotels fill up and prices rise sharply; book months ahead or stay in Antibes, Mandelieu or Nice.

Tips for visiting the Cannes Film Festival

  • Get to the barriers early to see the red carpet.
  • Come midweek rather than the last weekend, the busiest.
  • Take time to climb up to Le Suquet, which is calmer.

Frequently asked questions

When is the Cannes Film Festival 2027?

From 11 to 22 May 2027, for its 80th edition.

Can the public attend the Cannes Film Festival?

Official screenings are for professionals and guests, but the Cinéma de la Plage is free and open to all.

Where can you see the stars in Cannes?

Along the barriers on the Croisette, in front of the Palais des Festivals, during the red carpet arrivals.
