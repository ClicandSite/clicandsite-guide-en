The Monaco Grand Prix 2027

From 4 to 6 June 2027, Formula 1 returns to the streets of the Principality, with a Saturday sprint race for the first time.

The 2027 Monaco Grand Prix will be held from 4 to 6 June as the eighth round of the season. The big news: Monaco will host a sprint race for the first time, on top of the Sunday Grand Prix.

The circuit runs through the streets of the Principality, from Port Hercule to the casino and through the tunnel. All weekend long, Monaco lives to the sound of engines, yachts moored in the port and packed terraces.

A bit of history

The first Monaco Grand Prix was held on 14 April 1929, created by Antony Noghès of the Automobile Club de Monaco. It was won by William Grover-Williams in a Bugatti.

The race joined the Formula One World Championship when it began in 1950. Its route through the streets of the principality has changed little since the start.

Things to see during the Grand Prix

The schedule

Friday: practice and sprint qualifying; Saturday: sprint and qualifying; Sunday: the Grand Prix. Book a tour of Monaco and the circuit

The grandstands

Paid grandstands around the port, the swimming pool and La Rascasse, to book very early.

The Rock

A paid access area on the Rock gives a bird’s-eye view of part of the circuit.

The circuit outside sessions

Outside sessions the circuit streets are often open, so you can walk the track.

How to get to the Monaco Grand Prix

By train

The train from Nice is the safest option; extra trains run but they are packed all weekend.

By car

Roads are closed and car parks full during sessions: get dropped off outside the circuit.

Tips for visiting the Monaco Grand Prix

  • Buy grandstand seats as soon as ticket sales open.
  • Bring ear protection, especially for children.
  • Get to Monaco before the first session to avoid the crowds.
