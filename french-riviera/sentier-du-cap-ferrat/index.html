The Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat coastal path

Around the peninsula on foot, at the edge of the rocks, between villas, a lighthouse and turquoise coves.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 15 November 2026

Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat is ringed by coastal paths. They go around the peninsula between Villefranche and Beaulieu, as close to the sea as possible.

There are three, which you can walk separately or in one go: the Promenade Maurice Rouvier from Beaulieu, the Pointe Saint-Hospice loop and the Cap Ferrat loop, the longest and wildest.

A bit of history

The Cap Ferrat lighthouse, on Pointe de Malalongue, has guided sailors since the 19th century. Destroyed in 1944, it was rebuilt after the war.

On Pointe Saint-Hospice stand a chapel and a large statue of the Virgin, visible from far out at sea.

The three paths

  1. Promenade Maurice Rouvier: 1.3 km (20 minutes) from the Baie des Fourmis in Beaulieu-sur-Mer to the port of Saint-Jean, past the villas.
  2. Pointe Saint-Hospice loop: 1.8 km (40 minutes) from the Jardin de la Paix, around the point and past Paloma beach.
  3. Cap Ferrat loop: 4.8 km (1 hour 30 minutes) from Chemin de la Carrière, past the lighthouse, to Passable beach.

Joined together, the three paths make about 8 km, or 3 hours on foot. The cape loop is the roughest, with steps and rocks. The paths are closed in bad weather.

What to see on the walk

The Cap Ferrat lighthouse

On Pointe de Malalongue, at the south of the peninsula, above the rocks.

Paloma beach

A small cove of white pebbles and clear water, facing Èze and Beaulieu.

Pointe Saint-Hospice

A chapel, a statue of the Virgin and a view of Monaco and Italy.

How to get to the start

By train

Beaulieu-sur-Mer station, on the Nice – Ventimiglia line: the Promenade Maurice Rouvier starts from the Baie des Fourmis, close by.

By car

Parking is limited on the peninsula. Suggested car parks: Place du Centenaire or the port beach (Cros deï Pin).

Practical info 2026: Cap Ferrat coastal path

  • Access: free, open all year.
  • Weather: the paths are closed in bad weather and rough seas.
  • Difficulty: easy for the Promenade Maurice Rouvier and Saint-Hospice, medium for the cape loop.

Information checked in November 2026 with the Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat tourist office; it may change, so check before you go.

Tips for the walk

  • Do not do the cape loop in heavy swell: waves sweep over the path.
  • Bring a swimsuit for Paloma or Passable beach.
  • Wear good shoes: the cape loop crosses rocks.
  • For a full day, add the Villa Ephrussi de Rothschild.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the Cap Ferrat coastal path?

The cape loop is 4.8 km (1 hour 30 minutes). With Pointe Saint-Hospice and the Promenade Maurice Rouvier, it comes to about 8 km and 3 hours.

Where does the Cap Ferrat loop start?

On Chemin de la Carrière in Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat; it ends at Passable beach.

Is the path open all year?

Yes, except in bad weather and rough seas.

What is there to see near the Cap Ferrat coastal path?

In this guide, the closest places are Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat (right there), Villa Ephrussi de Rothschild (about 5 min by car) and Beaulieu-sur-Mer (about 5 min by car).
