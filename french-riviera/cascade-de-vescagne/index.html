The Cascade de Vescagne hike

A 5 km out-and-back walk from the Col de Vence to a waterfall hidden in the Cagne valley.

Photo: Plyd, public domain, Wikimedia Commons

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 15 November 2026

The Cascade de Vescagne is hidden in the upper Cagne valley, in the commune of Coursegoules, north of Vence. You reach it on foot from the Col de Vence road.

The trail goes down into a wild valley, passes an old mine and fords the river before reaching the foot of the falls.

A bit of history

The valley was mined for lignite, a soft coal: the Vescagne mine worked until 1953. A few traces can still be seen along the trail.

The route

  1. Start from the small car park beside the D2, at waymark 143, on the Col de Vence road.
  2. Go down into the valley past the old lignite mine.
  3. Cross the Cagne on stepping stones, then follow the stream up to the waterfall. Return the same way.

Allow about 2 hours 20 minutes for 5 km out and back with 320 metres of climb. The difficulty is medium. The trail was damaged by storm Alex in 2020: some stretches are in poor condition.

What to see on the hike

The waterfall

A waterfall at the bottom of the valley, at its best after the spring and autumn rains.

The old Vescagne mine

Traces of the lignite workings, closed in 1953.

The Col de Vence

At the start, a limestone plateau with a wide view of the coast.

How to get to the start

By car

From Vence, take the D2 up towards the Col de Vence and Coursegoules. The car park is small, at waymark 143.

Without a car

No bus stops at the trailhead: plan for a car or a driver.

Practical info 2026: Cascade de Vescagne

  • Access: free, open all year.
  • Signal: no mobile phone signal in the valley.
  • Trail condition: damaged stretches since storm Alex; a ford over the Cagne, wet feet likely.

Information checked in November 2026; it may change, so check before you go.

Tips for the hike

  • Wear shoes that can get wet for the ford.
  • Do not go after heavy rain: the river may be impassable.
  • Tell someone your route, there is no signal.
  • Come in spring or autumn to see the waterfall flowing.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the Cascade de Vescagne hike?

Allow about 2 hours 20 minutes for 5 km out and back with 320 metres of climb.

Where do you park?

In a small car park beside the D2, at waymark 143, on the Col de Vence road.

Do you have to cross the river?

Yes, you cross the Cagne on stepping stones: wet feet are likely.
