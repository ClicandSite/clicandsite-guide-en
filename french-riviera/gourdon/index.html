Gourdon is listed among the Most Beautiful Villages in France. Clinging to a cliff, it overlooks the Gorges du Loup and offers one of the most impressive panoramas in the hinterland.

The village is tiny: a few lanes, craftspeople, sweet shops and a viewpoint square. You can see it in an hour, then carry on to the gorges and neighbouring villages.

Things to do in Gourdon

Place Victoria

The village viewpoint, on the edge of the cliff. On clear days the view takes in the whole coast. Book a guided tour or activity in Gourdon.

The lanes and craftspeople

Soap makers, perfumers, confectioners and glassblowers occupy the stone houses.

Gourdon castle

A medieval fortress above the village, surrounded by terraced gardens.

The Gorges du Loup

Below, the road runs through spectacular gorges, with the Courmes waterfall and the Saut du Loup.

Tourrettes-sur-Loup

The neighbouring hilltop village, famous for its violets and artists’ studios.

How to get to Gourdon

By car or with a driver

The only real option: Gourdon is about half an hour from Grasse on a mountain road. The car park is at the village entrance.

By bus

There is a service from Grasse, but it is infrequent: check times before you go.

Practical info 2026: Gourdon

  • By bus: regional Zou! line 651 (Grasse–Vence) serves the area and Pont-du-Loup, at the foot of the village; check times on zou.maregionsud.fr, buses are infrequent.
  • By car: the easiest way to reach Gourdon; combine it with Grasse, about twenty minutes away.

Tips for visiting Gourdon

  • Pick a clear day: the view is the main reason to come.
  • Do a loop: Grasse, Gourdon, Gorges du Loup, Tourrettes-sur-Loup, then back via Vence or Saint-Paul.
  • The road is winding: take care if you suffer from car sickness.

Frequently asked questions

Can you get to Gourdon without a car?

It is difficult: buses are rare. An organised tour or a private driver is more practical.

How long does it take to visit Gourdon?

An hour is enough for the village. Allow half a day with the Gorges du Loup and Tourrettes-sur-Loup.

Is Gourdon one of the Most Beautiful Villages in France?

Yes, Gourdon is a member of the Plus Beaux Villages de France association.
