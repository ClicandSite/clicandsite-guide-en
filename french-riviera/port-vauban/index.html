Antibes

Port Vauban, the superyacht harbour

Hundred-metre yachts, a 16th-century star-shaped fort and a statue made of letters facing the sea: Antibes’ harbour is a show in itself.

At the foot of Old Antibes, Port Vauban is one of the largest marinas in the Mediterranean. Its billionaires’ quay, along the jetty, hosts some of the biggest yachts in the world.

The harbour is overlooked by Fort Carré, a star-shaped fortress reworked by Vauban, and by Jaume Plensa’s Nomade statue, seated on the Saint-Jaume bastion. It is also where boat trips leave for Cap d’Antibes and the Lérins Islands.

A bit of history

Antibes harbour already existed in ancient times, in the days of Antipolis. As a border town, Antibes was fortified by Vauban in the late 17th century, and the port took his name.

Fort Carré, built in the 16th century and strengthened by Vauban, still guards the entrance. Today it is one of the largest marinas in the Mediterranean, with its superyacht quay.

Things to see at Port Vauban

The billionaires’ quay

The jetty lined with superyachts, best admired from the quayside. Book a boat trip from Antibes

Fort Carré

The 16th-century star-shaped fortress, open to visitors, with views over the port and bay.

The Nomade statue

Jaume Plensa’s giant of white letters, seated facing the sea on the Saint-Jaume bastion.

The quays

Cafés, fishing boats and harbour life at the foot of the ramparts.

How to get to Port Vauban

By train

Antibes station is about ten minutes’ walk from the old town, with frequent regional trains from Nice (about 20 minutes) and Cannes (about 10 minutes).

From Nice airport

About twenty kilometres; allow 25 to 45 minutes by car depending on traffic.

Practical info 2026: Port Vauban

  • Fort Carré: open to visitors, closed on Mondays; check opening times before you go.
  • Boat trips: in season, book the day before, especially for sunset.
  • Fort Carré: €5 full price, €3 reduced; closed on Mondays; 10 am to 5 pm from September to June, 10 am to 6 pm in July and August. It closes in bad weather; included in the €15 Antibes museum pass.

Tips for visiting Port Vauban

  • See the Nomade statue at sunset.
  • Walk round the port to Fort Carré, about twenty minutes.
  • Then climb back up into Old Antibes.

Frequently asked questions

What is the billionaires’ quay?

The jetty at Port Vauban reserved for the largest yachts, visible from the port.

Can you visit Fort Carré?

Yes, the fortress is open to visitors, except on Mondays.

Where do boats leave from in Antibes?

From Port Vauban, at the foot of the old town.

How long should you spend at Port Vauban?

Allow two hours, plus a boat trip.

What is there to see near Port Vauban?
