By the Clic&Site team · Updated 13 November 2026

Launched in 2006, Les Plages Électroniques has become one of France’s biggest open-air electronic music festivals. Every August, the Palais des Festivals beach in Cannes turns into a giant dance floor facing the sea.

The 2026 edition celebrated the festival’s 20th anniversary, from 7 to 9 August, with Martin Garrix, Marshmello and a day curated by DJ Snake. The next edition is announced for 6 to 8 August 2027.

A bit of history

Les Plages Électroniques was born in 2006 on the beaches of Cannes, with the idea of creating a big electronic music festival by the sea.

From a single beach night, it has grown to three days and several stages, from the sand up to the roof of the Palais des Festivals. In 2026 it celebrated its 20th edition.

Things to see at Les Plages Électroniques

The beach stage

The main stage with your feet in the sand, facing the bay of Cannes. See activities in Cannes

The Palais terrace

People also dance on the terrace and roof of the Palais des Festivals, overlooking the Croisette.

Several stages

Stages with different moods, from EDM to techno and house.

Sunset

The festival starts in the afternoon: the best moment is the evening, as the sun sets over the Estérel.

Cannes around it

The Palais is at the end of the Croisette, close to the Old Port and Le Suquet.

How to get to Les Plages Électroniques

By train

Cannes station is about ten minutes’ walk from the Palais des Festivals.

By car

Parking is very difficult in August: take the train or a chauffeur.

With a chauffeur

From Nice airport, allow about 35 to 45 minutes; also handy for getting back at the end of the night.

Practical info 2026: Les Plages Électroniques 2027

  • 2027 dates: announced for Friday 6 to Sunday 8 August 2027, to be confirmed on the festival’s official website.
  • Venue: Palais des Festivals beach and Riviera terrace, 1 boulevard de la Croisette, Cannes.
  • Tickets: on sale on the official website; in 2026 passes ranged from about €66 to €163. The first “blind passes” go on sale before the line-up is announced.

Prices and opening times checked in November 2026 with official websites and local tourist offices; they may change, so check before you go.

Tips for visiting Les Plages Électroniques

  • Buy tickets early: the best days sell out fast.
  • Book accommodation months ahead; August is peak season in Cannes.
  • Bring water, sunscreen and shoes that can handle sand.
