Visiting the Lérins Islands

Fifteen minutes by boat from the Croisette, two car-free islands: pine woods, coves, a fort where the Man in the Iron Mask was held, and an abbey where monks still live.

The Lérins Islands are the best escape from Cannes. Sainte-Marguerite, the larger one, is covered in forest and fringed with coves. Saint-Honorat, smaller, belongs to a community of monks who grow vines there.

There are no cars and no main roads: just paths, shade and very clear water. Plan the whole day and bring water and a picnic.

Things to do on the Lérins Islands

Fort Royal (Sainte-Marguerite)

Visit the cell of the mysterious Man in the Iron Mask, imprisoned under Louis XIV. The fort also houses the Musée de la Mer, with finds from ancient shipwrecks.

Walking around Sainte-Marguerite

A path follows the coast all the way round the island, with coves for swimming. The inland trail crosses a forest of pines and eucalyptus.

Lérins Abbey (Saint-Honorat)

The Cistercian monks welcome visitors in silence. You can visit the abbey church and the fortified monastery by the water.

The monks’ vineyards and wines

Saint-Honorat is covered in vines. The monks make a renowned wine and liqueurs, sold on the island.

How to get to the Lérins Islands

By boat from Cannes

Shuttles leave from Quai Laubeuf in Cannes harbour all year round. About fifteen minutes to Sainte-Marguerite, a little more to Saint-Honorat. The two islands are served separately: check before you go if you want to visit both.

From other ports

In season, boats also leave from Juan-les-Pins, Golfe-Juan and Nice, less frequently.

Tips for visiting the Lérins Islands

  • Take one of the first boats in the morning: you will have the coves to yourself.
  • Bring water and lunch; places to eat are limited.
  • On Saint-Honorat, calm is part of the experience: dress modestly and keep your voice down near the abbey.

Frequently asked questions

Can you visit both Lérins Islands in one day?

Yes, but they are served separately from Cannes. Check the crossings on the day and start early.

Can you swim on the Lérins Islands?

Yes, Sainte-Marguerite is fringed with coves of very clear water. There is no lifeguarded beach: take care with children.

Are there restaurants on the islands?

There are a few, often packed in summer. A picnic is the simplest option.
