Le Suquet, old Cannes

Above the Old Port, the cobbled lanes of Cannes’ original village climb to a medieval tower and the finest view over the bay.

Long before the Croisette and its grand hotels, Cannes was a fishing village huddled on Mont Chevalier. That is Le Suquet: a maze of lanes and steps climbing from the Old Port to the church and the castle of the Lérins monks.

It is still the most authentic part of town. People dine in the small restaurants of rue Saint-Antoine, shop at Forville market in the morning and climb the tower to take in the bay, the Lérins Islands and the Esterel.

A bit of history

Le Suquet is the birthplace of Cannes: in the Middle Ages the monks of the Lérins islands, lords of the area, built a castle here to watch over the coast. Its great square tower, completed in the 14th century, served as a lookout.

The church of Notre-Dame-d’Espérance dates from the 16th and 17th centuries. The castle now houses the Musée des Explorations du Monde.

Things to see in Le Suquet

Rue Saint-Antoine

The cobbled lane winding up to the top, lined with restaurants and terraces. Book a guided tour of Le Suquet and old Cannes

Notre-Dame-de-l’Espérance

The 17th-century church at the top, whose square is a lookout over the bay.

The tower and museum

In the old castle, the Musée des Explorations du Monde and the Suquet tower, with panoramic views.

Forville market

The covered market at the foot of the district: fruit, fish, flowers and local produce in the morning.

The Old Port

Fishing boats and yachts, and the quays where ferries leave for the islands.

How to get to Le Suquet

On foot

Le Suquet is a ten-minute walk from the station, just behind the Palais des Festivals; the streets are steep and cobbled.

By bus

Small town buses serve the bottom of the district, handy for avoiding the climb in high summer.

Tips for visiting Le Suquet

  • Go up late in the day, when the golden light over the bay is at its best.
  • Wear good shoes: the lanes are steep and cobbled.
  • Head back down to the Croisette for sunset.

Frequently asked questions

What is Le Suquet?

The historic quarter of Cannes, the old fishing village perched on Mont Chevalier above the Old Port.

How do you get up to Le Suquet?

On foot via rue Saint-Antoine or the steps from the Old Port, in ten to fifteen minutes.

When is Forville market held?

Tuesday to Sunday mornings; Monday is flea market day.

How long should you spend in Le Suquet?

Allow two to three hours.

What is there to see near Le Suquet?

In this guide, the closest places are Cannes (about 5 min on foot), La Croisette (about 25 min on foot) and Palm Beach (about 3 km away).
