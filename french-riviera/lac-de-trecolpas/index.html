The Lac de Trécolpas hike in the Mercantour

An 8 km loop from Le Boréon to a mountain lake at 2,150 metres, in the heart of the national park.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 15 November 2026

Lac de Trécolpas is a high mountain lake in the Mercantour National Park, at 2,150 metres, below the Pas des Ladres. You reach it from Le Boréon, above Saint-Martin-Vésubie.

It is one of the easiest mountain hikes to reach from Nice, about 1 hour 30 minutes by road. Ibex and chamois are often seen on the way.

A bit of history

Le Boréon is a hamlet of Saint-Martin-Vésubie and a gateway to the Mercantour National Park, created in 1979. The lake lies in the core zone of the park, where wildlife and plants are protected.

The route

  1. Start from the Vacherie du Boréon car park, above Lac du Boréon.
  2. Climb the waymarked valley trail to Lac de Trécolpas, below the Pas des Ladres (2,448 m).
  3. Come down the other branch of the loop back to Le Boréon.

Allow about 4 hours for 8 km and 500 metres of climb. The difficulty is medium. The season runs from June to November depending on snow.

What to see on the hike

Lac de Trécolpas

A clear lake surrounded by scree and peaks, below the Pas des Ladres.

Mercantour wildlife

Ibex and chamois roam the slopes around the lake; keep your distance.

Le Boréon

The hamlet and its lake at the start, with a waterfall, chalets and many trailheads.

How to get to the start

By car

From Nice, follow the Vésubie valley to Saint-Martin-Vésubie, then the road to Le Boréon and the Vacherie car park.

By bus

In summer, Lignes d’Azur bus 90 (or the Été Vésubie line, booking required) runs from Nice to Saint-Martin-Vésubie, connecting with the Boréon shuttle (Fridays and weekends, July to mid-September).

Practical info 2026: Lac de Trécolpas

  • Access: free, in the core zone of the Mercantour National Park.
  • Season: June to November depending on snow; very busy in July and August.
  • Park rules: no dogs; overnight bivouac allowed from 7 pm to 9 am; no fires.

Information checked in November 2026 with the Mercantour National Park and the Nice Côte d’Azur tourist office; it may change, so check before you go.

Tips for the hike

  • Start early in summer: the Boréon car park fills up fast and storms break in the afternoon.
  • Bring warm clothes: it is cool by the lake, even in summer.
  • Check the weather and snow at the start and end of the season.
  • Do not feed the animals and keep your distance.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to reach Lac de Trécolpas?

Allow about 4 hours for the 8 km loop from Le Boréon, with 500 metres of climb.

When can you hike to Lac de Trécolpas?

From June to November, depending on snow. July and August are the busiest months.

Can you bring a dog?

No, dogs are not allowed in the core zone of the Mercantour National Park.

What is there to see near Lac de Trécolpas?

In this guide, the closest places are Nice (about 1 h 30 by car), Mont Gros (about 1 h 30 by car) and Vieil Aspremont (about 1 h by car).
