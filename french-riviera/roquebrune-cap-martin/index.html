Visit Roquebrune-Cap-Martin

A medieval village perched above Monaco and a pine-covered cape where Le Corbusier spent his summers in a cabin.

Roquebrune-Cap-Martin is split between a hilltop village built around a medieval castle and Cap Martin, a wooded peninsula between Monaco and Menton.

It is one of the best-preserved spots on the Riviera, ideal for a quieter day than Monaco, just minutes away.

Things to do in Roquebrune-Cap-Martin

Roquebrune castle

A medieval keep at the top of the village, with a plunging view over Monaco and the sea. Book a guided tour or activity in Roquebrune-Cap-Martin.

The hilltop village

Vaulted lanes and steps cut into the rock, such as Rue Moncollet.

The thousand-year-old olive tree

One of the oldest olive trees in the region, on the way to Menton below the village.

Promenade Le Corbusier

The Cap Martin coastal path runs along the sea among the pines. Le Corbusier had his cabin here, a UNESCO World Heritage site; it can be visited on booked guided tours.

How to get to Roquebrune-Cap-Martin

By train

Roquebrune-Cap-Martin and Carnolès stations are served by TER trains between Nice and Menton.

On foot from Monaco

The village is a good walk from Monaco, uphill. You can also reach Cap Martin along the seafront.

Practical info 2026: Roquebrune-Cap-Martin

  • Cap Moderne (Eileen Gray’s E-1027 villa, Le Corbusier’s cabanon): closed from 1 November to 31 March. The rest of the year, guided tours by compulsory booking: €19 adults, €10 for ages 7-18.
  • By train: TER Nice–Ventimiglia, Cap-Martin-Roquebrune stop (Cabbé station), 450 m from the Cap Moderne reception.
  • By bus: line 100 Nice–Menton, “Quatre Chemins” stop, then about 20 minutes on foot.

Prices and opening hours checked in November 2026 with tourist offices and the sites themselves; they can change, so check before you go.

Tips for visiting Roquebrune-Cap-Martin

  • Walk the coastal path in the morning, when it is in the shade and quiet.
  • Book the Cap Moderne tour ahead if you want to see Le Corbusier’s cabin.
  • Combine Roquebrune with Monaco or Menton on the same day.

Frequently asked questions

Can you visit Le Corbusier’s cabin?

Yes, only on a booked guided tour with the Cap Moderne site.

How long is the Promenade Le Corbusier?

Allow one to two hours depending on the section, on an easy seaside path.

How do you get to Roquebrune from Monaco?

By train to Roquebrune-Cap-Martin station or on foot along the seafront.
