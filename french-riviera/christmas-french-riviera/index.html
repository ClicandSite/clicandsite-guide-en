Christmas on the French Riviera 2026

Chalets under palm trees, a big wheel facing the sea and a village on Monaco’s harbour: Christmas in the sunshine, from late November to early January.

By Anthony, Cannes · Updated 12 November 2026

On the French Riviera, Christmas markets are often a sweater affair rather than a winter-coat one. Nice, Cannes and Monaco each set up their own village, less than an hour apart by train.

The 2026 dates: Nice and Cannes from 27 November 2026 to 3 January 2027, Monaco from 4 December 2026 to 3 January 2027. Entry to the villages is free.

The 2026 Christmas markets on the French Riviera

The Nice Christmas village

About sixty chalets in the Jardin Albert-Ier and attractions on Place Masséna, with a big wheel and a carousel, from 27 November 2026 to 3 January 2027. Free entry, with bag checks.

Christmas in Cannes

The Christmas village sets up on the Allées de la Liberté, facing the Old Port, from 27 November 2026 to 3 January 2027, with a second spot on Place Roubaud in La Bocca from 19 to 24 December.

The Monaco Christmas village

Chalets on Quai Albert-Ier at Port Hercule, from 4 December 2026 to 3 January 2027, with the nearby ice rink at €8 including skates.

The lights

Place Masséna, the Croisette and Monte-Carlo’s Place du Casino are lit up throughout the festive season.

Getting between the Christmas markets

By train

Regional trains link Cannes, Antibes, Nice, Monaco and Menton all along the coast. Nice-Ville, Cannes and Monaco-Monte-Carlo stations.

By tram in Nice

Line 1, Masséna stop, right by the Christmas village.

Monaco: beware of Uber

A ride-hailing driver from France can drop you off in Monaco, but no Uber ride can be booked from Monaco to leave. Plan the train, a Monaco taxi or a pre-booked driver for the way back.

Tips for Christmas on the French Riviera

  • Do Nice and Monaco on the same day by train: the two are less than 30 minutes apart.
  • Come on a weekday in the late afternoon to see the lights without the weekend crowds.
  • Opening times change on 24, 25 and 31 December and 1 January: check before you go.
  • 8 December is a public holiday in Monaco: the principality is busier than usual.

Frequently asked questions

When does the Nice Christmas market open in 2026?

From Friday 27 November 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027, in the Jardin Albert-Ier and on Place Masséna. Entry is free.

What are the dates of the Monaco Christmas village?

From 4 December 2026 to 3 January 2027, on Quai Albert-Ier at Port Hercule.

Can I get an Uber back from Monaco?

No: a ride-hailing driver can drop you in Monaco, but no Uber ride can be booked from the principality. Plan the train, a Monaco taxi or a pre-booked driver.
