Plage de la Mala and the Cap-d’Ail coastal path

A turquoise cove at the foot of the cliffs that you have to earn: down a hundred steps or along the coastal path from Monaco.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 15 November 2026

Plage de la Mala is a small cove in Cap-d’Ail, between Èze and Monaco, tucked below white rock cliffs. Its turquoise water makes it one of the most beautiful beaches on the French Riviera.

You cannot park there: you walk down, either by the Mala steps or along the coastal path that follows the shore from Plage Marquet, on the edge of Monaco.

A bit of history

Cap-d’Ail grew in the Belle Époque, when wealthy families built villas by the water. The Mala steps run alongside the former Eden grand hotel, now a residence.

The coastal path runs below these villas and near an old quarry where millstones were cut from the rock.

The Cap-d’Ail coastal path

  1. Start from Plage Marquet, on the edge of Monaco (Fontvieille side). The path is wide there and wheelchair-accessible with help for 1.5 km, as far as the Pointe des Douaniers.
  2. The path then runs past the villas, the Gramaglia cove and flat rocks where you can swim.
  3. The last stretch is steeper, over Cap Mala, down to Plage de la Mala.

Allow 3.6 km and about 1 hour 30 minutes from Plage Marquet to Plage de la Mala. The path is easy, with steps near the end. From the road, the way down the Mala steps takes 10 to 15 minutes.

What to see

Plage de la Mala

A cove of pebbles and fine sand with turquoise water, with a private beach section and a public one.

Pointe des Douaniers

Little coves and flat rocks beside the path.

The Belle Époque villas

The path runs below large estates from the early 20th century.

How to get there

By train

Cap-d’Ail station, on the Nice – Ventimiglia line, is about 15 minutes’ walk from the beach.

By car

There is no parking at the beach: use the Plage Marquet car park on the lower corniche road, then follow the coastal path.

By the steps

From the centre of Cap-d’Ail, Allée Mala leads to the steps (more than 100) that run alongside the Eden residence.

Practical info 2026: Plage de la Mala

  • Beach: public and free, with a private beach section and restaurants in season.
  • Coastal path: free; several stretches are exposed to waves and closed in rough seas.
  • Accessibility: path wheelchair-accessible with help from Plage Marquet to the Pointe des Douaniers; the beach itself can only be reached by steps.

Information checked in November 2026 with the Cap-d’Ail tourist office and the regional tourist board; it may change, so check before you go.

Tips

  • Come early in summer: the beach is small and fills up fast.
  • Avoid the path in rough seas: waves wash over it.
  • Wear good shoes for the steps and the path, and water shoes for the pebbles.
  • Not ideal with a pushchair: there are a lot of steps.

Frequently asked questions

How do you get to Plage de la Mala?

On foot only: down the Mala steps from the centre of Cap-d’Ail, or along the coastal path from Plage Marquet.

Can you park at Plage de la Mala?

No. Park at the Plage Marquet car park or come by train to Cap-d’Ail station.

How long is the Cap-d’Ail coastal path?

About 3.6 km from Plage Marquet to Plage de la Mala, or 1 hour 30 minutes at an easy pace.

What is there to see near Plage de la Mala?

In this guide, the closest places are Monaco (about 10 min by car), Nietzsche Path (about 10 min by car) and Èze (about 15 min by car).
