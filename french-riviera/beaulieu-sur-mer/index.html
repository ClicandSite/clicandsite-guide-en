Beaulieu-sur-Mer is a small seaside resort between Nice and Monaco, sheltered in a bay facing the Cap Ferrat peninsula. Most visitors come for Villa Kérylos, one of the most surprising houses on the Riviera.

Built between 1902 and 1908, the villa recreates an ancient Greek house, décor and furniture included. It belongs to the Institut de France and is open all year.

A bit of history

Villa Kérylos was the dream of Théodore Reinach, an archaeologist and scholar of ancient Greece. Between 1902 and 1908 he had it built by the architect Emmanuel Pontremoli, modelled on the houses of the island of Delos.

Kérylos is Greek for a sea swallow, a bird of good omen. On his death in 1928, Théodore Reinach bequeathed the villa to the Institut de France; it is a listed historic monument.

Things to see in Beaulieu-sur-Mer

Villa Kérylos

A recreated ancient Greek house, with its peristyle of marble columns, mosaics, frescoes and furniture copied from antiquity. See tickets for Villa Kérylos

The grand salon

The reception room, all marble, mosaics and alabaster lamps.

The Mediterranean garden

Olive trees, vines, myrtle and laurels around the villa, with views of Cap Ferrat.

Baie des Fourmis

The seafront walk in front of the villa, with palm trees and Belle Époque villas.

The path to Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat

A seaside walk links Beaulieu to Cap Ferrat and the Villa Ephrussi de Rothschild.

How to get to Beaulieu-sur-Mer

By train

Beaulieu-sur-Mer station, on the Nice–Monaco line, is a few minutes’ walk from the villa.

By bus

Lines 15 and 100 from Nice stop at “Kérylos”.

With a chauffeur

From Nice airport, allow about 30 to 40 minutes depending on traffic.

Practical info 2026: Beaulieu-sur-Mer and Villa Kérylos

  • Opening times: September to April, 10 am to 5 pm; May to August, 10 am to 6 pm. Last entry 30 minutes before closing.
  • Closed: 1 January, 1 May, 1 and 11 November, 25 December.
  • Tickets: free for under-18s; combined tickets with the Villa Ephrussi de Rothschild or the Trophée d’Auguste in La Turbie. Current prices on the official website.

Tips for visiting Beaulieu-sur-Mer

  • Get the combined ticket if you are also visiting the Villa Ephrussi de Rothschild.
  • Come by train: the station is close and parking is limited.
  • Leave time for the seaside walk towards Cap Ferrat.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a visit to Villa Kérylos take?

Allow about an hour for the villa and its garden.

How do you get to Villa Kérylos from Nice?

By train to Beaulieu-sur-Mer station, by bus (lines 15 and 100, Kérylos stop) or with a chauffeur.

How long should you spend in Beaulieu-sur-Mer?

Allow half a day with the walk.
