Castle Hill is the birthplace of Nice: the town was founded here. Of the castle that stood on top for centuries, only ruins remain.

Today it is a large, free, shady public park with the best view over the Baie des Anges, Old Nice and the port.

A bit of history

Castle Hill is where Nice was founded. For centuries a fortified castle and the town’s cathedral stood on top; only ruins remain today.

In 1882 the town turned it into a public promenade, and the waterfall, fed by water from the Vésubie, was built in 1885.

Things to see on Castle Hill

The view over the Baie des Anges

Nice’s most famous view: the Promenade des Anglais, the beach and the rooftops of Old Nice. See guided tours of Nice

The waterfall

Built in 1885 and fed by water from the Vésubie, it tumbles down in the middle of the park.

The view of the port

On the east side, Port Lympia and its colourful boats.

The ruins

The remains of the old cathedral and the castle.

The park

Shady paths and children’s play areas.

How to get to Castle Hill

By lift

A free lift runs from Quai des États-Unis, at the corner of Rue des Ponchettes. It may close in bad weather or for maintenance.

On foot

Up the Lesage steps from the quay or the lanes from Old Nice: allow 15 to 20 minutes.

With a chauffeur

From Nice airport, allow about 20 minutes to Quai des États-Unis.

Practical info 2026: Castle Hill (Colline du Château)

  • Entry: the park and the lift are free.
  • Opening times: roughly 8.30 am–8 pm April to September and until 6 pm October to March; times may vary.
  • Access: lift, Lesage steps from Quai des États-Unis or lanes up from Old Nice.

Prices and opening times checked in November 2026 with official websites and local tourist offices; they may change, so check before you go.

Tips for visiting Castle Hill

  • Go up late in the day for the light over the bay.
  • Walk up and come down through Old Nice.
  • Have a plan B in case the lift is closed.

Frequently asked questions

Is Castle Hill free?

Yes, both the park and the lift are free.

How do you get up Castle Hill?

By the free lift on Quai des États-Unis, the Lesage steps or on foot from Old Nice.

How long should you spend on Castle Hill?

Allow about 1 hour.

What is there to see near Castle Hill?

In this guide, the closest places are Vieux-Nice (about 5 min on foot), Port Lympia (about 5 min on foot), Place Masséna and the Carré d’Or (about 15 min on foot).
