Bastia, on the east coast, is the second town of Corsica and the prefecture of Haute-Corse. It grew up around two districts: Terra Vecchia, around the old port, and Terra Nova, the citadel.

Everything is walkable between Place Saint-Nicolas, the old port and the citadel: allow a day. Bastia is also the ideal starting point for a drive around Cap Corse.

A bit of history

Bastia takes its name from the “bastiglia”, the keep the Genoese built above the port at the end of the 14th century. The citadel, Terra Nova, grew around it, facing the old town, Terra Vecchia.

Capital of the island under Genoa and seat of its governors, Bastia long remained Corsica’s main town. Today it is the prefecture of Haute-Corse and a major ferry port to the mainland.

Things to see in Bastia

The old port and Terra Vecchia

The heart of town, with its fishing boats, café terraces and tall façades. See tours and trips in Bastia

The Church of Saint-Jean-Baptiste

With its twin bell towers it overlooks the old port; it is a listed historic monument.

The citadel and Governors’ Palace

The former Genoese palace houses the Bastia museum, devoted to the town’s history; its gardens overlook the port.

Place Saint-Nicolas

Nearly 300 metres long, lined with palm trees and open to the sea, it is where Bastia meets.

Cap Corse

North of town, a corniche road loops around the peninsula, past fishing villages and Genoese towers.

How to get to Bastia

By air

Bastia-Poretta airport is about twenty kilometres south.

By ferry

Regular crossings from Marseille, Toulon, Nice and Italy.

By car

About 3 hours from Ajaccio and 2 hours from Porto-Vecchio.

  • Climb to the citadel through the Romieu garden.
  • Enjoy the Terra Vecchia market on weekend mornings.
  • Keep a day for the Cap Corse drive.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to visit Bastia?

A day is enough for the old port, the citadel and Place Saint-Nicolas; two with Cap Corse.

What is there to see in Bastia’s citadel?

The Governors’ Palace, home to the Bastia museum, the Cathedral of Sainte-Marie and the lanes of Terra Nova.

How long should you spend in Bastia?

Allow one day.

What is there to see near Bastia?

In this guide, the closest places are Ajaccio (about 131 km away), Porto-Vecchio (about 155 km away), Bonifacio (about 184 km away).
