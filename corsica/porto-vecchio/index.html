Porto-Vecchio, in south-east Corsica, is the island’s third town. Its Genoese citadel, built from 1539, overlooks the marina and the salt marshes.

The old town takes an hour or two; the rest of the time is for the beaches of Palombaggia, Santa Giulia or Cala Rossa, or boat trips to the Cerbicale and Lavezzi islands.

A bit of history

The Genoese founded Porto-Vecchio in 1539 and fortified it on a promontory of pink porphyry at the head of its gulf. The early years were hard: malaria from the marshes long decimated the population.

The salt marshes, still worked below the citadel, earned the town its nickname of “city of salt”. In the 20th century its beaches made it one of the island’s major holiday destinations.

Things to see in Porto-Vecchio

The citadel and old town

Lanes, Place de la République and café terraces within the 16th-century Genoese walls. See boat trips and activities in Porto-Vecchio

The Genoese gate

The old entrance to the citadel has a fine view over the harbour and salt marshes.

Palombaggia beach

Fine sand, turquoise water, pink porphyry rocks and umbrella pines, facing the Cerbicale islands.

Santa Giulia beach

A shallow lagoon with calm water, ideal with children.

The Cerbicale islands

A small protected archipelago off Palombaggia, popular for diving and boat trips.

How to get to Porto-Vecchio

By air

Figari airport is about 30 minutes away.

By car

About 30 minutes from Bonifacio and 2 hours from Bastia.

Shuttles

In summer, shuttles link the town to Palombaggia and Santa Giulia beaches.

Practical info 2026: Porto-Vecchio

  • Beaches: free access; car parks often paid and full early in summer.
  • Old town: free to explore all year.

Tips for visiting Porto-Vecchio

  • Get to the beaches before 10 am in July and August.
  • Walk up to the Genoese gate at sunset.
  • Book boat trips to the Lavezzi ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best beach near Porto-Vecchio?

Palombaggia is the most famous; Santa Giulia is ideal with children.

Can you visit the Lavezzi Islands from Porto-Vecchio?

Yes, boat trips leave from Porto-Vecchio, though Bonifacio is the most common departure point.

How long should you spend in Porto-Vecchio?

Allow 2 to 3 days with the beaches.

What is there to see near Porto-Vecchio?

In this guide, the closest places are Bonifacio (about 31 km away), Lavezzi Islands (about 36 km away), Ajaccio (about 72 km away).
