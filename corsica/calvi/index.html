Calvi lies on the north-west coast of Corsica, in the Balagne. Its Genoese citadel, perched on a headland above the port, overlooks a wide bay lined by a beach of about 4.5 km.

The citadel and lower town take half a day, then enjoy the beach or a boat trip to the Scandola reserve and the Calanques de Piana.

A bit of history

The Genoese fortified Calvi at the end of the 13th century and made it one of their most loyal strongholds, hence the motto carved at the citadel entrance. The town withstood several sieges, including that of 1555. Locals also claim Christopher Columbus was born in the citadel.

In 1794 the town was besieged by the British fleet: this is where the future Admiral Nelson lost the sight of his right eye. In the 20th century the Foreign Legion settled in Calvi, where its 2nd Parachute Regiment is still based.

Things to see in Calvi

The citadel

Ramparts, lanes and the Cathedral of Saint-Jean-Baptiste; at the entrance, the Genoese motto “Civitas Calvi semper fidelis”. See boat trips and tours in Calvi

The port and Quai Landry

Cafés, restaurants and terraces facing the boats, below the citadel.

Pinède beach

A long sandy beach backed by pine woods, a short walk from the centre.

Notre-Dame de la Serra

A 15th-century chapel on the heights, with the best view of the bay.

The Balagne tramway

A little train follows the coast to L’Île-Rousse, from beach to beach.

How to get to Calvi

By air

Calvi-Sainte-Catherine airport is a few kilometres from town.

By ferry

Seasonal crossings from Nice and Marseille.

By car

About 2 hours from Bastia and 2 h 15 from Piana.

Practical info 2026: Calvi

Tips for visiting Calvi

  • Climb to Notre-Dame de la Serra early for the light on the bay.
  • Take the Balagne tramway for a car-free beach day.
  • Book Scandola trips ahead in summer.

Frequently asked questions

What can you see in Calvi in a day?

The citadel, the port, Pinède beach and the view from Notre-Dame de la Serra.

Can you visit Scandola from Calvi?

Yes, boat trips leave Calvi port for the Scandola reserve and Girolata.

How long should you spend in Calvi?

Allow 1 to 2 days.

What is there to see near Calvi?

In this guide, the closest places are Piana and its calanques (about 47 km away), Bastia (about 73 km away), Ajaccio (about 90 km away).
