Wind-sculpted granite boulders, crystal-clear coves and a nature reserve between Corsica and Sardinia.

The Lavezzi Islands are a small archipelago south-east of Bonifacio, at the heart of the Bouches de Bonifacio nature reserve. They can only be reached by boat.

Shuttles from Bonifacio drop visitors on Lavezzu island for a few hours: swimming, snorkelling and walks on marked paths. Day trips add the Bonifacio cliffs and the island of Cavallo.

A bit of history

On the night of 15 February 1855 the frigate La Sémillante, carrying soldiers to the Crimean War, broke up on the Lavezzi reefs in a storm, with no survivors. Alphonse Daudet told the story in his Letters from My Windmill.

Long visited only by shepherds and fishermen, the archipelago is now protected within the Bouches de Bonifacio nature reserve, which looks after its seabirds and seabed.

How to get to the Lavezzi Islands

By boat from Bonifacio

Shuttles and trips from the marina, the easiest way to get there.

From Porto-Vecchio

Day boat trips also leave from Porto-Vecchio.

By sailing boat or kayak

Possible from nearby beaches, for experienced sailors.

Practical info 2026: Lavezzi Islands

  • Access: by boat only; book ahead in summer.
  • Nature reserve: no leaving marked paths, picking plants or fishing; no animals.
  • On the island: no shops, bring water, a picnic and sun protection.

Tips for visiting the Lavezzi Islands

  • Take the first shuttle in the morning to beat the crowds.
  • Bring a mask and snorkel.
  • Bring water and shade: there is nothing on the island.

Frequently asked questions

How do you get to the Lavezzi Islands?

By boat only, usually from Bonifacio marina; trips also leave from Porto-Vecchio.

Can you swim at the Lavezzi Islands?

Yes, the coves of Lavezzu island are ideal for swimming and snorkelling.

How long should you spend on the Lavezzi Islands?

Allow half a day to a day.

What is there to see near the Lavezzi Islands?

In this guide, the closest places are Bonifacio (about 12 km away), Porto-Vecchio (about 36 km away), Ajaccio (about 97 km away).
