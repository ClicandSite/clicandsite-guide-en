Cap Corse, the finger of Corsica

A peninsula of mountains plunging into the sea, fishing villages and Genoese towers, north of Bastia.

Cap Corse is the long peninsula reaching north from the island above Bastia, for about forty kilometres. A clifftop road loops around it, between hilltop villages and small harbours.

The gentler east coast looks towards Italy; the wilder west coast drops in cliffs into the sea.

A bit of history

Cap Corse long lived from the sea and trade with Italy. Its coast is dotted with Genoese towers, built to watch for pirate raids. Many people from the Cape emigrated to the Americas and came back to build large houses, the “Americans’ houses”.

What to see on Cap Corse

Erbalunga

A fishing village of tightly packed houses on a rocky point, 10 km from Bastia.

Nonza

A hilltop village above a black pebble beach, with its Genoese tower.

Centuri

A small fishing harbour known for its lobsters.

The customs officers’ path

A coastal path between Macinaggio and Centuri, round the northern tip of the cape.

How to tour Cap Corse

By car

Allow a day for the loop from Bastia; the road is narrow and winding.

On a tour

One-day minibus trips leave from Bastia.

On foot

The customs officers’ path takes one to three stages, depending on your pace.

Practical information 2026: Cap Corse

  • Time needed: a day by car, several days for hiking.
  • Road: narrow, winding and with few petrol stations: fill up in Bastia.
  • Season: May to October for swimming and hiking.

Information checked in November 2026; it may change, check before you go.

Our tips for Cap Corse

  • Leave Bastia early: the photo stops take longer than you think.
  • Stop in Nonza in the late afternoon for the light on the black beach.
  • Try the Patrimonio wines, at the foot of the cape, on the way back.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to drive round Cap Corse?

A day by car from Bastia, taking time to stop in the villages.

What is the black beach on Cap Corse?

Nonza beach, covered with black pebbles, below the village.

Can you walk Cap Corse?

Yes, on the customs officers’ path between Macinaggio and Centuri.
