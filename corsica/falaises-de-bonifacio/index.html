The Bonifacio cliffs, a town suspended above the sea

White limestone cliffs carved by the sea, an old town perched on top and a turquoise strait facing Sardinia.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 13 November 2026

At the southern tip of Corsica, Bonifacio’s upper town sits some 70 metres above the sea, on white limestone cliffs hollowed out by the waves. Across the water, about twelve kilometres away, you can see the coast of Sardinia.

There are two ways to see the cliffs: by boat from the marina, to see them from below with the sea caves, or on foot along the Campu Rumanilu trail, which follows the clifftop to the Pertusato lighthouse.

A bit of history

The Bonifacio cliffs are made of layers of limestone and sandstone laid down on the seabed several million years ago, then uplifted and carved by the waves. The horizontal strata can still be read like the pages of a book.

This impregnable position made Bonifacio a coveted stronghold for centuries: the upper town was built on the edge of the drop to defend itself better. Today the coastline is protected within the Bouches de Bonifacio nature reserve.

Things to see at the Bonifacio cliffs

The cliffs by boat

From the port, boats follow the cliffs, the old town hanging over the void and the sea caves. See boat trips along the cliffs

The Grain de Sable

A huge block of limestone broken off the cliff and standing in the water below the town; a staircase leads down to it.

The Sdragonato cave

Its roof has an opening shaped like Corsica; boats approach it without going in.

The Campu Rumanilu trail

A paved path starts at the Saint-Roch chapel and runs along the clifftop, 80 metres above the sea.

Pertusato lighthouse and Saint-Antoine beach

At the end of the trail, the lighthouse guards the island’s southern tip, with the widest view over the town and Sardinia.

How to get to the Bonifacio cliffs

On foot

The Campu Rumanilu trail starts at the Saint-Roch chapel, between the marina and the upper town.

By boat

Many companies on Bonifacio marina, with regular departures in season.

By car

About 30 minutes from Porto-Vecchio; paid parking in Bonifacio.

Practical info 2026: The Bonifacio cliffs

  • Campu Rumanilu trail: free; about 30 minutes as a loop, 3 hours return to the Pertusato lighthouse.
  • Boat trips: departures from the marina, booking advised in summer.
  • Sdragonato cave: seen from the boat, it is no longer open to the public.

Prices and opening times checked in November 2026 with official websites and local tourist offices; they may change, so check before you go.

Tips for visiting the Bonifacio cliffs

  • Walk the trail early or late in the day: there is no shade.
  • Take the boat late in the afternoon for golden light on the cliffs.
  • Keep away from the edge: the cliffs are fragile.

Frequently asked questions

How high are the Bonifacio cliffs?

The upper town stands about 70 metres up; further east the trail is 80 metres above the sea.

How can you see the Bonifacio cliffs?

By boat from the marina, or on foot along the Campu Rumanilu trail to the Pertusato lighthouse.

How long should you spend at the Bonifacio cliffs?

Allow half a day.

What is there to see near the Bonifacio cliffs?

In this guide, the closest places are Bonifacio (about 15 min on foot), Lavezzi Islands (about 11 km away), Porto-Vecchio (about 31 km away).
