Piana lies on the west coast of Corsica, above the Gulf of Porto. The village is one of the Most Beautiful Villages of France, and its calanques have been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1983, along with the Gulf of Porto, Girolata and the Scandola reserve.

Discover the calanques by the D81 road, which winds between the rocks towards Porto, on foot along marked trails, or by boat from Porto to see the cliffs from the sea.

A bit of history

Piana village grew mainly in the 18th century, on a ledge above the Gulf of Porto; its Baroque church dates from the end of that century. For a long time the village was linked to nearby hamlets by old mule tracks, some of which are now hiking trails.

The calanques, red granite carved by erosion into strange shapes, were added to the UNESCO World Heritage list in 1983, together with the Gulf of Porto, the Gulf of Girolata and the Scandola reserve.

Things to see in Piana

The calanques road

The D81 squeezes between red granite boulders, with plunging sea views. See boat trips to the Calanques de Piana

Piana village

Lanes, granite houses and a Baroque church, with a view over the Gulf of Porto.

The Capo Rosso walk

A trail leads to a Genoese tower at the tip of the cape, with a huge view over the gulf.

The Tête de Chien

A famous rock and a short walk to admire the shapes carved by erosion.

The calanques by boat

From Porto, trips follow the cliffs to Capo Rosso, or go on to Scandola and Girolata.

How to get to Piana

By car

About 1 h 45 from Ajaccio and 2 h 15 from Calvi, on the D81; narrow, winding road.

By boat

Boat trips from Porto, about twelve kilometres away.

On foot

Several trails start from the D81 and the village.

Practical info 2026: Piana and its calanques

  • Calanques and village: free access; parking along the D81, full early in summer.
  • Boats: many trips from Porto to the calanques, Capo Rosso and Scandola.

Prices and opening times checked in November 2026 with official websites and local tourist offices; they may change, so check before you go.

Tips for visiting Piana

  • Drive early in the morning or late in the day, when the light reddens the granite.
  • Bring water and good shoes for Capo Rosso.
  • Avoid meeting coaches on the D81 at peak times.
