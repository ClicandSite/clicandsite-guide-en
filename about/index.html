About Clic&Site

Clic&Site is a practical guide to the most beautiful places in France. Each page answers the three questions you ask before a day out: what is there to see, how do you get there, and when should you go to avoid the crowds.

Who writes this guide

Clic&Site is written by Anthony, based in Cannes. He works in passenger transport on the French Riviera, from Saint-Tropez to Monaco, driving these roads all year round, in high season and in winter. Our advice on access, traffic, parking and the best times comes from those trips.

For regions further away, each guide is built from official sources (monument websites, tourist offices, national parks), checked at the time of each update.

Prices and opening hours are those of the current season, taken from official websites. They can change during the year, so always check with the site before you go. The update date is shown at the top of each guide.

Clic&Site is funded by affiliate links: when you book an activity after clicking one of our links, the platform pays us a commission at no extra cost to you. It does not change what we recommend.

We started with the French Riviera, which we know best, then Paris, the Alps, Normandy, the Loire Valley, Provence and Alsace.
