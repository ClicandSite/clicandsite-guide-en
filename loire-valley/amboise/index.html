Amboise: the royal château and Clos Lucé

Francis I’s favourite château, where Leonardo da Vinci is buried, and 500 metres away Clos Lucé, the house where the genius spent his last three years.

By Anthony, Cannes · Updated 12 November 2026

Amboise brings together two essential visits. The royal château, perched above the Loire, holds Leonardo da Vinci’s tomb in the Saint-Hubert chapel. Below it, Clos Lucé is the house where he lived from 1516 until his death on 2 May 1519, at the invitation of Francis I.

Both sites are open every day except 25 December and 1 January. In winter the royal château closes over lunch; Clos Lucé opens 9 am to 6 pm in November and December and holds its Christmas “Fééries” from 28 November.

What to see in Amboise

The royal apartments

The château’s Renaissance rooms, brought to life with the HistoPad tablet.

The Saint-Hubert chapel

A Flamboyant Gothic gem where Leonardo da Vinci is buried.

Clos Lucé

The room where Leonardo died, his restored workshops and models of his inventions.

The Leonardo da Vinci park

Twenty giant hands-on machines along a landscaped trail.

How to get to Amboise

By train

About 2 hours from Paris, or 20 minutes from Tours. The station is a 15-minute walk from the château.

By car

About 2 h 15 from Paris on the A10. Car parks along the Loire.

On foot

Clos Lucé is 500 metres from the royal château, up a sloping street in the old town.

Our tips for visiting Amboise

  • Start with the royal château, then walk down to Clos Lucé: allow a full day for both.
  • Go up to the château terraces for the best view over the Loire.
  • At Clos Lucé, save time for the park, where you can handle the machines.
  • Amboise makes a good base for Chenonceau, 20 minutes away.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to visit the Château d’Amboise and Clos Lucé?

Allow about €17.50 for the royal château with the HistoPad and €20 for Clos Lucé and its park, adult prices.

Where is Leonardo da Vinci buried?

In the Saint-Hubert chapel of the royal château of Amboise.

Can you get to Amboise by train from Paris?

Yes: about 2 hours by train from Paris, then a 15-minute walk to the château.
