The Royal Château de Blois

Seven kings and ten queens lived here: four architectural styles around a single courtyard, in the heart of the town.

The Royal Château de Blois overlooks the Loire in the town centre. Unlike châteaux lost in the countryside, you can walk there from the station.

Its courtyard brings together four wings from four periods: Flamboyant Gothic, Renaissance and Classical. It is an architecture lesson in a single visit.

A bit of history

A royal residence in the 15th and 16th centuries, the château was home to seven kings and ten queens of France. It was here that Henri III had the Duke of Guise assassinated in 1588.

What to see at the Château de Blois

The Louis XII wing

Red brick and white stone, with the king’s equestrian statue above the entrance.

The François I staircase

The famous open spiral staircase, carved with salamanders, the emblem of François I.

The royal apartments

Painted rooms and restored interiors, including Catherine de’ Medici’s bedroom and her secret cabinets.

The sound and light show

In the evening, in season, a show projected on the courtyard façades tells the château’s story.

The House of Magic

Opposite the château, the house dedicated to the magician Robert-Houdin, with dragons that emerge at set times.

How to get to the Château de Blois

By train

Blois is about 1 hour 30 minutes from Paris-Austerlitz. The château is 10 minutes’ walk from the station.

By car

Via the A10, Blois exit. Car parks in the town centre.

To the other châteaux

Chambord and Cheverny are about twenty minutes away, Chaumont-sur-Loire about 25 minutes.

Practical info 2026: Château de Blois

  • Opening: all year, with shorter hours in winter.
  • Sound and light show: in 2026, from 4 April to 26 September and during the autumn half-term; about 45 minutes, after dark.
  • Tickets: daytime visit, sound and light show or combined ticket, sold on site and online.

Information checked in November 2026; prices and opening times may change, so check the château’s website before you go.

Tips for visiting Blois

  • Come by train: everything is within walking distance of the station.
  • Stay for the evening sound and light show in season.
  • Combine with Chambord or Cheverny on the same day.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a visit to the Château de Blois take?

When is the Blois sound and light show?

In 2026, from 4 April to 26 September and during the autumn half-term, after dark.

Can you get to Blois by train?

Yes, about 1 hour 30 minutes from Paris-Austerlitz, and the château is 10 minutes’ walk from the station.
