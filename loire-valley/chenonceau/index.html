Château de Chenonceau

The “Ladies’ Château”, built on arches across the Cher and shaped by Diane de Poitiers and Catherine de’ Medici.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 12 November 2026

Chenonceau spans the river Cher on a row of arches: it is the most visited château in the Loire after Chambord. Its nickname, the “Ladies’ Château”, comes from the women who built, embellished and protected it, from Catherine Briçonnet to Diane de Poitiers and Catherine de’ Medici.

The château is open every day of the year; from mid-November to December, 9.30 am to 5 pm. From 28 November 2026 to 3 January 2027 the rooms are decorated for Christmas, with an evening opening on 23 December.

What to see at Chenonceau

The grand gallery

A 60-metre gallery above the Cher, used as a military hospital during the First World War.

Diane’s and Catherine’s gardens

Two formal gardens on either side of the Marques tower.

The kitchens

Set in the piers of the bridge just above the water, with their copper pans and butchery.

The queens’ bedrooms

Diane de Poitiers’ bedroom, the Five Queens’ bedroom and Louise de Lorraine’s black room.

How to get to Chenonceau

By train

Chenonceaux station is a few minutes’ walk from the château, with trains from Tours.

By car

About 2 h 30 from Paris, 35 minutes from Tours and 20 minutes from Amboise. Free parking.

On a tour

Day trips from Paris, Tours or Amboise pair Chenonceau with Chambord.

Our tips for visiting Chenonceau

  • Arrive at opening time: the gallery fills up quickly from 11 am.
  • Take the audio guide, which tells the story of the château’s women well.
  • Walk round through the gardens for the best view of the arches.
  • Allow a good half-hour’s walk there and back between the entrance and the château.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a ticket to Château de Chenonceau?

Allow €15 with the visitor leaflet, or €19 with the audio guide, for the château, gardens and galleries.

Is Chenonceau open in winter?

Yes, the château is open every day of the year. From mid-November to December, 9.30 am to 5 pm.

Can you get to Chenonceau by train?

Yes: Chenonceaux station is a few minutes’ walk from the château, with trains from Tours.
