The Domaine de Chaumont-sur-Loire brings together a Renaissance château, a large park overlooking the Loire and, every year from April to November, the International Garden Festival.

It is also a contemporary art centre: works are shown in the château, the stables and the park all year round.

A bit of history

Catherine de’ Medici bought Chaumont in 1550. When Henri II died, she forced Diane de Poitiers, the king’s favourite, to swap Chenonceau for Chaumont.

The International Garden Festival was created in 1992. Each edition invites landscape designers from around the world to work on a theme.

What to see at Chaumont-sur-Loire

The château

The apartments, Catherine de’ Medici’s bedroom and the view of the Loire from the terrace.

The International Garden Festival

About twenty temporary gardens by landscape designers; in 2026 the theme was “The garden goes to the movies”.

The stables

Luxurious 19th-century stables, among the finest in France.

The Prés du Goualoup

An extension of the park with gardens inspired by the whole world.

How to get to Chaumont-sur-Loire

By train

Onzain – Chaumont-sur-Loire station is about 2 km from the estate, on the Blois – Tours line.

By car

About 25 minutes from Blois and 20 minutes from Amboise.

By bike

The estate is on the Loire à Vélo cycle route.

Practical info 2026: Chaumont-sur-Loire

  • Opening: all year, except 25 December and 1 January.
  • Garden festival: from 22 April to 1 November 2026; adult ticket €21, €6 for ages 6-11, free under 6.
  • Christmas: “Rêves d’antan”, from 28 November 2026 to 10 January 2027.

Prices and dates checked in November 2026; they may change, so check the estate’s website before you go.

Tips for visiting Chaumont-sur-Loire

  • Allow at least half a day during the garden festival.
  • Arrive at opening time to enjoy the gardens in peace.
  • Combine with Amboise or Blois, both under 30 minutes away.

Frequently asked questions

When is the Chaumont garden festival?

In 2026, from 22 April to 1 November. The estate stays open the rest of the year.

How much does Chaumont-sur-Loire cost?

During the 2026 festival, €21 for adults and €6 for ages 6 to 11; it is cheaper out of season.

Can you get to Chaumont-sur-Loire by train?

Yes, Onzain – Chaumont-sur-Loire station is about 2 km from the estate.
