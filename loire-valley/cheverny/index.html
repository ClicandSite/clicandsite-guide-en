The Château de Cheverny

A Classical château that is still lived in, with furnished interiors, and the model for Tintin’s Marlinspike Hall.

The Château de Cheverny, about fifteen kilometres south of Blois, is a 17th-century Classical château with a perfectly symmetrical white stone façade.

It has belonged to the same family for more than six centuries and is still lived in. Its drawing rooms, armoury and bedrooms are furnished, which makes it a very lively visit.

A bit of history

Hergé drew on Cheverny to create Marlinspike Hall (Moulinsart), Captain Haddock’s home: he simply removed the two outer pavilions. A permanent exhibition, “The Secrets of Moulinsart”, tells the story.

What to see at the Château de Cheverny

The furnished interiors

Dining room, armoury, king’s bedroom: one of the best-furnished châteaux in the Loire.

The Secrets of Moulinsart

An exhibition on Tintin and Marlinspike Hall, with life-size sets.

The gardens

A park, an apprentices’ garden and a flower-filled kitchen garden, with rides in season.

The kennels

The estate’s pack of around a hundred hunting hounds, visible through the railings.

How to get to the Château de Cheverny

By car

About 20 minutes from Blois and Chambord. Free parking.

From Blois

Shuttles in season and day trips link Blois with the nearby châteaux.

On a day trip

Day trips from Paris or Tours combine Cheverny with Chambord and Chenonceau.

Practical info 2026: Château de Cheverny

  • Opening: every day of the year, including 25 December and 1 January.
  • Hours: 10 am to 5 pm from October to March; later in summer (9.15 am to 6.30 pm in July and August).
  • Tickets: château and gardens, with or without the Moulinsart exhibition and rides.

Opening hours checked in November 2026 with the château; prices and hours may change, so check before you go.

Tips for visiting Cheverny

  • Allow 2 hours for the château, the gardens and the Moulinsart exhibition.
  • With children, do not miss the Tintin exhibition.
  • Combine with Chambord or Blois on the same day.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Château de Cheverny open all year?

Yes, every day, including 25 December and 1 January.

What is the link between Cheverny and Tintin?

Hergé based Marlinspike Hall on Cheverny, without its two side pavilions.

How long does a visit to Cheverny take?

About 1 hour 30 minutes for the château and gardens, 2 hours 30 minutes with the Moulinsart exhibition.
