Château de Villandry and its gardens

The last of the great Renaissance châteaux built on the Loire, famous for its terraced gardens and ornamental kitchen garden.

By the Clic&Site team · Updated 12 November 2026

Villandry is famous for its gardens: six hectares on three levels, recreated in the early 20th century by the Carvallo family in a Renaissance spirit. The ornamental kitchen garden, the formal garden and the water garden can be visited all year round.

In winter the gardens open 9.30 am to 5.30 pm. The château is closed from 12 to 28 November, then reopens for Christmas from 28 November to 3 January, with an evening opening on 6 December.

What to see at Villandry

The kitchen garden

Vegetables planted in geometric squares, replanted twice a year.

The ornamental garden

Clipped box hedges forming allegories of love beneath the château windows.

The maze and water garden

The upper terraces, with an ornamental pool and a hornbeam maze.

The belvedere

An overview of the gardens, the château and the Cher valley.

How to get to Villandry

By car

15 km from Tours, about 20 minutes. Parking at the entrance.

By bike

On the Loire à Vélo route from Tours, about an hour’s ride.

On a tour

Half-day trips from Tours pair Villandry with Azay-le-Rideau.

Our tips for visiting Villandry

  • Start at the belvedere to understand the layout of the gardens.
  • In winter, when the château is closed, the gardens-only ticket is enough and cheaper.
  • Combine with Azay-le-Rideau, 10 km away, for a full day.
  • Come in spring or summer to see the kitchen garden in full growth.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to visit Villandry?

€14 for the château and gardens, €8.50 for the gardens only. Free for under-8s.

Is Villandry open in winter?

Yes, the gardens are open all year, 9.30 am to 5.30 pm in winter. The château is closed from 12 to 28 November, then reopens for Christmas.

How do you get to Villandry without a car?

By bike from Tours on the Loire à Vélo route, or on a tour from Tours.
